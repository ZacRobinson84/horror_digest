George A. Romero's long-lost 1962 debut short, [*Expostulations*, thought destroyed in a basement flood](https://ihorror.com/expostulations-george-romero-lost-1962-debut-restored/), has been restored and will screen at a one-night-only event next year with a live orchestral score. Details sparse, but this is archival recovery worth attention.

Diablo Cody finished the [*Jennifer's Body* sequel](https://ihorror.com/jennifers-body-2-diablo-cody-imminent-sequel/) and says it's coming imminently. The script explores a more sympathetic side of the demon possessing Megan Fox's character—a hard left from the first film's setup.

Alfonso Gomez-Rejon (who directed the 2014 *Town That Dreaded Sundown*) will helm the pilot and additional episodes of the [*Afterlife with Archie*](https://ihorror.com/afterlife-with-archie-alfonso-gomez-rejon-pilot-disney-plus/) zombie series for Disney+. The Riverdale spin-off is moving, albeit slowly, toward production.

A24 dated Jeremy Saulnier's [*Slayground*](https://ihorror.com/slayground-release-date-january-29-2027-a24-poster/) (a haunted attraction slasher) for [January 29, 2027](https://rue-morgue.com/a24-reveals-new-poster-sets-january-2027-release-date-for-slayground/), with a newly unveiled poster featuring a clock stopped at the moment of the last homicide.

Practical-effects horror-fantasy [*The Relic | Dragon*](https://rue-morgue.com/exclusive-trailer-premiere-news-practical-effects-horror-fantasy-the-relic-dragon/) blends cosmic horror, Viking legend, and treasure hunting. Rue Morgue has an exclusive trailer.
