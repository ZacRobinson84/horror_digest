Paolo Strippoli's [*The Holy Boy*](https://bloody-disgusting.com/reviews/3976960/the-holy-boy-review-a-strange-town-hides-a-terrifying-secret-in-paolo-strippolis-morality-chiller/) lands on Shudder—a slow-simmering morality chiller about an outsider arriving in an isolated Italian town built on buried sins. Strippoli twists the newcomer-in-a-strange-place setup into something that actually earns the buildup.

Ken Russell's uncut *The Devils* (1971) in theaters this year has [renewed interest in Oliver Reed's work](https://bloody-disgusting.com/editorials/3976763/oliver-reed-scream-king/) across his Hammer period and beyond. Reed's particular brand of unhinged intensity made him one of horror's most effective scream kings.

Paul Schrader's 1982 *Cat People* remake [took Jacques Tourneur's 1942 original](https://bloody-disgusting.com/editorials/3976840/cat-people-revenge-of-the-remakes/) and ran it through pure 80s excess—a genuinely wild reinvention, not a dutiful update.

*Bleeding Nightmare*, a psychological horror game about grief and family trauma from Darkania Works and GrimTalin, [drops December 4](https://bloody-disgusting.com/video-games/3976954/piece-together-your-family-tragedy-next-week-in-psychological-horror-game-bleeding-nightmare/) on PS5, Xbox Series, and Steam. A demo's available now.

*The Haunted Season: The Fated Hour*, the third in Severin Films and Shudder's Christmas ghost story series, [debuts next week](https://rue-morgue.com/exclusive-trailer-christmas-ghost-story-the-haunted-season-the-fated-hour-debuting-next-week/).
