Rob Jabbaz (The Sadness) is adapting Garth Ennis's Crossed for film, with [a teaser trailer](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3971039/crossed-teaser-trailer-the-boys-creator-and-the-sadness-director-preview-their-extreme-horror-experience/) now live. Details sparse, but the source material is among the most depraved comics ever published.

Jonás Cuarón, co-writer of Gravity, [says his next project is horror](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3971070/alfonso-cuaron-next-project-horror-movie/). It's been over a decade since he worked in the genre. No details yet.

A24's Masque of the Red Death, Charlie Polinger's "wildly revisionist and darkly comedic" take on Poe, is [expected in 2027](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3971066/a24-the-masque-of-the-red-death/) according to star Mikey Madison. She plays twin sisters.

[Clive Barker's Hellraiser: Revival](https://rue-morgue.com/video-game-review-clive-barkers-hellraiser-revival-has-such-sights-to-show-you/) drops as a single-player game. Early takes call it an "almost-perfect homage" to the original film series—a rare win for a licensed horror game.

Shudder acquired Split Rock, Ryan Glover's 16mm supernatural feature shot in the badlands, [ahead of its Sitges premiere](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3971061/shudder-sets-christmas-release-for-16mm-supernatural-horror-film-split-rock/). It lands on the streamer December 25.

Rob Savage's Other Mommy, starring Jessica Chastain as a demonic double, opened at [20% on Rotten Tomatoes](https://ihorror.com/other-mommy-opens-at-20-on-rotten-tomatoes-as-critics-question-its-jump-scares/). Critics found the jump scares formulaic and the film haphazard despite a strong premise.

C.W. Morgan, who played Billy Loomis's father in Scream, [has died at 72](https://www.dreadcentral.com/news/592753/scream-actor-c-w-morgan-has-passed-away-at-72/). No cause of death was released.
