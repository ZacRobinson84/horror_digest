Alexandre Aja returns to direct [Crawl 2](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3962790/alexandre-ajas-crawl-2-to-star-mason-gooding-and-emily-rudd/), with Mason Gooding and Emily Rudd starring in the New York-set sequel. Aja's also juggling Under Paris 2 for Netflix, so the director's staying committed to aquatic horror.

Na Hong-jin's [Hope](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3962781/hope-character-posters/)—a sci-fi thriller with Taylor Russell, Cameron Britton, Alicia Vikander, and Michael Fassbender—has new South Korean character posters that may finally explain why the cast has been noticeably absent from marketing materials so far. Details sparse, but worth watching.

[Hate Thyself](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3962744/hate-thyself-trailer-serial-killer-thriller-inspired-by-true-story-stars-the-late-tony-todd/), a serial killer thriller starring the late Tony Todd, arrives in theaters and digital September 1. Todd's final film before his death this year.

Ryan Murphy's [Monster: The Lizzie Borden Story](https://bloody-disgusting.com/tv/3962741/monster-the-lizzie-borden-story-vicky-krieps-playing-elizabeth-bathory-with-sarah-paulson-as-aileen-wuornos/) hits Netflix September 17, expanding beyond Borden to feature Vicky Krieps as Elizabeth Báthory and Sarah Paulson as Aileen Wuornos.

Lionsgate is developing a new [Leprechaun](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3962733/new-leprechaun-in-the-works-at-lionsgate-with-patrick-melton-marcus-dunstan-writing/) with Patrick Melton and Marcus Dunstan (Saw IV-3D, The Collector) writing. Nearly a decade since the last entry.

Jane Schoenbrun's [Teenage Sex and Death at Camp Miasma](https://bloody-disgusting.com/interviews/3962692/jane-schoenbrun-on-challenging-teenage-sex-and-death-at-camp-miasma-one-take-massacre/)—a lighter, playful slasher take after the isolating dread of her previous work—opens in limited release this week through MUBI.

John Carpenter released [Cathedral](https://www.fangoria.com/john-carpenter-cathedral-album-comic-review/), his first original graphic novel paired with an accompanying soundtrack. A rare artifact for Carpenter completists.
