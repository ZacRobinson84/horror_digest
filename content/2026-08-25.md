Jacob Chase (Insidious 6) is reteaming with Blumhouse Atomic Monster on [Sleepover](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3965111/insidious-6-director-jacob-chase-reteams-with-blumhouse-atomic-monster-for-sleepover/), a "quintessential sleepover nightmare" hitting Universal theaters October 8, 2027. Details sparse beyond that.

[The Institute season two](https://bloody-disgusting.com/tv/3965101/the-institute-season-2-first-images-tease-the-next-chapter-of-stephen-kings-horror-series/) is coming to MGM+ this fall with eight episodes. Mike Flanagan's Stephen King adaptation returns with new images; no exact premiere date yet.

Takashi Shimizu (Ju-On) is back with [Village of Eight Gravestones](https://www.dreadcentral.com/trailer/586337/ju-on-director-takashi-shimizu-returns-with-village-of-eight-gravestones-trailer/), a new take on a classic Japanese mystery.

[Ghost in the Cell](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3964939/joko-anwars-horror-comedy-ghost-in-the-cell-gets-gory-first-trailer-ahead-of-october-release/), Joko Anwar's horror-comedy set in a high-security prison, opens October 2. The trailer is packed with inventive gore.

[Lầu Chú Hoả: The Deadliest Mansion](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3964923/lau-chu-hoa-the-deadliest-mansion-vietnamese-found-footage-movie-coming-to-u-s-theaters/), Vietnam's first theatrical found-footage horror film, hits AMC, Regal, and Cinemark on September 4.
