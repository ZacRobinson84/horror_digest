David Bruckner [takes over Smile 3](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3973822/smile-3-officially-moving-forward-with-david-bruckner-directing-nick-antosca-writing/) with Nick Antosca writing. Parker Finn shifts to *Possession* but stays as producer. Straightforward handoff, not much gossip here.

Ben Wheatley replaces Rob Savage on [*Molepeople*](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3973783/ben-wheatley-to-direct-molepeople-as-lakeith-stanfield-and-rob-savage-exit-horror-film/), the Blumhouse subterranean thing. LaKeith Stanfield exited too. Kill List and *Normal* are strong credentials; Wheatley's an interesting choice for what sounds like creature feature territory.

Alfonso Cuarón's Apple TV+ [*Ascension*](https://bloody-disgusting.com/tv/3973790/alfonso-cuarons-horror-series-ascension-adds-amanda-plummer-christopher-chung-tnia-miller/) (based on Adam Nevill's *Last Days*) adds Amanda Plummer, Christopher Chung, and T'Nia Miller to a cast that already has Caitríona Balfe. Folk horror via prestige TV.

Peter Medak, [director of *The Changeling*](https://ihorror.com/peter-medak-dead-88-the-changeling-director/), died at 88. That 1980 haunted house film holds up. He also directed episodes of *Tales from the Crypt* and *Masters of Horror*.

*Alien: Isolation 2* [goes planetside](https://bloody-disgusting.com/video-games/3973719/alien-isolation-2-weather-details/) with a smarter Xenomorph and dynamic weather. Moving from the Sevastopol's tight corridors to open terrain is a real tonal shift.

Paolo Strippoli's [*The Holy Boy*](https://www.fangoria.com/the-holy-boy-shudder-new-trailer/) lands on Shudder November 27. Italian folk horror about a village hiding something behind its happiness. Fantastic Fest award-winner.

Bong Joon Ho's [*The Host* gets a 4K restoration](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3973738/bong-joon-hos-the-host-restoration-gets-new-trailer-ahead-of-theatrical-release/) back in theaters November 20 with a conversation between Bong and Edgar Wright. Two decades and it still reads as creature-feature craft.
