Kaylee Hottle, deaf actress from the MonsterVerse films *Godzilla vs. Kong* and *Godzilla x Kong*, [died in a car accident in Maryland](https://bloody-disgusting.com/news/3960229/godzilla-vs-kong-and-godzilla-x-kong-actress-kaylee-hottle-has-passed-away-at-18-years-old/). She was 18.

Nicolas Winding Refn returns after a decade with [*Her Private Hell*](https://rue-morgue.com/exclusive-interview-sex-cinema-and-psychological-scars-nicolas-winding-refn-and-the-cast-of-her-private-hell/), a psychedelic genre pastiche. Pino Donaggio scored it. The film arrives this Friday.

JT Mollner reunites *Strange Darling* leads Willa Fitzgerald and Kyle Gallner for [*Skeletons*](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3960213/skeletons-creature-feature-from-strange-darling-director-receives-summer-2027-release-date/), a creature feature also starring Brie Larson and John Goodman. June 4, 2027 from Sony.

Tobe Hooper's *Texas Chain Saw Massacre* is [getting a hand-drawn rotoscoped remake](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3960285/original-texas-chain-saw-massacre-being-reimagined-with-old-school-rotoscoping-animation/). A sneak peek screens during Texas Chain Saw Day on August 18; the full film arrives in 2027.

Casper Kelly's *Buddy*—a riff on '90s nostalgia featuring a bright orange unicorn mascot with a violent streak—[hits theaters August 28](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3960243/buddy-teaser-trailer-2-this-bright-orange-unicorn-wants-to-ruin-your-childhood/). Kenneth Branagh voices the creature.

Rob Savage's *Other Mommy*, adapted from Josh Malerman's *Incidents Around the House*, [premieres October 9](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3960201/other-mommy-official-trailer-jessica-chastain-wants-to-go-inside-your-heart-this-october/). Jessica Chastain plays the sinister entity. James Wan produces.

Mia'Kate Russell's feature debut [*Penny Lane Is Dead*](https://www.dreadcentral.com/exclusive/581824/gory-oz-ploitation-thriller-penny-lane-is-dead-is-making-audiences-pass-out-at-fantasia-film-festival-exclusive/) is an Ozploitation gore film causing walkouts at Fantasia.
