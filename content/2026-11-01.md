The Horror Game Awards 2026 takes place November 20 in London, with [*Reanimal* and *Resident Evil Requiem* leading nominations](https://bloody-disgusting.com/videos/3974174/reanimal-resident-evil-requiem-lead-nominations-for-the-horror-game-awards-2026/) across 21 categories. Over 100 titles in the running.

[*Patches*, a new puppet-gore short from Jesse Blanchard](https://www.dreadcentral.com/news/596054/no-cgi-just-puppet-gore-watch-patches-this-halloween-exclusive/), premiered in full on Dread Central. Four minutes, no CGI.

Jeremy Saulnier's *Green Room* nearly didn't happen—[Patrick Stewart stepped in to save it mid-production](https://www.fangoria.com/patrick-stewart-saved-green-room/), a fact Saulnier revealed after the late actor's death.

[*Hungry*, James Nunn's killer hippo creature feature, hit Prime Video's No. 1 movie slot](https://ihorror.com/hungry-killer-hippo-prime-video-number-one-james-nunn/) four months after its digital release, beating out Mike Flanagan's *Carrie* in the overall chart.

Melanie Lynskey will [lead *Mushroom Murders*, a six-episode Paramount+ series about the 2023 death cap poisonings in Australia](https://ihorror.com/mushroom-murders-melanie-lynskey-erin-patterson-paramount-plus/). Based on the true crime event.

HBO Max dropped [*A Story of Derry*, a 30-minute *IT: Welcome to Derry* bonus episode](https://ihorror.com/welcome-to-derry-halloween-special-a-story-of-derry-hbo-max/) at midnight on Halloween, set centuries before Pennywise. No advance notice.

Sony's R-rated animated *Bloodborne* film [now has a writer and director attached](https://ihorror.com/bloodborne-movie-writer-director-jacksepticeye-sony/), producer Sean McLoughlin said, with the story further developed than at its April announcement.
