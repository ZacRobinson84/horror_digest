Larry Fessenden's [Trauma Or, Monsters All](https://rue-morgue.com/larry-fessendens-monster-mash-trauma-or-monsters-all-stalks-theaters-november-4/) unites the vampire from *Habit*, the creature from *Depraved*, and the werewolf from *Blackout* in a single upstate town. Dark Sky Films releases it November 4.

[IT: Welcome to Derry](https://bloody-disgusting.com/tv/3972342/it-welcome-to-derry-season-2-will-feature-a-pennywise-youve-never-seen-before/) season 2 will feature Bill Skarsgård playing Pennywise in a form the character hasn't taken before, according to returning showrunner Brad Caleb Kane.

Jeremiah Kipp, director of *Slapface* and *The Mortuary Assistant*, [has died](https://rue-morgue.com/the-mortuary-assistant-director-jeremiah-kipp-has-died/). The independent horror community has lost a longtime collaborator and critic.

[Cannibal Holocaust](https://rue-morgue.com/blood-soaked-cannibal-holocaust-ultimate-edition-devours-4k-uhd-this-november/) arrives as a six-disc Ultimate Edition from Grindhouse Releasing November 24, featuring a new 4K restoration from original camera negatives.

Scarlet Stallone's SRYN Productions has optioned [Fears to Fathom](https://ihorror.com/fears-to-fathom-movie-tv-scarlet-stallone-sryn/), the six-game anthology, for both film and television adaptations developed with creator Mukul Negi.

[Clayface](https://www.fangoria.com/clayface-body-horror-exclusive-clip/), James Watkins' body-horror take on the DC villain, releases next week—its plane sequence has already drawn attention.

Nicolas Cage and Antonio Banderas are heading [True Detective season 5](https://ihorror.com/true-detective-season-5-nicolas-cage-antonio-banderas/) for HBO, with Issa López returning as showrunner and production starting in 2027.
