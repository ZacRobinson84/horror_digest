Zach Cregger's Resident Evil [opened with](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3968201/zach-creggers-resident-evil-breaks-franchise-record-with-8-8-million-at-thursday-night-box-office/) $8.8 million in Thursday previews—franchise record. The film plays survival horror straight while loading in dark comedy; it's a tonal shift from Anderson's action bloat, and early word suggests it lands both registers.

Brad Pitt is returning for [World War Z 2](https://bloody-disgusting.com/news/3968266/world-war-z-2-brad-pitt-return-official/) with Edward Berger directing. Berger's filmography (Conclave, All Quiet on the Western Front) suggests a harder edge than the first film. Script by Dennis Kelly.

Gerrit Graham, who [played Beef in Phantom of the Paradise](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3968300/gerrit-graham-phantom-of-the-paradise-and-terrorvision-star-passes-away-at-77/) and spent decades as a reliable low-budget fixture (TerrorVision, Child's Play 2), has died at 77.

A [live-action Lollipop Chainsaw film](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3968362/live-action-lollipop-chainsaw-film-adaptation-announced/) is in development as an R-rated prequel to the game, announced at Tokyo Game Show. Details sparse.

Shudder acquired [Red Snow](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3968255/red-snow-finnish-vampire-santa-horror-comedy-finds-a-home-at-shudder/), a Finnish vampire-Santa horror-comedy from Hatching writer Ilja Rautsi, ahead of its Fantastic Fest premiere this weekend. The premise alone is enough.

Bahman Ghobadi's [Trees Are Watching](https://bloody-disgusting.com/reviews/3968126/trees-are-watching-review-eva-green-cant-save-imbalanced-supernatural-thriller-tiff-2026/) played TIFF with Martin Scorsese as executive producer. It's a haunted-house film where the malevolent spirit is a wooden chair. Reviews suggest the concept outpaces the execution, even with Eva Green on board.
