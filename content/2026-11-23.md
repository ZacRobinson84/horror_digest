David Fincher recalls pitching the original *Alien 3* concept to Sigourney Weaver. The [filmmaker still believes in the idea](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3976598/alien-3-director-david-fincher-recalls-his-original-pitch-for-sigourney-weaver-sequel/) that sold her on his vision, even if he hasn't made peace with the finished film.

Mike P. Nelson (*Silent Night, Deadly Night*) will direct an untitled *Saw* installment arriving in 2028. [Nelson promises the film will "go all the way"](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3976575/mike-p-nelsons-saw-installment-expected-in-2028-director-promises-movie-will-go-all-the-way/) on extremes, written by Gregory Weidman and Scott Z. Burns.

Luca Guadagnino's *American Psycho* remake is [officially dead](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3976564/luca-guadagnino-confirms-american-psycho-remake-is-no-longer-happening/). The director confirmed to Deadline the project won't be happening.

Robert Eggers wants out of horror after *Werwulf* drops Christmas Day. Speaking at a masterclass, [the filmmaker said he's eager to pivot](https://rue-morgue.com/robert-eggers-says-he-wants-a-break-from-horror-after-werwulf/) away from genre work—next up: Shakespeare.

*The Holy Boy* arrives on Shudder November 27. The film follows a teacher tormented by a student who possesses the ability to [erase pain at a terrifying cost](https://bloody-disgusting.com/exclusives/3976450/the-holy-boy-exclusive-clip-introduces-a-teen-who-erases-pain-at-a-terrifying-cost/), and the locals can't handle it.

*Yaga* is [renewed for season two](https://bloody-disgusting.com/tv/3976561/yaga-renewed-for-season-two-with-carrie-anne-moss-returning/) with Carrie-Anne Moss returning. The Crave series drops its season one finale this week.

Bleecker Street is [releasing *The Third Parent* in January](https://rue-morgue.com/bleecker-street-sets-january-release-date-for-the-third-parent/), giving Crispin Glover's creepypasta villain Tommy Taffy a theatrical date to crash your family dinner.
