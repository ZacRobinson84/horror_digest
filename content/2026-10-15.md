Trevor Henderson's creature podcast [Mayfair Watchers Society gets a film adaptation](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3972012/trevor-hendersons-horror-podcast-mayfair-watchers-society-getting-a-movie-adaptation/) via Cineverse. Siren Head and Cartoon Cat are moving to the big screen.

Raven Banner has [acquired Polish folk horror ÒPI](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3971910/raven-banner-acquires-polish-folk-horror-opi-first-teaser-debuts-ahead-of-fest-premiere/) ahead of its Brooklyn Horror Fest premiere next week. Directorial debut from Marta Herszage.

[Crystal Lake](https://bloody-disgusting.com/tv/3971883/crystal-lake-will-be-a-6-5-hour-friday-the-13th-experience-episode-titles-revealed/) drops tomorrow on Peacock as a single 6.5-hour event. A24's Friday the 13th prequel hits all eight episodes at once.

Ethan Hawke and Scott Derrickson reunite for [Midnight Radio](https://rue-morgue.com/ethan-hawke-and-scott-derrickson-reteam-for-midnight-radio-horror-podcast/), a nine-episode late-night AM radio horror podcast premiering October 22. Self-funded and self-released.

Clive Barker is [fully on board with Hellraiser: Revival](https://www.dreadcentral.com/gaming/593586/clive-barker-hellraiser-revival-approval-interview/), calling himself "proud as fuck" of how far the game goes. Exclusive interview confirms his blessing.

[Whalefall](https://www.dreadcentral.com/reviews/593538/whalefall-review-a-mostly-suffocating-deep-sea-nightmare-led-by-a-vulnerable-austin-abrams/) — Brian Duffield's adaptation of Daniel Kraus's whale-belly survival novel — carries Austin Abrams through a suffocating premise. Reviews split on whether the claustrophobia pays off or just treads water.

Hulu's [X-Files reboot](https://rue-morgue.com/hulu-orders-full-season-of-ryan-cooglers-the-x-files-reboot/) from Ryan Coogler has been greenlit for a full season. Chris Carter returns as a non-writing producer; Danielle Deadwyler and Himesh Patel lead.
