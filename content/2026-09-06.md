Katie Dippold's *Widow's Bay* (Apple TV+) has made it through season one without killing off its entire New England curse-afflicted cast, though [the show's track record suggests no one should get comfortable](https://bloody-disgusting.com/editorials/3966214/widows-bay-ranking-the-characters-most-likely-to-die-in-season-2/). Abel Ferrara's *The Addiction* (1995) holds up as [a genuinely unsettling vampire film](https://www.dreadcentral.com/editorials/586233/the-addiction-an-uncommonly-grim-and-seductive-vampiric-tale/), one that treats the mythology as philosophy rather than mechanics. Black-and-white, grimy, seductive—it's the rare '90s indie vampire entry that didn't age into camp.
