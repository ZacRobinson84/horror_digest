Jalmari Helander (Sisu) is directing a revenge thriller for Netflix with [Aaron Pierre joining the cast](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3975704/fairytale-in-new-york-aaron-pierre-netflix-sisu-revenge-thriller/). Details sparse beyond the premise, but Helander's track record with escalating action suggests this won't waste time on justification.

Steve Miner is directing [a new Friday the 13th film](https://dailydead.com/steve-miner-returns-to-direct-new-friday-the-13th-film-2027-theatrical-release-planned/) for 2027 theatrical release. Miner helmed Parts 2 and 3, so this is less reboot than franchise continuity—the only sensible move.

Joachim Rønning (Tron: Ares) is shooting [System in abandoned London Underground tunnels](https://bloody-disgusting.com/news/3975678/freya-allan-set-to-lead-system-from-tron-ares-director-joachim-ronning/) with Freya Allan. Plot locked down, but the location work suggests something claustrophobic.

Takashi Miike is [casting Jon Hamm and Andrew Koji](https://ihorror.com/takashi-miike-bullfrog-jon-hamm-andrew-koji/) in Bullfrog, a serial killer thriller set in near-future Tokyo under lockdown, shooting summer 2027. Miike + Hamm is a pairing worth tracking.

Robert Eggers' Werwulf got a [song collaboration](https://bloody-disgusting.com/music/3975684/listen-to-ethel-cains-original-werwulf-song-performed-in-middle-english/) between Ethel Cain and Nosferatu composer Robin Carolan—"Wynter Waxeth," performed in Middle English, now available on vinyl.

PS1-style sci-fi FPS [DERELIKT is out now](https://bloody-disgusting.com/video-games/3975691/ps1-inspired-sci-fi-horror-fps-derelikt-now-available-on-pc-consoles-trailer/) on PC and console. Claims to feel like a lost 1999 title, which is either the hook or the whole joke.
