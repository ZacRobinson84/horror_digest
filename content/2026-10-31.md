HBO Max dropped [a new 30-minute special](https://bloody-disgusting.com/tv/3974157/a-story-of-derry-hbo-max-has-released-a-brand-new-welcome-to-derry-bonus-episode/) for *IT: Welcome to Derry* on Halloween called "A Story of Derry," exploring the origins of Pennywise. Details sparse, but the timing is worth noting.

Michael Dougherty teased [*Season's Greetings 2: Mischief Night*](https://bloody-disgusting.com/the-further/3974038/seasons-greetings-2-mischief-night-halloween-icon-sam-is-back-in-new-grindhouse-style-trailer/) on Halloween Eve—a grindhouse-style continuation of his 1993 short that introduced Sam. Marking 30 years since the original.

Holt McCallany, fresh off *Mindhunter*, is [leading *Paradise Disturbed*](https://bloody-disgusting.com/news/3974143/paradise-disturbed-holt-mccallany-serial-killer-movie/), a serial-killer film riding the wave of '90s true-crime cinema. Few details yet.

Josh Lobo's [*Night After Night*](https://bloody-disgusting.com/reviews/3974061/night-after-night-review-the-night-shift-becomes-an-existential-nightmare/) is a slow-burn experimental follow-up to *I Trapped the Devil*—surreal night-shift horror that doesn't answer questions. A second feature from someone worth watching.

Grindhouse Releasing and Full Moon are [bringing Scott Spiegel's *Intruder* (1989) back to theaters](https://ihorror.com/intruder-1989-grindhouse-releasing-theatrical-directors-cut/) this holiday season with the unrated director's cut and restored gore. 

Julian Doan's [*Long's Long Lost & Mini Mart*](https://ihorror.com/ripcord-halloween-film-festival-winners-longs-long-lost-mini-mart/) took the $100,000 grand prize at Ripcord's inaugural Halloween Film Festival. All 13 finalists got paid.

Junji Ito sat down to discuss [*Strange: Junji Ito's Tales for Sleepless Nights*](https://dailydead.com/interview-exploring-the-horrors-of-strange-junji-itos-tales-for-sleepless-nights-with-junji-ito-yuta-shimotsu-and-mayu-nobe/), his animated adaptation. A rare interview with the mangaka himself.
