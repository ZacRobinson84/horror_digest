Christopher Nolan has expressed longtime interest in directing a horror film. His 1989 short [*Tarantella*](https://www.fangoria.com/christopher-nolan-horror-film-tarantella/) already showed the visual and narrative fingerprints that would define his later work—so the question isn't whether he *can*, but when he'll actually commit.

Paramount has picked up *Freaks Part II* from the [directors behind *Final Destination: Bloodlines*](https://www.dreadcentral.com/exclusive/581700/exclusive-paramount-lands-sci-fi-sequel-freaks-part-ii-from-final-destination-bloodlines-directors/), following the film's Fantasia premiere. Details sparse, but this one landed a major studio faster than most.

*Penny Lane Is Dead*, an Australian '80s-set slasher from debut director Mia-Kate Russell, [arrives on Shudder August 28](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3960115/australian-horror-movie-penny-lane-is-dead-premieres-august-28-on-shudder-trailer/). Described as Ozploitation meets survival horror with dark comedy—the kind of regional stuff worth watching.

David Robert Mitchell's *The End of Oak Street* played as a preview at Fantasia. One observer called it ["a people movie with monsters,"](https://www.dreadcentral.com/editorials/581661/what-we-saw-during-a-preview-of-the-end-of-oak-street-a-people-movie-with-monsters/) which is either praise or critique depending on what you want from Mitchell these days.

Larry Fessenden's *Trauma Or, Monsters All* caps off a quadrilogy and [pits monsters against each other](https://ihorror.com/trauma-or-monsters-all-movie-review/)—the indie-horror lifer's latest from Fantasia.

Mike Mignola and Thomas E. Sniegoski's *Grim Death and Bill the Electrocuted Criminal* [is becoming a TV series](https://bloody-disgusting.com/tv/3960072/mike-mignolas-grim-death-and-bill-the-electrocuted-criminal-getting-a-tv-series-from-echoverse/) after its podcast iteration last year. Mignola handling adaptation of his own work is the draw here.

Survival horror *Nature of Dread* [lands on Steam](https://bloody-disgusting.com/video-games/3960064/escape-a-literal-hellhole-in-first-person-survival-horror-title-nature-of-dread-trailer/)—first-person, Lovecraft/Gogol/Bulgakov mashup in the northern wilderness.

**Worth reading in full:** [Mitchell's *The End of Oak Street* preview piece](https://www.dreadcentral.com/editorials/581661/what-we-saw-during-a-preview-of-the-end-of-oak-street-a-people-movie-with-monsters/) and [Fessenden's *Trauma Or, Monsters All*](https://ihorror.com/trauma-or-monsters-all-movie-review/).
