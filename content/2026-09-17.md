Mike Flanagan is writing his adaptation of Stephen King's *The Mist* for Warner Bros., with the script [due around Halloween](https://ihorror.com/mike-flanagan-the-mist-script-warner-bros/). He's not remaking Frank Darabont's 2007 film.

Zach Cregger's *Resident Evil* opens tomorrow, and early [critic reactions suggest](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3967905/resident-evil-rotten-tomatoes-score-reviews-video-game-record/) it's strong—the game adaptation is hitting unexpected heights for the franchise. Cregger made something that honors the games while being its own thing.

[*Death of a Yellow Bird*](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3967848/ghostly-voices-bleed-through-a-vhs-tape-in-death-of-a-yellow-bird-teaser/) (Cody Calahan) premieres at Fantastic Fest this week. A terminally ill man retreats to a remote Irish inn to scatter his parents' ashes; a buried VHS tape becomes a conduit for something worse. Atmospheric stuff.

[Damian McCarthy's *Hokum*](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3967885/hokum-damian-mccarthys-atmospheric-horror-movie-arrives-on-hulu-for-halloween/) lands on Hulu October 10, starring Adam Scott as a reclusive novelist checking into an Irish inn where the staff's tales of an ancient curse start to bleed into reality.

John Carpenter [volunteered to direct a music video for Metallica](https://bloody-disgusting.com/news/3967883/john-carpenter-metallica-music-video-directing/). Details are sparse, but it's Carpenter doing what Carpenter does.

[Ripcord's inaugural Halloween Film Festival](https://bloody-disgusting.com/news/3967878/ripcord-halloween-2026/) runs October 19–29 with 50 shorts competing for a $200,000 prize pool.
