Jane Schoenbrun (I Saw the TV Glow) makes their slasher debut with [Teenage Sex and Death at Camp Miasma](https://bloody-disgusting.com/interviews/3962167/how-the-teenage-sex-and-death-at-camp-miasma-stars-prepared-for-their-first-slasher-role/), a meta take on franchise reboots hitting theaters August 7. Hannah Einbinder plays a queer filmmaker hired to revive the Camp Miasma franchise.

Chris Carter's [R-rated director's cut of The X-Files: I Want to Believe](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3962131/the-x-files-i-want-to-believe-vrach-frankenshteyn-watch-the-trailer-for-scary-new-directors-cut/) lands on Hulu with the subtitle Vrach Frankenshteyn. The reworked version is scarier and closer to Carter's original vision than the 2008 theatrical release.

Fantasia wrapped its 30th edition with [125 features and 200+ shorts](https://bloody-disgusting.com/reviews/3962121/fantasia-2026-reviews-big-break-sleep-no-more/). Notable titles included No Rest for the Wicked (a vampire spin) and The Peril (a DIY horror comedy).

[Godzilla Minus Zero](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3962050/godzilla-minus-zero-will-world-premiere-at-the-new-york-film-festival-in-september/) will world premiere at NYFF64 in September. Takashi Yamazaki directs the MonsterVerse entry.

Temple Hill Entertainment is producing [Clown in a Cornfield 2](https://www.fangoria.com/clown-in-a-cornfield-2-frendo-lives-movie-details/), bringing back Frendo for another round after last year's slasher hit.

Mike P. Nelson's [Beware Boiúna](https://bloody-disgusting.com/exclusives/3961965/beware-boiuna-first-look-exclusive/) follows a medical team in the Amazon fighting a legendary colossal snake. The creature feature arrives October 2.

Warner Bros. is [fast-tracking a new Nightmare on Elm Street](https://ihorror.com/warner-bros-elm-street-movie-races-paramount-to-the-finish-line/) after Paramount announced their own Elm Street project under their new Primal genre label. Details remain sparse, but the race is on.

Rob Hollocks' [The Observance](https://ihorror.com/the-observance-rob-hollocks-interview-god-is-fear/) builds a cult narrative around a woman who wakes from a five-year coma to find her town rebuilt around a church that didn't exist before. Co-written with Amy Rhinehart Bailey.

**Worth reading in full:** [Luke Larkin on The Unheld](https://dailydead.com/startling-monsters-and-occult-oddities-abound-in-our-exclusive-excerpt-from-the-unheld-plus-a-qa-with-author-luke-larkin/), a horror Western arriving August 25. [Arkar Soe Oo's The Last Footage](https://ihorror.com/the-last-footage-review/) premiered at Fantasia—a found-footage piece that pushes the subgenre's immersion further than usual.
