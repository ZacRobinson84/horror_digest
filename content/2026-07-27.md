**The Last Temptation of Becky** closed out Fantasia with [Lulu Wilson's Nazi-stomping action heroine](https://bloody-disgusting.com/reviews/3960721/the-last-temptation-of-becky-review/) in what may be the trilogy's final chapter. The threequel apparently knows what it is.

**The Mouths**, screening at Fantasia, is a [ghost story structured like Rashomon](https://www.dreadcentral.com/reviews/582576/the-mouths-is-horror-rashomon-thats-rich-in-psychological-scares-suffocating-curses-fantasia-review/) — multiple perspectives on the same haunting, layered with psychological weight and mythification.

[Larry Fessenden's **Trauma**](https://rue-morgue.com/fantasia-26-interview-larry-fessenden-talks-trauma-monsters-and-the-fear-of-everything/) played Fantasia as part of his own monster-cinema universe. The interview covers his approach to classic creatures and contemporary anxiety.

Fantasia '26 [spread awards across several films](https://rue-morgue.com/fantasia-26-nightborn-no-rest-for-the-wicked-and-more-score-top-awards/), with **Nightborn** and **No Rest for the Wicked** among the winners. Details sparse on the others, but the festival clearly had range.

Gwendolyn Kiste (Bram Stoker winner, *The Haunting of Velkwood*) [has a novella coming](https://rue-morgue.com/exclusive-interview-great-gatsbys-ghost-gwendolyn-kiste-delivers-a-haunting-twist-to-a-literary-classic-in-in-these-gilded-ghostly-hearts/) called *In These Gilded, Ghostly Hearts* — a horror retelling centering the forgotten women of *The Great Gatsby* beneath the rotting American Dream facade.

Mike Flanagan's **Carrie** miniseries dropped [its first teaser at Comic-Con](https://dailydead.com/comic-con-2026-watch-the-teaser-trailer-for-mike-flanagans-carrie/). Eight episodes land on Prime Video October 7.

IDW's *Event Horizon* comic trilogy wraps in December with *Pandemonium*, [written by Eisner winner Christian Ward](https://dailydead.com/sdcc-horror-highlights-event-horizon-pandemonium-marvels-midnight-fangoria/). The premise: heaven exists and it's worse than hell.

**Worth reading in full:** [Fessenden on Trauma and monsters](https://rue-morgue.com/fantasia-26-interview-larry-fessenden-talks-trauma-monsters-and-the-fear-of-everything/)
