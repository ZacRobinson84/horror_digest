Christopher Landon is adapting [Grady Hendrix's *The Final Girl Support Group*](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3961429/christopher-landon-to-helm-feature-adaptation-of-grady-hendrixs-the-final-girl-support-group/) for Paramount, reuniting with him after *My Best Friend's Exorcism*. The slasher follow-up makes sense given Landon's track record with the genre.

Emily Bennett and Justin Brooks' [*Blood Shine*](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3961427/blood-shine-trailer/) arrives August 21 from Dark Sky Films. Set in upstate New York with Larry Fessenden leading a cult, it's a folk horror piece from the *Alone With You* pair.

Parker Finn's [*Possession* remake](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3961412/madeline-brewer-emory-cohen-and-nicholas-alexander-chavez-possession/) now has Madeline Brewer, Emory Cohen, and Nicholas Alexander Chavez joining Margaret Qualley, Callum Turner, Diego Calva, and Paul Dano. Filming is underway in New Jersey.

Na Hong-jin's [*Hope*](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3961359/new-hope-poster-gives-closer-look-at-rampaging-monster/) hits U.S. theaters September 9 via NEON. The Korean creature feature shows a snarling beast tearing through a remote village; the director of *The Wailing* isn't playing it subtle.

[*Fall 2: Deadpoint*](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3961289/fall-2-deadpoint-official-trailer-finds-new-fear-5000-feet-above-thailand/) trades the radio tower for a Thai mountain. Harriet Slater leads this September 2 sequel, swapping vertigo for rock climbing.

Eli Roth wanted [*Ice Cream Man*](https://bloody-disgusting.com/interviews/3960946/eli-roth-ice-cream-man-interview/) to have more kills than every previous film he's made combined. He's explicit about what he's chasing.
