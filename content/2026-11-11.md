Stacy Keach, the veteran character actor who appeared in Richard Fleischer's *Road Games* and John Carpenter's *Body Bags*, [has died at 85](https://bloody-disgusting.com/news/3975426/road-games-and-body-bags-actor-stacy-keach-has-passed-away-at-85/). He'd been fighting heart disease for some time.

Robert Eggers' *Werwulf* [got its rating](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3975439/werwulf-gets-hard-r-rating-for-disturbing-violence-gore-sexual-assault-graphic-nudity/): hard R for "strong bloody/disturbing violent content, gore, sexual assault, graphic nudity and strong sexual content." Eggers has called it his darkest work. Focus Features releases it in time for Christmas.

Ken Russell's *The Devils*—the uncut, NC-17 director's cut—[is coming to 4K UHD](https://bloody-disgusting.com/home-video/3975307/ken-russells-the-devils-nc-17-directors-cut-hits-physical-media-in-april-2027-full-release-details/) in April 2027 via Warner Bros. Clockwork. First physical release ever. The 3-disc collector's edition will carry supplements.

Maika Monroe says the *It Follows* sequel [is "much bigger"](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3975389/it-follows-2-maika-monroe-update-they-follow/) in scope than the original. Details sparse on *They Follow*, but she's still attached.

[*Behind the Mask 2* is officially underway](https://www.dreadcentral.com/news/597415/behind-the-mask-2-ben-pace-illinois/) with Ben Pace returning as Doug, twenty years after the original mockumentary.

Jess Franco's 1976 *Jack the Ripper*—the Klaus Kinski vehicle—[gets its 4K debut](https://rue-morgue.com/klaus-kinski-stalks-whitechapel-as-cauldron-films-brings-jess-francos-lurid-jack-the-ripper-to-4k-uhd/) via Cauldron Films on December 15 in a limited slipbox.

Park Syeyoung's dystopian *The Fin*, centered on mutated mermen as disposable labor, [drops its trailer](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3975330/the-fin-trailer-dives-into-a-dystopian-sci-fi-fever-dream-of-mutated-mermen/) ahead of a limited North American theatrical run this month. VOD in January.
