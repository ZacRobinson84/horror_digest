Craig Zobel will direct [the feature adaptation of "Something Is Killing the Children"](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3971311/something-is-killing-the-children-movie-director-craig-zobel/) for Blumhouse, with an adult animated series in development alongside it. Zobel's filmography (Compliance, The Hunt, Z for Zachariah) suggests a willingness to venture into uncomfortable territory.

Rob Savage is directing [Molepeople](https://bloody-disgusting.com/news/3971308/molepeople-lakeith-stanfield-rob-savage-ben-mendelsohn/), a subterranean horror film starring LaKeith Stanfield and Ben Mendelsohn for Paramount Primal and Blumhouse Atomic Monster. Savage's viral pandemic short work has translated into a feature career, and this one hits theaters August 27, 2027.

Mike Flanagan's [The Exorcist: Martyrs trailer](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3971230/the-exorcist-martyrs-full-trailer-scarlett-johansson-mike-flanagan/) dropped and it's something else—serial killers, possession, and the kind of grimy handheld aesthetic that has more in common with Se7en than the original Exorcist. Flanagan says it's the scariest thing he's made. Arrives March 12, 2027.

The new [Paranormal Activity](https://www.dreadcentral.com/news/592997/paranormal-activity-new-movie-original-house/) returns to the original house from 2007, and the Broadway adaptation of the franchise [extended its run](https://bloody-disgusting.com/news/3971256/paranormal-activity-broadway-run-extended/) after sellout shows—an odd flex for a found-footage property, but details are sparse on how that actually works.

[Play House](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3971225/play-house-movie-blumhouse-atomic-monster-distribution/), a debut feature shot for just over $100k, sold to Blumhouse-Atomic Monster and Focus Features for seven figures out of Fantastic Fest. It came out of the inaugural Fantastic Pitches initiative.

Danny Boyle confirmed [28 Years Later 3 is on the back burner](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3971212/28-years-later-3-is-on-the-back-burner-per-danny-boyle/)—the sequel's box office disappointment means the trilogy's finale can't lock financing yet.

C.W. Morgan, who played Hank Loomis in [the original Scream](https://rue-morgue.com/scream-actor-c-w-morgan-dead-at-72/), died at 72.
