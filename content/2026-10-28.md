Peter Medak, director of the 1980 haunted-house classic *The Changeling*, [died this week at 88](https://bloody-disgusting.com/news/3973653/the-changeling-director-peter-medak-has-passed-away-at-88/). The Hungarian-British filmmaker built a prolific career across film and television before becoming a fixture in horror.

HBO is [developing a limited series adaptation of *The Birds*](https://bloody-disgusting.com/tv/3973542/hbo-developing-the-birds-limited-series-adaptation-starring-sarah-snook/) with Sarah Snook starring. Tom Spezialy, who wrote pilots for *The Leftovers* and *Watchmen*, is attached to write.

Darren Lynn Bousman (*Saw II-IV*, *Spiral*) is directing [*Serpentine*](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3973566/serpentine-darren-lynn-bousman-directing-figure-skating-thriller/), a New England-set thriller centered on figure skating. He's stepping away from horror proper, though the premise suggests it won't stay clean.

Scott Glosserman, behind the meta cult film *Behind the Mask: The Rise of Leslie Vernon*, is [adapting *iHunt: Killing Monsters in the Gig Economy*](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3973472/ihunt-killing-monsters-in-the-gig-economy-scott-glosserman-directing-film-adaptation/) to film. Details sparse on the source material.

Sosie Bacon (*Smile*) and Nikolaj Coster-Waldau are [leading MGM+'s *The Black Tapes*](https://bloody-disgusting.com/tv/3973469/smile-star-sosie-bacon-joins-mgms-the-black-tapes-horror-series/), a limited series pairing a psychiatrist with a mysterious past against an obsessed physicist investigating unexplained phenomena.

*Mimic*, a solo developer's blend of *Among Us* and *Alien Isolation*, [is now in early access on Steam](https://bloody-disgusting.com/video-games/3973633/social-deduction-survival-horror-title-mimic-now-in-early-access-on-steam-trailer/). Ryan Laley's social-deduction survival game dropped after a demo last week.

Curry Barker (*Obsession*) has a new short, [*Mama Bear*](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3973535/curry-barker-teams-with-windows-on-new-horror-short-premiering-this-week/), premiering on YouTube Thursday. Details withheld until release.

**Worth reading in full:** [Joe Bob Briggs anthology](https://bloody-disgusting.com/books/3973501/joe-bob-fights-for-the-drive-in-dark-horse-book-will-feature-rare-writing-unseen-for-decades-exclusive/) collects decades of unseen cult-film writing.
