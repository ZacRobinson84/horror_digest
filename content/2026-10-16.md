Steven Soderbergh is finishing principal photography on [**The Batman 2**](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3972203/the-batman-2-matt-reeves-director-replaced-steven-soderbergh/) after Matt Reeves stepped away for a family matter. No word yet on how much of the film remains or what this means for the final cut.

[**Crystal Lake**](https://bloody-disgusting.com/tv/3972074/crystal-lake-peacock-releases-60-spoiler-images-showing-young-jason-gory-kills-and-big-reveals/) is now streaming all eight episodes on Peacock. Showrunner Brad Caleb Kane is [already plotting season two](https://bloody-disgusting.com/news/3972065/crystal-lake-season-2-plans-friday-the-13th-brad-caleb-kane/) with a *Twin Peaks* angle; young Jason and some genuinely brutal kills are visible in the official stills.

[**The Mummy: Book of the Dead**](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3972145/the-mummy-4-new-title-book-of-the-dead-brendan-fraser/) (the Brendan Fraser sequel) lands October 15, 2027, with Radio Silence directing. Fraser and Rachel Weisz are back.

Clive Barker [calls **Hellraiser: Revival**](https://www.dreadcentral.com/interviews/593584/clive-barker-hellraiser-future-weinstein-exclusive/) "a wake-up call" and says he has plenty more stories to tell—after decades of watching others run the franchise into the ground.

[**Trevor Henderson's Mayfair Watchers Society**](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3972012/trevor-hendersons-horror-podcast-mayfair-watchers-society-getting-a-movie-adaptation/) podcast is getting a film adaptation courtesy of Cineverse. The Siren Head and Cartoon Cat creator's creatures finally get the live-action treatment.

[**Jeremiah Kipp**](https://www.dreadcentral.com/news/594014/jeremiah-kipp-director-of-the-mortuary-assistant-has-passed-away/), director of *The Mortuary Assistant* and *Slapface*, has passed away.

[**Popcorn Frights' Wicked Weekend**](https://www.dreadcentral.com/festival-coverage/593851/freddy-jason-and-a-man-eating-plant-walk-into-a-film-festival-popcorn-frights-unleashes-its-wicked-weekend/) returns November 11–15 with fresh programming and special guests.
