[Blade Runner 2099](https://bloody-disgusting.com/tv/3975190/blade-runner-2099-final-trailer-prime-video/) dropped its final trailer. Prime Video's adaptation arrives without much narrative detail in circulation yet.

[Clayface](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3975199/clayface-hits-100-million-as-james-gunn-and-peter-safran-step-down-from-dc-universe/) crossed $100 million worldwide as James Gunn and Peter Safran abruptly stepped down from DC Studios. The body horror film's box office success comes as the studio reshuffles leadership.

Vera Farmiga returns to horror in [Inferno](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3975188/vera-farmiga-returns-to-horror-in-supernatural-movie-inferno/), a supernatural serial killer film directed by Bryan Hill (Titans), with Farmiga also producing. The Conjuring veteran is back in the genre proper.

[Dead of Night](https://bloody-disgusting.com/exclusives/3975133/dead-of-night-trailer-chris-jericho-produced-this-horror-movie-in-theaters-november-13/) hits theaters November 13, produced by Chris Jericho and inspired by the 1980s Night Stalker murders. Trailer is live.

Majid Al Ansari's [The Vile](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3975132/emirati-horror-film-the-vile-gets-eerie-trailer-ahead-of-november-release/), an Arabic-language psychological horror from the Emirati director of Zinzana and Paranormal, arrives November 20. A second wife brings darkness into a polygamous household.

Nicholas Denton and Tim Downie star in [Tragedy Man](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3975151/tragedy-man-will-dramatize-the-filming-of-vincent-price-horror-movie-witchfinder-general/), dramatizing the fraught production of Michael Reeves' Witchfinder General. Starts shooting late 2026.

[Return of the Living Dead: Aftermath](https://www.dreadcentral.com/news/597115/return-of-the-living-dead-aftermath-ai-quigley-randolph/) went live on Halloween as a cast reunion—except original star Beverly Randolph says [she never consented to AI](https://ihorror.com/return-of-the-living-dead-aftermath-ai-quigley-randolph/) recreating her likeness and wants it removed.
