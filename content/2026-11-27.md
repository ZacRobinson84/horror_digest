Ken Russell's uncut [The Devils (1971)](https://bloody-disgusting.com/editorials/3976763/oliver-reed-scream-king/) is back in theaters this year, drawing fresh attention to Oliver Reed's work at Hammer. The piece traces Reed's career as one of horror's most committed scream kings—less about the acting craft, more about his willingness to fully inhabit derangement.

[The Haunted Season: The Fated Hour](https://rue-morgue.com/exclusive-trailer-christmas-ghost-story-the-haunted-season-the-fated-hour-debuting-next-week/), a Christmas ghost story from Severin Films and Shudder, debuts next week. It's the third in their seasonal anthology series.

[Freaks Part II](https://rue-morgue.com/freaks-part-ii-bleeds-its-way-to-an-r-rating/) got an R rating from the MPA. The Final Destination: Bloodlines directors' follow-up to 2018's Freaks will be distributed by Paramount, with details sparse on what the sequel actually does.

Focus Features is mounting a full Oscar push for [Obsession](https://ihorror.com/obsession-oscar-campaign-billboard-focus-features/), a $750,000 horror pic that now has a Los Angeles billboard calling it best picture of the year. The campaign reach suggests they're betting on genre legitimacy, not just a category slot.

Barotrauma's [Home & Harbor expansion](https://bloody-disgusting.com/video-games/3976952/new-dev-diary-arrives-ahead-of-home-harbor-expansion-for-barotrauma-next-week-video/) arrives December 2. A new dev diary went live detailing the Descendants lore ahead of the drop.

[Remote Control](https://bloody-disgusting.com/video-games/3976928/psychological-horror-typing-game-remote-control-launches-january-29-trailer/), a psychological horror typing game, launches January 29 on Steam. A demo is playable now.

**Worth reading in full:** [The Devils piece](https://bloody-disgusting.com/editorials/3976763/oliver-reed-scream-king/) if you care about Reed's legacy.
