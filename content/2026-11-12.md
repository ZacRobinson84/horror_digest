Scott Glosserman's [long-gestating sequel to *Behind the Mask*](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3975533/behind-the-mask-ii-production-begins/) is now in production. Robert Englund and David Howard Thornton (Terrifier) are on board for the crowdfunded project.

Vera Farmiga leads Michael Gallagher's [*The Leader*](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3975518/the-leader-heavens-gate-cult-thriller-starring-vera-farmiga-releasing-march-2027-from-dark-sky-films/), a Heaven's Gate cult thriller hitting March 2027 via Dark Sky Films.

Don Mancini signed with Independent Artist Group and is [developing the next *Chucky* film](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3975511/don-mancini-developing-new-chucky-movie-along-with-original-film-and-tv-projects/) alongside original projects.

Samman Roy directs *[The Undying](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3975492/bengali-gothic-horror-the-undying-brings-classic-1892-story-to-life/)*, a Bengali-language gothic horror adapting Rabindranath Tagore's 1892 short story. Set in 19th-century rural Bengal, it follows a widow pronounced dead who mysteriously reappears.

Stephen Cognetti [teased the franchise's future](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3975503/hell-house-llc-creator-teases-franchises-future-after-lineage-cliffhanger-ending/) after *Hell House LLC: Lineage*'s cliffhanger ending, suggesting the supposedly final entry may not be.

Stacy Keach, the [veteran character actor](https://rue-morgue.com/horror-veteran-stacy-keach-dies-at-85/) whose credits included *Children of the Corn* and *Class of 1999*, died at 85 after a long battle with heart disease.
