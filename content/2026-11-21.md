Clive Barker's [Hellraiser: Revival](https://bloody-disgusting.com/video-games/3976498/hellraiser-revival-wins-game-of-the-year-at-the-horror-game-awards-2026/) won Game of the Year at The Horror Game Awards, with Doug Bradley taking Best Performance for his return as Pinhead 39 years after the original. Resident Evil Requiem took home the most awards overall.

Osgood Perkins is developing a [Longlegs follow-up](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3976469/longlegs-2-sterling-k-brown-in-talks-to-join-nicolas-cage-in-osgood-perkins-horror-movie/) with Nicolas Cage, and Sterling K. Brown is in talks to join. Details sparse on whether it's sequel, prequel, or spinoff.

Adam Wingard's [Onslaught](https://bloody-disgusting.com/home-video/3976457/adam-wingards-onslaught-sets-holiday-streaming-debut/) (set in The Guest universe) hits HBO Max December 22 with Adria Arjona as a PTSD-haunted Army vet. Wingard demanded to score [Zack Snyder's Escape from New York remake](https://ihorror.com/carpenter-escape-from-new-york-remake-score/) as well—wait, that's John Carpenter. Carpenter says he demanded the composing gig and will rebuild rather than reuse the original score.

Mike Flanagan's [Carrie](https://bloody-disgusting.com/interviews/3976233/carrie-star-amber-midthunder-reveals-deleted-scene-prom-night-secrets/) remake is a hit on Prime Video; Amber Midthunder discusses deleted scenes and working with Flanagan. The same director is producing body horror thriller [Rot](https://rue-morgue.com/mike-flanagan-produced-body-horror-rot-begins-filming/), which begins filming with Matthew Lillard and Midthunder again, marking director Alexandra Magistro's feature debut.

Netflix's [The Body](https://bloody-disgusting.com/news/3976438/the-body-petition-netflix-cancellation-backlash-religious-group-signature/) has crossed 130,000 signatures on a religious group's cancellation petition. The backlash centers on the film's themes and critique of religious institutions.

[FROM](https://bloody-disgusting.com/tv/3976423/horror-series-from-is-a-massive-hit-on-netflix-with-5-billion-minutes-viewed-across-three-weeks/) pulled 5 billion minutes viewed in three weeks after landing on Netflix, introducing the MGM+ series to a wider audience with four seasons already available and a fifth greenlit.

Dan Scully's found-footage project [Peak Form](https://rue-morgue.com/peak-form-muscles-into-the-dark-side-of-online-masculinity/) mines horror from the manosphere.
