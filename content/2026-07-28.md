Zach Cregger tells Empire that [his Resident Evil adaptation is the most violent thing he's made](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3961096/zach-cregger-says-resident-evil-is-the-most-violent-thing-hes-ever-made/), outpacing even Barbarian and Weapons. Details sparse beyond the claim itself.

Emily Robinson's feature directorial debut, [Ugly Cry, has been picked up by Obscured Releasing](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3961090/ugly-cry-body-dysmorphia-horror-movie-acquired-by-obscured-releasing/) for U.S. distribution. The body dysmorphia horror-comedy arrives in theaters this fall.

[Mia'Kate Russell's Penny Lane is Dead](https://bloody-disgusting.com/reviews/3961047/penny-lane-is-dead-review-a-bloody-ozploitation-throwback-fueled-by-punk-rock-energy-fantasia-2026/) played Fantasia as an unhinged Ozploitation riff—a cruel birthday prank spiraling into 80s-throwback violence with a modern sapphic angle. Russell moves from makeup artist to director with infectious energy.

[Adam Wingard's Onslaught](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3960940/onslaught-official-trailer-unstoppable-slashers-enter-the-guest-universe-this-september/) completes a loose trilogy with You're Next and The Guest, set in the same town and built around the same black-ops program. The A24 film leans hard into action—three masked super soldiers painted as heat-seeking missiles. Dan Stevens unrecognizable as a mad scientist.

[Jordan Downey's The Cycle](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3961008/deborah-ann-woll-faces-a-dark-family-secret-in-first-teaser-for-shudders-the-cycle/) arrives on Shudder this fall. Deborah Ann Woll discovers her estranged father's body at a crime scene, triggering a supernatural spiral into family history.

[Mike Flanagan's Carrie teaser](https://bloody-disgusting.com/editorials/3960977/what-mike-flanagans-carrie-teaser-reveals-about-his-bold-new-adaptation/) is out. Flanagan's framing suggests a fresh read on the material rather than another straightforward retelling.

Guillermo del Toro [confirmed no AI was used](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3960987/absolutely-no-goddamn-ai-was-used-to-bring-pans-labyrinth-to-theaters-in-4k-3d-this-october/) to restore Pan's Labyrinth for its October 9 4K 3D theatrical re-release, marking the film's 20th anniversary.

[Rue Morgue called Rubberhead: The Life & Monsters of Steve Johnson](https://rue-morgue.com/fantasia-26-movie-review-creature-effects-are-a-crazy-business-in-rubberhead-the-life-monsters-of-steve-johnson/) "the single greatest film about the art of special makeup ever made" at Fantasia.
