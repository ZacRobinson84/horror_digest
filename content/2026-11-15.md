"The Only One" earns its stripes—[a slasher that actually reinvents the formula](https://www.dreadcentral.com/reviews/597699/the-only-one-review-one-of-the-slasher-subgenres-best-in-years/) with an Australian sensibility and enough charm to justify the hype it'll inevitably get.

Clay McLeod Chapman's ["Mr. Lonelyhearts"](https://rue-morgue.com/tor-nightfire-sets-march-2027-date-for-mr-lonelyhearts/) turns a CPR dummy into a slasher villain. Tor Nightfire publishes March 23, 2027. Details sparse but the premise lands.

[John Minton's directorial debut "Game"](https://ihorror.com/game-trailer-john-minton-geoff-barrow-oscilloscope/) (written and produced by Portishead's Geoff Barrow) traps a raver and a poacher in the woods. Oscilloscope drops it December 4—worth watching for the pedigree alone.

[Michael W. Conrad and Noah Bailey's "The 99 Legs"](https://rue-morgue.com/the-99-legs-arrives-on-comixology-originals-november-17/), a giallo-inspired graphic novel about a crime writer hunted by his own unpublished work, hits Comixology Originals November 17.

Claudio Simonetti [reflected on "Tenebre" and his larger legacy](https://www.fangoria.com/claudio-simonetti-horror-disco-interview/) while discussing a live stage adaptation of the soundtrack with his six-piece band. One of the few interviews worth the time.
