Lucio Fulci's [*Conquest*](https://rue-morgue.com/analog-abattoir-conquest-fulci/) (1983) gets a retrospective look as his post-*Conan* sword-and-sorcery entry—described as an acid trip through gore and sex in the fantasy register. Worth digging into if you've slept on his genre work outside giallo.

[*Evil Follows*](https://rue-morgue.com/movie-review-evil-follows-is-a-solid-demon-posession-flick/), a demon possession film from writer-director Brenden Rudnicki, is heading to Bloodstream. Hits familiar ground but apparently does it with enough style to warrant attention.

[*Yokai Crossing*](https://rue-morgue.com/yokai-crossing-is-a-love-letter-to-j-horror/), an indie game from Jotaz Larraine, translates J-horror sensibility (*Ringu*, *The Grudge*) to PC. If the execution lands, this could scratch an itch the film side isn't filling right now.

[*The Lost Boys* is getting a Broadway adaptation](https://rue-morgue.com/exclusive-interview-the-lost-boys-on-broadway-the-frog-brothers-speak/), and the Frog Brothers are talking about it. Details sparse, but the pairing of that film with live theater is at least conceptually interesting.

[*Soulm8te*](https://dailydead.com/review-soulm8te-is-a-techno-panic-love-letter-that-lacks-a-backbone/) (the M3GAN spinoff) died in release limbo—no marketing, no theatrical, straight to streaming with no critic screenings. The review suggests the film didn't deserve better, but the whole arc is worth noting as a case study in studio panic.
