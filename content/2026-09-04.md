Halsey co-writes and stars in [Replacer](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3966418/karl-glusman-and-red-rooms-star-juliette-gariepy-join-psycho-sexual-horror-movie-replacer/), a psychosexual horror film from Avan Jogia (Backrooms, Resident Evil: Welcome to Raccoon City). Karl Glusman and Juliette Gariépy round out the cast; filming starts in Montreal this month.

[Ravenloft](https://bloody-disgusting.com/tv/3966441/dungeons-dragons-gothic-horror-campaign-ravenloft-becoming-live-action-netflix-series/), the gothic horror D&D campaign, is becoming a live-action Netflix series from John August (Frankenweenie, Big Fish) with Alfonso Cuarón as executive producer.

[Ignore It](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3966421/ignore-it-lily-collias-to-star-in-feature-adaptation-of-viral-youtube-horror-short/), a feature adaptation of Sam Evenson's viral YouTube short, will star Lily Collias (Cape Fear). Temple Hill Entertainment and Anton—the Smile producers—are backing Evenson's directorial feature debut.

Zach Cregger's [Resident Evil](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3966373/resident-evil-watch-an-explosively-bloody-clip-from-zach-creggers-movie/) just released a clip showing Austin Abrams trapped with exploding infected creatures. Details sparse but it's Cregger so worth watching.

[Curse of the Undead](https://bloody-disgusting.com/editorials/3966395/curse-of-the-undead-the-forgotten-horror-western-that-paved-the-way-for-near-dark-and-bone-tomahawk/) (1956), a Universal horror-western hybrid, gets [examined](https://bloody-disgusting.com/editorials/3966395/curse-of-the-undead-the-forgotten-horror-western-that-paved-the-way-for-near-dark-and-bone-tomahawk/) as the proto-template for Near Dark and Bone Tomahawk.

[The 3 Killer Pigs](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3966453/the-3-killer-pigs-trailer-classic-fable-gets-a-horror-movie-twist-this-halloween/), a horror take on the classic fable from director Thomas J. Churchill, hits limited theaters September 25.

[Until Dawn 2](https://bloody-disgusting.com/video-games/3966390/until-dawn-2-will-release-january-2027-watch-the-new-trailer-now/) launches PlayStation 5 on January 28, 2027. Pre-orders open September 10.
