Jang Jae-hyun (Exhuma) is next with [Vampir](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3964854/vampir-a-contract-killer-takes-on-pagans-in-new-horror-movie-from-exhuma-director/), a contract-killer-versus-pagans thriller starring Yoo Ah-in. Shooting this month, targeting 2028. The premise echoes Ben Wheatley's Kill List.

Na Hong-jin's creature feature [Hope](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3964794/see-na-hong-jins-creature-feature-hope-early-with-nationwide-premium-format-screening-event/) (The Wailing) gets early access screenings in 175 PLF locations on September 7, two days before its wide release. Neon's doing a full-court premium push.

Casper Kelly's feature debut [Buddy](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3964748/casper-kellys-buddy-is-hiding-an-analog-horror-arg-across-youtube/) is hiding an analog horror ARG across YouTube. The Too Many Cooks/V/H/S/Halloween mastermind's mascot horror film arrives with the marketing tactics you'd expect.

Benjamin Finkel's [Family](https://bloody-disgusting.com/exclusives/3964485/ruth-wilson-is-absolutely-terrifying-in-creepy-family-trailer/) debuts in limited theaters September 25 with Ruth Wilson terrifying throughout. A 10-year-old summons something when desperation trumps fear.

Alex Ullom's low-budget feature debut [It Ends](https://dailydead.com/review-it-ends-is-a-universal-nightmare-that-is-ultimately-whatever-you-want-it-to-be/) opens this weekend—existential horror about recent grads encountering a liminal nightmare road trip. Multiple outlets are praising its ambiguity and DIY execution.

David Hackl (Saw V) is back with [Solar Storm](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3964830/solar-storm-saw-v-director-david-hackls-next-movie-takes-place-over-24-hours-during-a-blackout/), a neo-noir action thriller unfolding during a 24-hour blackout. Jack Hudson and Lorena Andrea star.

WereBuff Studio's [Kumarn: The Wandering Spirit](https://bloody-disgusting.com/video-games/3964765/wired-productions-inks-deal-to-publish-thai-horror-adventure-title-kumarn-the-wandering-spirit-trailer/)—a Thai folklore-steeped cinematic horror adventure—lands on Steam and Epic in 2027 via Wired Productions.
