Laika's [stop-motion feature *Wildwood*](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3964551/laikas-wildwood-set-to-be-the-longest-stop-motion-animated-film-ever-made/) is shaping up to be the longest in the studio's catalog—details sparse, but the October 23 release will tell.

Nicolas Cage is [heading into post-apocalyptic territory for *Parish*](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3964449/nicolas-cage-battles-cannibals-in-nuclear-apocalypse-action-horror-movie-parish/), a cannibals-and-survival thriller set 30 years after nuclear collapse. Cage playing a hardened survivor feels right.

Alex Ullom's follow-up to the well-received *It Ends* is [*4 X 4: The Event*](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3964431/4-x-4-the-event-sophia-lillis-dylan-minnette-more-join-new-horror-movie-from-it-ends-director/), which just wrapped with Sophia Lillis, Dylan Minnette, Jacob Tremblay, and Jaden Smith. Eight young cast members for what sounds like another ensemble piece.

Peacock's [*Crystal Lake* prequel series](https://bloody-disgusting.com/tv/3964464/crystal-lake-premiere-episode-will-screen-at-new-york-comic-con-in-october/) arrives October 15, with the premiere screening at NYCC first. The Friday the 13th spinoff is at least attempting something.

Kevin Bacon and Kyra Sedgwick's [meta horror-comedy *Family Movie*](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3964460/kevin-bacon-kyra-sedgwicks-meta-horror-comedy-family-movie-sets-2027-release/) (a filmmaking family's low-budget horror turns real) lands April 23, 2027 via Variance.

Fantastic Fest's 21st edition (September 17–24) is [stacked](https://www.fangoria.com/4-x-4-the-event-alex-ullom/), with *Red Snow* (a Finnish vampire-as-Santa comedy) among the highlights.

**Worth reading in full:** [Shudder's *Season of Screams* runs September through December](https://bloody-disgusting.com/exclusives/3964343/shudders-season-of-screams-returns-with-packed-halloween-slate-and-all-new-ghoul-log-from-hallowarrior-filmmakers/).
