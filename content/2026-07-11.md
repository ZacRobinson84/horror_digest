Sébastien Vaniček's [Evil Dead Burn](https://bloody-disgusting.com/editorials/3958907/evil-dead-burn-post-credit-scene-explained/) arrives this weekend with post-credit scenes that expand the franchise—skip them at your peril. The film follows a demonic siege on a grieving family, and Vaniček has already pitched a [vision for The Mask](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3958921/evil-dead-burn-director-wants-to-bring-the-mask-back-to-life-with-a-violent-dark-movie/) as the violent horror comic it was meant to be before Jim Carrey's 1994 detour.

[The Terror](https://bloody-disgusting.com/tv/3958949/the-terror-will-return-for-season-4-with-another-literary-horror-story/) is returning for Season 4 on AMC. The team has already closed a deal on their next literary adaptation—no other details yet.

[Paul Feig will direct Detention](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3958893/detention-paul-feig-directing-mysterious-horror-movie-for-blumhouse-atomic-monster/) for Blumhouse Atomic Monster and Platinum Dunes, with a spec script from Daniel Gold. Details are sparse beyond the title and talent.

[Attack of the Killer Tomatoes: Organic Intelligence](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3959025/attack-of-the-killer-tomatoes-organic-intelligence-poster-announces-august-release-date/) hits theaters in August. The franchise's fifth installment will debut its trailer at San Diego Comic-Con this month.

[Manhunter](https://rue-morgue.com/see-manhunter-sharper-than-ever-in-4k-restoration-trailer/) arrives in select U.S. theaters July 24 in a new 4K restoration for its 40th anniversary. Mann's adaptation of Red Dragon gets a proper theatrical showing at last.

[Dan Trachtenberg is directing Freddy the 13th](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3958885/freddy-the-13th-dan-trachtenbergs-animated-horror-comedy-gets-2028-release-date/) for Paramount Animation, adapting the indie comic from Yehudi Mercado. The film lands October 13, 2028—yes, a Friday the 13th.

[LA's Academy Museum opens "The Horror Show"](https://rue-morgue.com/las-academy-museum-celebrates-the-genre-with-the-horror-show-beginning-this-fall/) this fall, a major exhibition on screen horror with significant genre representation.
