Yeon Sang-ho's [Colony premieres at Popcorn Frights](https://www.fangoria.com/popcorn-frights-2026-first-wave-lineup/) (Aug 6-16), opening the 12th annual festival alongside restored prints of Train to Busan and Scanners. The lineup leans hard on both new work and genre essentials—interactive screenings, midnight slots, the usual bloodletting.

[Agnieszka Smoczyńska's Hot Spot](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3958660/hot-spot-starring-noomi-rapace-rated-r-for-gore-and-graphic-nudity/) (The Lure) lands August 21 via Focus Features with an R rating for gore and graphic nudity. Dystopian sci-fi thriller with Noomi Rapace. No plot details yet, but Smoczyńska doesn't do restraint.

David Howard Thornton (Terrifier) joins [Behind the Mask II: The Return of Leslie Vernon](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3958634/art-meets-leslie-david-howard-thornton-joins-behind-the-mask-ii-the-return-of-leslie-vernon/) in a key role. That's a solid pairing if the script knows what to do with it.

[Evil Dead Burn](https://dailydead.com/review-evil-dead-burn-is-a-mean-slice-of-deadite-devastation/) hits theaters July 10. Sébastien Vaniček (Infested) directs. Early word splits on the violence level—some call it the meanest entry yet, others say it tips past catharsis into endurance test. Worth seeing to decide for yourself.

The [Academy Museum is mounting The Horror Show](https://dailydead.com/the-horror-show-exhibition-is-coming-to-the-academy-museum-special-events-and-screenings-announced/), opening September 26. A 10-month survey with a John Carpenter: Prince of Darkness series embedded in the run. First museum-level treatment of the genre in a while.

Steve Niles and Andrea Mutti's graphic novel [Callisto](https://dailydead.com/preview-steve-niles-brings-his-unique-brand-of-horror-to-the-moon-of-callisto-with-artist-andrea-mutti/) arrives soon—sci-fi noir-horror set on Jupiter's moon, asking whether we can escape our problems by leaving Earth. Niles in his wheelhouse.

[Twisted Tower](https://bloody-disgusting.com/video-games/3958606/upcoming-willy-wonka-meets-bioshock-fps-twisted-tower-launches-on-august-18-trailer/) (Atmos Games) launches August 18. FPS described as "Willy Wonka meets BioShock." Details sparse, but the premise alone warrants attention.
