Ridley Scott's reteaming with "Raised by Wolves" creator Aaron Guzikowski on a new [Alien film](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3973162/ridley-scott-reteams-with-raised-by-wolves-creator-on-new-alien-movie/) that won't be a Romulus sequel. The script reportedly picks up threads from Prometheus and Covenant, though details remain sealed.

Ryū Murakami, author of the [1997 novel Audition](https://bloody-disgusting.com/news/3973118/audition-author-ryu-murakami-has-passed-away-at-74/) that became Takashi Miike's landmark film, has died at 74. His work bridged transgressive literature and extreme cinema.

Tom Skerritt, who played Captain Dallas in the original Alien, [has passed away at 93](https://rue-morgue.com/alien-star-tom-skerritt-dies-at-93/). A reliable presence across decades of genre and prestige work.

Henry Chaisson's feature debut, the gothic horror-thriller [Recluse](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3973076/recluse-tribeca-horror-movie-from-antlers-writer-getting-2027-release-from-vertical/), is getting a 2027 release from Vertical after premiering at Tribeca. Chaisson wrote Antlers and "Servant"; this one reportedly prioritizes sound design.

Shudder's final [Last Drive-In Halloween special](https://bloody-disgusting.com/the-further/3973060/shudders-final-the-last-drive-in-halloween-special-premieres-tonight-at-9pm-et/) airs tonight at 9pm ET as part of a four-special sendoff before the series wraps for good.

Alexandre Aja's [Crawl sequel](https://www.dreadcentral.com/news/595157/crawl-sequel-richard-brake-cast/) (July 2027) adds Richard Brake, Sarah Niles, Ray Panthaki, Charley Palmer Rothwell, and Flula Borg to the cast.

Jason Eisener (Hobo with a Shotgun) directed [Project Shamrock](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3973044/project-shamrock-jason-eiseners-must-watch-fan-film-connects-halloween-iii-and-michael-myers/), a fan film that connects Halloween III: Season of the Witch back to Michael Myers—rare territory for Halloween fandom.
