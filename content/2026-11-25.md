Robert Eggers' [Werwulf drops its first scene](https://ihorror.com/this-week-in-horror-werwulf-drops-its-first-clip-carrie-season-2-gets-a-script-and-art-the-clown-opens-his-bag/), a month before release. Medieval werewolf, lore-accurate. That's the entire pitch and it works.

Mike Flanagan's [The Exorcist: Martyrs](https://rue-morgue.com/the-exorcist-martyrs-earns-an-r-rating/) lands March 12 with Scarlett Johansson. R-rated for bloody violence and grisly images. Flanagan claims it's his most terrifying work to date, which is a statement.

[Osgood Perkins' The Young People](https://ihorror.com/the-young-people-rated-r-osgood-perkins-neon/) (NEON) stars Nicole Kidman, Lola Tung, Nico Parker. R for strong bloody violence, gore, and teen drug use. Details sparse but the cast and rating suggest Perkins isn't holding back after Longlegs and The Monkey.

[Damian Mc Carthy's Oddity](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3976786/oddity-movie-damian-mc-carty-4k-blu-ray/) (his follow-up to 2026's Hokum) is getting a 4K restoration from Severin Films. Worth grabbing if you missed it.

[In a Violent Nature 2](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3976872/in-a-violent-nature-2-new-look-at-the-return-of-johnny/) hits theaters February 12 with Ry Barrett back as the masked slasher Johnny. New image confirms the body count continues.

[American Dollhouse](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3976843/american-dollhouse-trailer-unwraps-a-psychotic-neighbors-christmas-obsession-ahead-of-release/) (Dark Sky Films, January 15) leans into Christmas slasher territory with a psychotic neighbor angle. Psychological horror à la Psycho and Black Christmas.

Francis Lawrence [discusses why Constantine got an R rating](https://www.dreadcentral.com/comics-books/598819/constantine-mpaa-r-rating-francis-lawrence/) and hints the sequel will push further. Demons, apparently, are more controversial than orcs.
