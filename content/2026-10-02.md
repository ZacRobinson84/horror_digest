Romero's final zombie film wraps with Kate Beckinsale and Betty Gabriel starring in [*Twilight of the Dead*](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3970240/twilight-of-the-dead-filming-wraps-on-kate-beckinsale-zombie-movie-based-on-george-a-romero-story/), based on a treatment he wrote before his death. Positioned as the last chapter of his saga.

Sam Raimi and Jordan Peele are producing together for the first time on [*Portrait of God*](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3970166/chase-infiniti-to-star-in-lionsgates-portrait-of-god-with-sam-raimi-and-jordan-peele-producing/), a Lionsgate feature adaptation of a viral short. Chase Infiniti stars in the religious horror film.

Jeremy Saulnier's Halloween-set fugitive thriller [*Slayground*](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3970184/slayground-posters-from-a24-welcome-you-to-a-haunted-house-with-no-exit/) drops new A24 posters. Stars Imogen Poots, James Badge Dale, and Matty Matheson.

*River*, an acclaimed slasher with Jane Levy and Jessica Rothe that premiered at Toronto, lands a January 15, 2027 theatrical release through Universal and Blumhouse Atomic Monster after a $10 million acquisition.

Bong Joon-ho's [*The Host*](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3970112/bong-joon-hos-the-host-heads-to-us-theaters-with-4k-restoration/) gets a 4K restoration and returns to US theaters November 20 for its 20th anniversary, with an exclusive conversation with the director included.

Joachim Rønning (*Tron: Ares*, *Pirates of the Caribbean: Dead Men Tell No Tales*) is directing a mysterious [*System*](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3970088/mystery-horror-movie-system-from-tron-ares-director-joachim-ronning-begins-filming/) from his own script. Currently filming in London; cast and plot remain under wraps.

Michael Dougherty launches [*Sam: The Soul of Halloween*](https://ihorror.com/sam-the-soul-of-halloween-michael-dougherty-launch/), a multimedia hub celebrating the *Trick 'r Treat* mascot's 30th anniversary with new shorts, merchandise, and content throughout the month.
