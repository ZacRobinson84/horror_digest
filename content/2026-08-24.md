Yeon Sang-ho's [*Colony*](https://bloody-disgusting.com/reviews/3964274/colony-review-yeon-sang-hos-pulse-pounding-zombie-thriller-takes-aim-at-ai/) continues his run of weaponizing zombie premises toward contemporary targets—this time AI and individualism sit alongside the usual corporate grotesquerie. He's done the work Romero left on the table.

[*SKINFACE*](https://bloody-disgusting.com/video-games/3964882/pose-as-your-victims-friend-by-wearing-their-face-in-dark-hitman-game-skinface-trailer/) is a hitman game where you steal and wear your target's face to move through their social circles. Adam Pype and Ties Groen (both *No Players Online*) built this from an itch prototype into something darker than the premise wants to admit.

[*MERMADE*](https://rue-morgue.com/mermade-takes-body-horror-under-the-sea/) launches on itch.io as aquatic body horror without the Disney coating. Details sparse, but the pitch—no romance, no singing—suggests something genuinely committed to discomfort.

*Rock, Paper, Severed* rounds out its playable roster with Eleanor Ashford, a gambler with an abusive backstory stepping up to a high-stakes table. Card game with teeth.

[*From Dusk Till Dawn*](https://rue-morgue.com/tie-in-me-up-tie-in-me-down-from-dusk-till-dawn-is-a-full-blooded-novelization-of-a-cult-classic/) gets a novelization—Rodriguez's '96 vampire crime epic finally hits the page.

Box office: the new *Insidious* opened to $25 million. *Camp Miasma* flatlined around $2 million.
