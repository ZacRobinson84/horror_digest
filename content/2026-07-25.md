Chuck Russell, director of *A Nightmare on Elm Street 3: Dream Warriors* and *The Blob*, [has passed away at 74](https://bloody-disgusting.com/news/3960851/the-blob-and-witchboard-filmmaker-chuck-russell-has-passed-away-at-74/). Russell's career spanned directing, writing, and producing, with his breakout *Dream Warriors* defining the trajectory of the franchise.

AMC has [renewed *Interview with the Vampire* for season four](https://bloody-disgusting.com/tv/3960866/interview-with-the-vampire-gets-official-season-4-renewal-and-rebrand-as-queen-of-the-damned/), officially retitling it *Anne Rice's Queen of the Damned*. The announcement came during Comic-Con's Hall H panel, with Jacob Anderson, Assad Zaman, Eric Bogosian, and Sheila Atim in attendance.

Nicolas Winding Refn's [*Her Private Hell*](https://bloody-disgusting.com/reviews/3960830/her-private-hell-review-nicolas-winding-refn-returns-with-a-hypnotic-inscrutable-neon-fever-dream/) is now in theaters—his first feature in a decade, made after a near-fatal heart attack in 2023. The film is described as a hypnotic neon fever dream.

Jacques Audiard is [adapting the French horror novel *La Nuit ravagée* for Netflix](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3960897/netflix-reteams-with-emilia-perez-director-on-la-nuit-ravagee-horror-adaptation/), reuniting with the streamer after *Emilia Perez*. The novel by Jean-Baptiste Del Amo is set in a fictional suburb of Toulouse.

Adam Wingard confirmed that [*Onslaught* shares a universe with *The Guest*](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3960802/adam-wingards-onslaught-takes-place-in-the-same-town-and-same-universe-as-the-guest/), taking place in the same town. Both films are cut from similar cloth—visceral, tight genre work.

Ti West's *Ebenezer* trailer dropped, [reframing *A Christmas Carol* as a ghost story](https://ihorror.com/ebenezer-trailer-ti-west-ghost-story/) with Johnny Depp. The film leans into the Dickens original's supernatural framework rather than treating it as a redemption narrative.
