Richard Riehle, the character actor who appeared in Adam Green's *Hatchet* and Rob Zombie's *Chillerama*, [died at 78](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3972536/richard-riehle-death-obituary/) over the past weekend.

Jeremy Saulnier's haunted-attraction thriller [*Slayground*](https://rue-morgue.com/first-trailer-for-jeremy-saulniers-haunted-attraction-thriller-slayground/) hits A24 on January 29. A scare actor wakes in the attraction surrounded by dead coworkers with no memory of how they got there—then a manhunt closes in. The *Green Room* director's latest trades violence for psychological dread.

George A. Romero's long-lost 1962 short *Expostulations*, feared destroyed, [surfaces for a one-night premiere](https://rue-morgue.com/george-a-romeros-long-lost-first-film-expostulations-to-premiere-at-pittsburgh-symphony-event/) next March at a Pittsburgh Symphony event with a new live orchestral score.

Joseph and Vanessa Winter (*Deadstream*) are [adapting the viral Reddit short *Future Me*](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3972555/deadstream-directors-joseph-and-vanessa-winter-to-helm-reddit-nosleep-horror-movie-future-me/) for Longevity Pictures and Spooky Pictures. Details sparse, but the Reddit nosleep pipeline keeps producing.

Diablo Cody says the long-awaited [*Jennifer's Body* sequel](https://rue-morgue.com/diablo-cody-says-jennifers-body-2-will-make-its-demon-more-sympathetic/) will dig into the demon's motivations—making it more sympathetic than the original's man-eating possession setup.

Japanese horror stalwarts Kiyoshi Kurosawa, Takashi Shimizu and others are uniting for [*Night Tales Club*](https://www.fangoria.com/night-tales-club-j-horror-series/), a new supernatural anthology series. First teaser is up.

Keanu Reeves is [in talks for James Wan's English-language remake](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3972604/keanu-reeves-in-talks-to-star-in-james-wans-the-gangster-the-cop-the-devil-remake/) of the 2019 Korean thriller *The Gangster, the Cop, the Devil*, with original star Ma Dong-seok also attached.
