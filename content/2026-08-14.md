Neil Marshall writing a serial killer thriller for Aaron Mirtes to direct, with Charlotte Kirk starring in [The Spiral Down](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3963549/the-spiral-down-neil-marshall-writing-serial-killer-thriller-starring-charlotte-kirk/). Kirk plays a woman kidnapped by a perfectionist killer; the plot involves detectives closing in as the dynamic shifts. Details sparse otherwise.

Ken Russell's [The Devils](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3963488/ken-russells-the-devils-4k-restoration-trailer-unveils-never-before-seen-footage/) gets its first-ever Director's Cut restoration—uncensored, NC-17, with never-before-seen footage. The 1971 film returns to theaters this October as the only version Russell wanted audiences to see.

Mike P. Nelson ([Wrong Turn](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3963458/beware-boiuna-trailer-awakens-a-colossal-ancient-snake-deep-in-the-amazon/), [V/H/S/85](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3963458/beware-boiuna-trailer-awakens-a-colossal-ancient-snake-deep-in-the-amazon/)) directs [Beware Boiúna](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3963458/beware-boiuna-trailer-awakens-a-colossal-ancient-snake-deep-in-the-amazon/), a creature feature about a medical team in the Amazon facing an ancient serpent. Jessica Rothe stars. Hits theaters October 2.

Na Hong-jin ([The Wailing](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3963501/hope-behind-the-scenes-featurette-highlights-high-octane-action/)) directs the Korean monster movie [Hope](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3963501/hope-behind-the-scenes-featurette-highlights-high-octane-action/), dropping September 9 via NEON. A creature terrorizes a remote South Korean village; expect high-octane action.

[Creepshow](https://bloody-disgusting.com/video-games/3963526/dreadxps-take-on-horror-anthology-creepshow-launches-today-trailer/) hits Steam today—DreadXP and PHL Games' interactive take on the Shudder series, episodic and inspired by the anthology format. 20% launch discount through August 27.

[Dark Hollow](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3963517/dark-hollow-trailer-unearths-appalachian-creature-feature/) (Daniel Byers) unleashes something inhuman from an Appalachian coal mine. Brainstorm Media releases it in theaters and on-demand September 4—old-school creature feature vibes, heavy on regional atmosphere.

André Øvredal's [Passenger](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3963483/van-life-horror-passenger-heads-to-paramount-next-week/) streams Paramount+ August 20. Supernatural road-trip horror involving a van-life couple a few weeks into their adventure; Øvredal's track record ([The Autopsy of Jane Doe](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3963483/van-life-horror-passenger-heads-to-paramount-next-week/), [Scary Stories to Tell in the Dark](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3963483/van-life-horror-passenger-heads-to-paramount-next-week/)) suggests competence.

John Carpenter's [Assault on Precinct 13](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3963523/tower-defense-paramount-primal-movie-described-as-medieval-supernatural-version-of-assault-on-precinct-13/) serves as the template for Paramount Primal's [Tower Defense](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3963523/tower-defense-paramount-primal-movie-described-as-medieval-supernatural-version-of-assault-on-precinct-13/)—a medieval supernatural siege film drawing from the Carpenter playbook.
