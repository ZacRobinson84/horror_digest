Maika Monroe's returning in a [Victorian Gothic adaptation](https://rue-morgue.com/exclusive-interview-maika-monroe-goes-victorian-psycho/) of Virginia Feito's novel—Rue Morgue has an interview. Details sparse on the project itself, but Monroe's track record suggests it won't be a straightforward period piece.

Geena Davis is [playing Dorothea Puente](https://ihorror.com/geena-davis-to-portray-serial-killer-dorothea-puente-in-hbo-series/) in an HBO limited series about the Sacramento boardinghouse serial killer. A shift from her usual victim roles.

*Evil Dead* Burns releasing this month. One reviewer [found it genuinely transgressive](https://ihorror.com/evil-dead-burn-is-filth-and-it-knows-it/)—the kind of thing that lands somewhere between laughter and genuine discomfort in a theater, which is rarer than you'd think from a studio franchise entry.
