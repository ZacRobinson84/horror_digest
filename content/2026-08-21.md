Richard L. Anderson, Oscar-winning sound designer from *Poltergeist* and *Raiders of the Lost Ark*, is writing and directing an '80s-set [creature feature called *Wekiwa*](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3964649/poltergeist-sound-designer-richard-l-anderson-to-direct-80s-creature-feature-wekiwa-starring-billy-zane/) starring Billy Zane, Kevin Chapman, and C.S. Lee. Production starts next month near Orlando. First-time director work with an impressive pedigree behind the camera.

Emily Bennett and Justin Brooks' [*Blood Shine*](https://bloody-disgusting.com/reviews/3964674/blood-shine-review-ambitious-psychosexual-cult-horror-fueled-by-faith-and-body-horror/) expands beyond their previous single-location work into something bigger—a psychosexual cult film mixing faith and body horror with genuine ambition.

Kevin Kölsch and Dennis Widmyer (*Starry Eyes*, *Pet Sematary*) are weaponizing quicksand in [*The Swallow*](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3964603/the-swallow-quicksand-creature-feature-will-world-premiere-at-fantastic-fest/), which world premieres at Fantastic Fest this year. An aggressively weird creature concept from two directors worth tracking.

Yeon Sang-ho (*Train to Busan*) returns with [*Colony*](https://rue-morgue.com/exclusive-clip-horror-master-yeon-sang-ho-returns-with-colony/), a zombie film that sidesteps the usual plague narrative to explore collective thinking instead. Early clips and an interview suggest a refreshingly different take on the subgenre.

*Yellowjackets* Season 4, the final season, [premieres November 20 on Paramount+](https://bloody-disgusting.com/tv/3964630/yellowjackets-season-4-premiere/). Season 3 broke records for the streamer.

Stephen King called the Japanese horror film [*Never After Dark*](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3964615/never-after-dark-trailer-stephen-king-called-this-horror-movie-amazing-and-haunting/) "amazing and haunting." It hits U.S. theaters this Halloween season via Magnolia Pictures.
