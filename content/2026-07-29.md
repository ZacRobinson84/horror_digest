Todd McFarlane revealed at San Diego Comic-Con that he's completed an [animated Spawn film](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3961181/todd-mcfarlane-reveals-hes-sitting-on-unreleased-animated-spawn-movie-starring-keith-david/) starring Keith David and Mark Hamill. No release date yet, while the live-action Blumhouse project remains stalled.

Mike Flanagan is in talks to [executive produce Amazon MGM's Warhammer 40,000 series](https://bloody-disgusting.com/tv/3961251/mike-flanagan-in-talks-to-executive-produce-warhammer-40000-series-starring-henry-cavill/) with Henry Cavill. The project has been in development for years; details sparse on where it lands with Flanagan involved.

Theo James Krekis' short film *Jealous People Are Ugly People* won a [bidding war](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3961144/jealous-people-are-ugly-people-lands-at-new-line-cinema-after-huge-bidding-war/) and landed at New Line Cinema for a feature expansion. Premiered at SXSW earlier this year.

Netflix's [*Below*](https://bloody-disgusting.com/tv/3961196/below-teaser-trailer-a-sea-creature-stalks-josh-hartnett-in-netflix-horror-series/) drops in October with Josh Hartnett as a fisherman protecting his family from a sea creature. From *Umbrella Academy* creator Jesse McKeown.

*Bury the Devil,* an [exorcism film shot in a single continuous take](https://bloody-disgusting.com/exclusives/3961071/bury-the-devil-trailer-unfurls-real-time-possession-horror-in-single-continuous-take/), hits select theaters and VOD August 21. Directed by Adam O'Brien.

*Evil Dead Burn* arrives [digitally August 4](https://bloody-disgusting.com/exclusives/3961154/evil-dead-burn-heads-home-to-digital-in-august-and-physical-media-in-september-exclusive/), physical media September 22. Sébastien Vaniček's entry is the franchise's most recent theatrical run.

Popcorn Frights (August 6–16 in South Florida) [announced its short film slate](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3961168/popcorn-frights-2026-short-films-lineup/): 72 shorts across seven programs, including 13 world premieres, available in-theater and online.
