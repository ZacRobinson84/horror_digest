Michael Dougherty celebrated the 30th anniversary of *Season's Greetings* with [a grindhouse-style sequel](https://bloody-disgusting.com/the-further/3974038/seasons-greetings-2-mischief-night-halloween-icon-sam-is-back-in-new-grindhouse-style-trailer/) featuring Sam, the trick-or-treating pumpkin-headed killer from the original short. *Season's Greetings 2: Mischief Night* dropped today.

Karyn Kusama is [set to direct a *Mouthwashing* adaptation](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3973975/mouthwashing-karyn-kusama-directing-movie-adaptation-of-popular-horror-video-game/) at Sony, with Scott Derrickson producing. The horror game, which just released on consoles, centers on a spacecraft crew in crisis.

[Ken Russell's *The Devils*](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3973962/nc-17-directors-cut-of-ken-russells-the-devils-staying-in-theaters-indefinitely/)—the notorious 1971 occult film—has extended its theatrical run indefinitely in an NC-17 director's cut. Warner Bros. dropped the one-week window after strong demand.

Sir Patrick Stewart [died at 86](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3973969/patrick-stewart-dies-86-obituary/). Beyond his sci-fi fame, he appeared in Tobe Hooper's *Lifeforce* and Jeremy Saulnier's *Green Room*.

Peter Medak, who directed the [1980 haunted-house classic *The Changeling*](https://rue-morgue.com/the-changeling-director-peter-medak-dies-at-88/), died at 88 in Los Angeles.

[*Pendulum*](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3973944/pendulum-trailer-takes-joseph-gordon-levitt-and-phoebe-dynevor-on-a-terrifying-spiritual-journey/), the directorial debut of *Black Swan* co-writer Mark Heyman, drops January 1. Joseph Gordon-Levitt and Phoebe Dynerov get caught in a spiritual retreat gone wrong.

Guillermo del Toro's [*Pan's Labyrinth*](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3973961/pans-labyrinth-20th-anniversary-4k-home-video-release-date/) arrives on digital next month in a 20th-anniversary restoration.
