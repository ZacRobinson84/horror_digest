Rob Savage's next, *Other Mommy*, adapted from Josh Malerman's *Incidents Around the House*, got its first trailer attached to Christopher Nolan's *The Odyssey*. Jessica Chastain stars. Details sparse, but Savage's *Host* suggests he understands how to build dread in confined spaces.

Maya Annik Bedward's *Black Zombie* [opens in select theaters](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3960013/black-zombie-documentary-tracing-evolution-of-zombie-cinema-sets-september-release/) this September. A documentary tracing the zombie from Haitian spiritual traditions through modern cinema—essentially a cultural reclamation wrapped around film history. Worth more than the usual genre doc.

Sydney Sweeney will star in and produce *Hollow*, a *Sleepy Hollow* retelling from *Pet Sematary: Bloodlines* director Lindsey Anderson Beer, now picked up by Sony. The hook: it centers Katrina Van Tassel instead of Ichabod Crane.

The contemporary *Herbert West: Reanimator* is shooting in Alton, Illinois with Joseph Morgan and Katie Cassidy. Script by Adam Simon and Tim Metcalfe (*The Haunting in Connecticut*). Lovecraft adaptations are a dime a dozen, but worth tracking if the execution lands.

*Namaslay* [drops August 6 in NY/LA](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3960022/namaslay-teaser-skewers-yoga-culture-with-masala-horror-satire/) before wider release. A masala-cinema horror satire dismantling wellness-industry appropriation. The teaser suggests actual teeth beneath the satire.

Peacock's *Crystal Lake* [teaser finally arrived](https://bloody-disgusting.com/editorials/3959832/the-forgotten-pamela-voorhees-backstory-that-could-shape-peacocks-crystal-lake-series/), first *Friday the 13th* footage in 17 years. Linda Cardellini as Pamela Voorhees. No frame yet of the actual series, but the existence of new *Friday* content after the long legal freeze is news.

Adam Wingard's *Onslaught* [stars Michael Biehn](https://dailydead.com/interview-michael-biehn-hypes-adam-wingards-onslaught-discusses-re-uniting-with-arnold-schwarzenegger-on-the-kellys-and-reflects-on-40-years-of-aliens/) in what he describes as "pedal-to-the-metal action, horror, and violence." Wingard directing action-horror is always worth the attention.
