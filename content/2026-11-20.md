Bill Hader's directorial debut, the psychological horror *They Know*, [lands with Black Bear](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3976398/bill-hader-they-know-movie-black-bear-release/). Hader stars opposite Linda Cardellini as a divorced father convinced his ex's new boyfriend is harming his daughters. In post-production.

[*Local 58*](https://bloody-disgusting.com/tv/3976335/analog-horror-trailblazer-local-58-brings-every-episode-to-shudder/), Kris Straub's analog horror series, is coming to Shudder in full next month. A new season debuted on the platform last month via Joe Bob Briggs' Halloween episode before quietly rolling out additional episodes on Shudder TV.

Phantom Fest's 2027 edition includes [a live-score screening of Argento's *Suspiria*](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3976392/phantom-fest-2027-first-wave/). Houston's festival for horror, sci-fi, and the weird runs four days at the historic River Oaks Theatre.

Jamie and Corinne Foxx are developing *Buried*, a [supernatural horror series with *The Conjuring* writers](https://bloody-disgusting.com/news/3976347/buried-tv-series-jamie-foxx/). It's drawn from the Black Hope case in Crosby, Texas—a real incident involving a cemetery displaced by suburban development.

John Carpenter [will score Zack Snyder's *Escape from New York* remake](https://www.fangoria.com/john-carpenter-score-zack-snyder-escape-from-new-york-remake/). Carpenter composed the original; Snyder directing the redo keeps him in the fold, at least musically.

Angus Silver's *Insectasy*, a [Fantasia standout about bug obsession](https://rue-morgue.com/fantasia-favourite-insectasy-lands-worldwide-sales-deal/), has secured worldwide sales representation. Details sparse on distribution timeline.

Disney+ cast [*Afterlife with Archie*](https://ihorror.com/afterlife-with-archie-cast-disney-plus-vasco-faria/) for its Halloween 2027 debut—a live-action zombie Riverdale from Roberto Aguirre-Sacasa.
