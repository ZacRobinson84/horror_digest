Sam Neill [died Monday in Sydney at 78](https://ihorror.com/remembering-sam-neill/). His roles—In the Mouth of Madness, Event Horizon, The Possession—turned on watching competent men lose their grip on reality. He stayed working until the end.

Georgia Bernstein and Cemre Paksoy discuss [bringing nurseploitation back](https://rue-morgue.com/exclusive-interview-georgia-bernstein-and-cemre-paksoy-on-night-nurse-and-the-return-of-nurseploitation/) with "Night Nurse," blending trauma and titillation in a subgenre that's been dormant long enough to deserve the look.

Brett McBean [novelizes Jamie Blanks' Storm Warning](https://rue-morgue.com/tie-in-me-up-tie-in-me-down-storm-warning-the-novelization-is-a-turbulent-work-of-2000s-ozploitation/), adapting the 2007 Australian home-invasion film with something beyond tie-in mechanics.

"Dead Estate," the gory roguelike from 2021, [arrives on PS5 and Switch](https://bloody-disgusting.com/video-games/3959054/action-roguelike-dead-estate-now-on-ps5-and-nintendo-switch-trailer/) with all expansions intact, including the survival-horror campaign "Assignment Anya."

Tiago Rech's psychological horror adventure [I SHALL NAME THE DEAD](https://bloody-disgusting.com/video-games/3959052/explore-the-cemetery-and-give-names-to-the-deceased-in-i-shall-name-the-dead-trailer/) is headed to Steam in early 2027. Details sparse beyond the cemetery premise.
