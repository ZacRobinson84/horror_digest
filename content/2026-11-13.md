Steve Miner is directing a new Friday the 13th film—the franchise's 13th feature and Jason's first theatrical outing in nearly 20 years. Miner helmed Parts II and III, so the math checks out. [Announced on actual Friday the 13th](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3975590/jason-voorhees-returning-to-theaters-with-13th-feature-film-directed-by-steve-miner/), naturally.

Don Mancini is developing a new Chucky film under Independent Artist Group after nearly a decade since the franchise's last theatrical entry. [Details sparse](https://rue-morgue.com/don-mancini-to-develop-a-new-chucky-film/) on what's in the works.

Wolfenstein 3 is officially in production at MachineGames, nine years after The New Colossus. [Xbox CEO Asha Sharma confirmed it](https://bloody-disgusting.com/video-games/3975649/wolfenstein-3-now-in-production-at-machinegames/) after casting leaks had already outed the project.

New Line has acquired remake rights to Paolo Strippoli's The Spiral, Italy's Oscar entry. The Faustian horror film will get both a theatrical release and an English-language remake, with [New Line winning a competitive bid](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3975601/new-line-cinema-acquires-italys-oscar-entry-the-spiral-for-awards-season-release-and-english-language-remake/).

Takashi Yamazaki would like to create an original kaiju for a third Godzilla film, assuming Toho greenlights it. Godzilla Minus Zero's $67 million worldwide haul in a week suggests [the appetite is there](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3975610/takashi-yamazaki-would-love-to-create-an-original-kaiju-for-a-third-godzilla-movie/).

David Mackenzie's untitled Oregon psychological thriller has wrapped and cast Zazie Beetz, Marin Ireland, and Brendan Sexton III. The Hell or High Water director's venture into the Pacific Northwest is described as a coming-of-age horror piece, [with Riley Prior as its lead](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3975605/untitled-oregon-project/).

Stacy Keach, who appeared in John Carpenter's Body Bags, Road Games, and The Ninth Configuration, [died Tuesday at 85](https://ihorror.com/stacy-keach-dead-85-body-bags-road-games-ninth-configuration/).
