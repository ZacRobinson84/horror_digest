Dario Argento is directing a new horror film with Isabelle Huppert attached. Details are sparse—no title, not yet on IMDb—but the project reportedly aims to be [the bloodiest of his career](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3958331/dario-argentos-next-horror-movie-stars-isabelle-huppert-and-may-be-the-bloodiest-of-his-career/).

The next V/H/S installment will [adapt the SCP Foundation](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3958435/v-h-s-scp-next-v-h-s-installment-takes-on-the-scp-foundation/), the collaborative online fiction project. Roy Lee produces; Spooky Pictures and Image Nation are handling production. It's the first feature-length treatment of the SCP universe.

Vera Drew (The People's Joker) is directing a cosmic found-footage horror comedy called [*Trandemic*](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3958395/trandemic-vera-drew-directing-cosmic-found-footage-horror-comedy/), funded through the Transgender Film Center's new Found Footage Feature Fund. This is the fund's first recipient.

Robbie Banfitch (*The Outwaters*) has a new found-footage film, [*Tinsman Road*](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3958349/found-footage-tinsman-road-available-to-watch-for-limited-time/), available for rent exclusively through Letterboxd Video Store's Unreleased Gems shelf through July 31.

Curry Barker's *Obsession* has [crossed $400 million worldwide](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3958301/obsession-just-passed-400-million-at-the-worldwide-box-office/), doubling its total in a month since hitting $200 million in early June. Still the highest-grossing horror film of 2026.

A24's *Backrooms* has a [1-hour ambience video](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3958354/a24-invites-you-into-the-backrooms-with-hour-long-ambience-video/) live on YouTube ahead of its digital release, following the extended "Everything Must Go Edition" that added 16 minutes of bonus footage.
