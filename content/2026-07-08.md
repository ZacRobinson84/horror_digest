David Lowery adapting John Langan's Bram Stoker Award-winning novel [*The Fisherman*](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3958526/the-fisherman-david-lowery-directing-film-adaptation-of-john-langans-horror-novel/) for Focus Features. The story follows two widowers bonding over fishing trips in upstate New York; Lowery's track record (*The Green Knight*, *Mother Mary*) suggests this won't be straightforward grief allegory.

Takashi Yamazaki (*Godzilla Minus One*) [directing sci-fi epic *Nue*](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3958564/nue-godzilla-minus-one-filmmaker-directing-original-epic-for-producer-ridley-scott/) for Ridley Scott and 20th Century. Details sparse, but Yamazaki juggling this alongside *Godzilla Minus Zero* and *Grandgear* in the next couple years.

JT Mollner's creature feature [*Skeletons*](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3958560/skeletons-john-goodman-joins-the-cast-of-strange-darling-directors-creature-feature/) assembling cast: Brie Larson, Willa Fitzgerald, Kyle Gallner reuniting with their *Strange Darling* director, plus John Goodman, Ione Skye, and others. J.J. Abrams producing for Sony. Described as a "modern take on the creature horror film."

Charli xcx [teasing a collaboration with David Cronenberg](https://bloody-disgusting.com/music/3958573/charli-xcx-teases-song-collaboration-with-horror-master-david-cronenberg/) for her upcoming album *Music, Fashion, Film*. She recently appeared in this year's *Faces of Death* remake.

Luke Sparke ([*Primitive War*](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3958471/dinosaurs-of-the-wild-west-trailer-primitive-war-director-adds-dinosaurs-to-the-western-genre/)) following up his Vietnam-dinosaur film with *Dinosaurs of the Wild West*, blending frontier mythology with kaiju. Inspired by artist Shaun Keenan's work, building toward a cinematic universe.

Louise Lasser, [best known for *Blood Rage*](https://www.dreadcentral.com/news/579600/louise-lasser-of-blood-rage-fame-passes-away-at-87/), passed away at 87. That annual Thanksgiving slasher rewatch just changed.
