Hideki Kamiya, director of the original *Resident Evil 2*, [says Capcom has made the series too scary](https://bloody-disgusting.com/video-games/3966710/original-resident-evil-2-director-hideki-kamiya-says-current-resident-evil-games-are-too-scary/), claiming the studio has become "completely numb" to its own design. The irony of the guy who made one of horror's most influential games complaining about escalation isn't lost.

Santa Barbara Games showed off [*Voidwood* at PAX West](https://bloody-disgusting.com/video-games/3966670/new-voidwood-footage-details-gameplay-mechanics-watch/), its co-op survival horror forest crawler. Footage covers scavenging and exploration mechanics; details sparse otherwise.

*Halloween: The Game* dropped into Early Access this weekend and [removed a sex mini-game](https://bloody-disgusting.com/video-games/3966696/halloween-the-game-removes-controversial-mini-game-allowing-characters-to-have-sex/) after backlash, though it still lets you smoke weed between Michael Myers slaughters. Full launch September 8.

Folk horror *Häxan* is [filming in Sweden with T'Nia Miller and Erika Linder](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3966685/psychological-folk-horror-haxan-to-star-tnia-miller-and-erika-linder/), drawing from the 17th-century Swedish witch trials and blending psychological dread with historical horror.

A [cancelled *Scream* game surfaced in recent Steam leaks](https://bloody-disgusting.com/video-games/3966667/unreleased-scream-game-discovered-as-part-of-recent-steam-leak/), developed by Fatshark before getting shelved. What could've been.

Charles Band's *[Subspecies V: Blood Rise](https://bloody-disgusting.com/editorials/3965760/subspecies-v-blood-rise-is-a-worthy-farewell-to-full-moons-greatest-vampire/)* closes out Full Moon's vampire saga as a surprisingly satisfying finale to Radu's long run.
