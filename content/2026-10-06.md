Casper Kelly's [Buddy](https://ihorror.com/buddy-digital-blu-ray-vhs-october-2026/) hits digital October 13 after a $26 million theatrical run. The film's getting a VHS release too, because of course it is.

Ryan Murphy's vampire series [Brides of Blood](https://ihorror.com/ryan-murphy-brides-of-blood-vampire-series-december/) starts shooting in December in Europe. No network or cast announced yet, but Murphy's moving fast.

[V/H/S/MIXTAPE](https://ihorror.com/vhs-mixtape-first-look-ernest-dickerson-music-of-mr-sze/) lands on Shudder October 9. Ernest Dickerson's segment, "The Music of Mr. Sze," follows a film student pursuing a reclusive musician—and the first-look images suggest it doesn't end well.

Stephen King's [The Institute](https://ihorror.com/the-institute-season-2-trailer-mgm-plus-november-8/) season two drops November 8 on MGM+. The new season leaves the book behind, with Mary-Louise Parker's Sigsby now a fugitive.

Hammer Films owner John Gore [wants to remake Baron Frankenstein](https://ihorror.com/hammer-remakes-john-gore-evil-frankenstein-countess-dracula/), noting the Peter Cushing version has never been properly remade. He's eyeing it as a potential starting point for Hammer's revival slate.

[Human](https://ihorror.com/human-trailer-matt-stuertz-dark-sky-films-october-23/) traps an actress alone on a film set for Matt Stuertz's splatter picture. Dark Sky Films releases it in select theaters and digital October 23.

A fan has [decompiled P.T.](https://bloody-disgusting.com/video-games/3970464/a-fan-has-created-a-decompilation-of-kojimas-p-t-using-the-original-game-files-video/) using the original game files, keeping Kojima's ghost story playable more than a decade after Sony pulled it from the PlayStation Store.

**Worth reading in full:** [FocusFest returns October 10](https://dailydead.com/focusfest-returns-on-october-10th-includes-nosferatu-and-obsession/) with early looks at Focus Features' upcoming horror slate, including Nosferatu and Obsession.
