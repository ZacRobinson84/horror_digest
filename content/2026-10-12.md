Carlo Rambaldi, the special effects pioneer behind the creatures in *Alien* and *E.T.*, receives a [posthumous Walk of Fame star](https://ihorror.com/carlo-rambaldi-hollywood-walk-of-fame-star/) on October 15. He died in 2012.

Netflix's *The Body* drops November 11—a [psychodrama from Quinn Shephard](https://ihorror.com/the-body-trailer-netflix-quinn-shephard/) about a missing girl that spirals into mass hysteria at a Catholic school. The influences she cites (*Carrie*, *Jennifer's Body*, *Heavenly Creatures*, *Mean Girls*) suggest something interested in scapegoating and collective delusion, not just jump scares.

[*River*](https://ihorror.com/river-trailer-jane-levy-jessica-rothe/) (January 15, 2027) pairs Jane Levy and Jessica Rothe in what looks like a solid backwoods slasher from director Joshua Giuliano. It's already been making rounds on the festival circuit.

*Other Mommy* opened at $27.5 million despite a [17% RT score and a D+ CinemaScore](https://ihorror.com/other-mommy-box-office-opening-weekend-cinemascore/)—same audience grade as *Hereditary*. Rob Savage's film is review-proof but not critic-proof.

Hammer Films has [acquired the rights](https://www.dreadcentral.com/news/593102/hammer-films-is-ready-to-resurrect-the-woman-in-black-and-more/) to Susan Hill's works through John Gore Studios, which owns the studio. *The Woman in Black* is on the table.

Mike Flanagan's *The Exorcist: Martyrs* [has fans dissecting trailer frames](https://www.dreadcentral.com/news/593068/exorcist-martyrs-regan-photo-theory/) for hints about Regan MacNeil's involvement. Details sparse.

PC-98 rarity *Dead of the Brain*—a cult horror adventure from the early aughts—[lands on Steam October 30](https://bloody-disgusting.com/video-games/3970975/pc-98-cult-classic-dead-of-the-brain-coming-to-steam-on-october-30-trailer/). Fakku acquired FairyTale's catalogue to make it happen.
