David Robert Mitchell reunites with Maika Monroe for [It Follows sequel They Follow](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3959565/they-follow-jackie-earle-haley-michael-gandolfini-and-more-join-the-it-follows-sequel/), adding Jackie Earle Haley, Michael Gandolfini, Justine Lupe, and others to the cast. No plot details yet.

Del Toro's [Frankenstein lands on Criterion this October](https://bloody-disgusting.com/home-video/3959563/guillermo-del-toros-frankenstein-with-extended-cut-the-silence-of-the-lambs-get-criterion-release-in-october/) with "The Reborn Cut"—an 8-minute extended version the director rebuilt for the release. The Silence of the Lambs joins the lineup.

Rick Baker shared [footage of an unused puppet](https://www.dreadcentral.com/news/580763/rick-baker-shares-stunning-video-of-unused-puppet-for-the-wolfman-transformation/) from The Wolfman transformation sequence. It's a good look at practical work that didn't make the final cut.

Adam Wingard's Onslaught for A24 landed an R rating for strong bloody violence, gore, sexual material, and language. The film reunites him with Dan Stevens and drops September 4.

[White Creek](https://bloody-disgusting.com/video-games/3959475/segas-dreamcast-to-live-again-with-brand-new-survival-horror-title-white-creek-trailer/), a new survival horror game from PixelHeart, is coming to Sega's Dreamcast. A developer passion project bringing new life to the defunct console.

Lincoln Center's ["Scary Movies" festival](https://rue-morgue.com/kevin-bacons-family-movie-henry-4k-premiere-and-much-more-at-lincolns-centers-scary-movies/) next month includes Kevin Bacon's Family Movie and a 4K premiere of Henry, among other new and classic work.

[Train to Busan returns to theaters](https://dailydead.com/train-to-busan-10th-anniversary-trailer-poster/) for its 10th anniversary. The zombie outbreak film is getting a theatrical run.
