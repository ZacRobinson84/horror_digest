Yeon Sang-ho's [*Paradise Lost*](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3965968/paradise-lost-director-yeon-sang-hos-new-thriller-has-been-purchased-by-well-go-usa-entertainment/) — described as a mystery-thriller — has been acquired by Well Go USA for spring 2027 theatrical release. World premiere at TIFF September 13. The director's been running hot since *Train to Busan*.

Ti West's [*Ebenezer*](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3965940/ebenezer-new-images-preview-ti-wests-christmas-ghost-story-starring-johnny-depp/) lands Johnny Depp as Scrooge and Ian McKellen as Jacob Marley in a reworking of *A Christmas Carol*. New images are out; no release date yet.

Isabelle Huppert plays Elizabeth Báthory in [*The Blood Countess*](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3965894/the-blood-countess-isabelle-huppert-is-elizabeth-bathory-in-next-years-horror-movie/), premiering at Fantastic Fest September 17. Strand Releasing handling U.S. theatrical in spring 2027.

Joko Anwar is [expanding *Satan's Slaves* into a trilogy](https://www.dreadcentral.com/images/586974/joko-anwars-satans-slaves-becomes-a-trilogy-with-satans-slaves-origin-image/) with *Satan's Slaves: Origin*, a prequel. First images confirm it's in motion.

[*Buddy*](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3965838/casper-kellys-buddy-doubles-box-office-projections-with-5-3-million-opening/), Casper Kelly's Barney-inspired indie horror, doubled its box office projections with a $5.3M opening weekend—enough to land #6 domestically against heavier competition.

Adam Wingard is [intrigued by the prospect of directing the next *A Nightmare on Elm Street*](https://www.dreadcentral.com/interviews/587006/exclusive-adam-wingard-intrigued-by-the-idea-of-directing-the-next-a-nightmare-on-elm-street-movie/). No deal yet; just enthusiasm.

Tippett Studio clarified it's [not shutting down](https://bloody-disgusting.com/the-further/3865861/tippett-studio-releases-official-statement-about-the-companys-future-not-shutting-down/), walking back weekend reports. Phil Tippett's practical effects shop is staying open.

**Worth reading in full:** [*Onslaught*](https://dailydead.com/review-onslaught-is-a-simple-yet-chilling-return-to-horror-for-adam-wingard/) — Wingard's back in genre territory and it reads like a spiritual sequel to *You're Next*.
