Ryū Murakami, author of *Audition* and *In the Miso Soup*, [died at 74](https://rue-morgue.com/horror-novelist-ryu-murakami-author-of-audition-dies-at-74/). No cause given. J-horror's literary wave loses its second major voice in 2026.

[Móbido Film Fest](https://rue-morgue.com/death-is-the-guest-of-honour-as-morbido-film-fest-unveils-its-2026-lineup/) (Oct 28–Nov 8) is leading with Ken Russell's uncut *The Devils*, Takashi Miike's *Bad Lieutenant: Tokyo*, and 11 Mexican titles. Latin America's premier festival isn't messing around.

Ice Pick Lodge's [*Pathologic 3* gets a major October update](https://bloody-disgusting.com/video-games/3973181/the-great-october-update-now-available-for-pathologic-3-trailer/) with 100+ dialogue and quest fixes plus accessibility and performance improvements. PC and console.

[*Recluse*](https://ihorror.com/recluse-vertical-north-american-rights-henry-chaisson/), a Tribeca debut from *Antlers* co-writer Henry Chaisson, lands at Vertical for theatrical in H1 2027. Details sparse but the pedigree holds.

[*Snapshot*](https://ihorror.com/snapshot-dark-star-pictures-found-footage-jack-the-ripper/), a found-footage horror set in 1888 Whitechapel with an all-women ghost-hunting society, goes to Dark Star Pictures. Joseph Archer directs.

Brooklyn Horror Fest opened with *I Love Paris*, a vampire mockumentary that apparently landed well. That's the extent of the coverage.
