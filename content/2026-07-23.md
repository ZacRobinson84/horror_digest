Pinocchio Unstrung hits theaters this Friday with [practical effects](https://bloody-disgusting.com/reviews/3960420/pinocchio-unstrung-review-practical-effects-bring-this-twisted-fairy-tale-to-life/) pulling weight—the latest entry in the Twisted Childhood Universe escalates the franchise's body horror. The Halloweenies podcast has a full episode with the effects team.

David Robert Mitchell (It Follows) brings [dinosaurs to suburbia](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3960424/the-end-of-oak-street-new-trailer-and-posters-bring-the-jurassic-park-to-suburbia/) in The End of Oak Street. New trailer's out and tickets are live.

DC's [Clayface](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3960416/clayface-official-trailer-dcs-body-horror-movie-gives-rise-to-a-new-cinematic-monster/) trailer arrived—body horror Cronenberg-style, with Tom Rhys Harries as Gotham's shape-shifting monster. Opens October 23.

NEON's [It Ends](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3960478/it-ends-official-trailer-existential-road-trip-nightmare-drives-into-theaters-this-summer/) is an existential zoomer nightmare about four college grads stuck on an endless road. Feature debut from Alexander Ullom. August 21.

Audible's casting Dracula audio drama: [Jonathan Bailey as the count and Ella Purnell as Mina](https://bloody-disgusting.com/the-further/3960564/dracula-audio-drama-for-audible-will-feature-jonathan-bailey-as-dracula-and-ella-purnell-as-mina-harker/). Out this fall.

Skybound's horror comic [Stillwater](https://bloody-disgusting.com/tv/3960506/stillwater-skybounds-horror-comic-getting-a-series-adaptation-at-prime-video/) is getting a Prime Video series with Greg Berlanti and Carly Wray (Watchmen, Westworld) attached.

Fantasia's running strong: [Home Bodies](https://bloody-disgusting.com/exclusives/3960482/home-bodies-image-poster-introduce-isolated-twins-in-sci-fi-nightmare-fantasia-2026/) (sci-fi isolation with twins) premieres July 23, and [Our Effed Up World](https://bloody-disgusting.com/reviews/3960036/our-effed-up-world-review-fantasia-2026/) is Alice Maio Mackay's alien-invasion hang-out film, which [Dread Central rated](https://www.dreadcentral.com/news/581983/our-effed-up-world-reminds-us-why-this-world-is-worth-saving-fantasia-review/) as a blend of Doctor Who and Kevin Smith.

The Skin Stapler, a [PSX-styled grindhouse slasher](https://bloody-disgusting.com/video-games/3960483/grindhouse-slasher-the-skin-stapler-stalks-august-6-release-trailer/), drops August 6 on Steam and GOG.
