Sébastien Vaniček's [Evil Dead Burn](https://bloody-disgusting.com/reviews/3958852/evil-dead-burn-review-in-laws-are-hell-in-sequel-burned-by-its-own-ambition/) lands this weekend as the franchise's darkest entry yet—ambitious callbacks and gore aplenty, though critics are split on whether it overextends itself with an allegory for domestic abuse that sits uneasily against the slapstick kills.

Tim Miller's [untitled time-loop thriller with Keanu Reeves](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3958821/keanu-reeves-describes-his-untitled-time-loop-thriller-as-groundhog-day-with-sharks/) (formerly *Shiver*) is—according to Reeves himself—*Groundhog Day* with sharks. Details sparse beyond that tease.

[Godzilla Minus Zero](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3958717/godzilla-minus-zero-watch-the-brand-new-teaser-trailer-now/) lands November 6 via GKIDS. Takashi Yamazaki returns to direct the sequel, shot for IMAX. The poster suggests something worth parsing, though Toho's keeping specifics close.

Parker Finn's [*Possession* remake](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3958790/parker-finns-possession-remake-adds-diego-calva-to-the-cast/) continues assembling: Diego Calva joins Margaret Qualley, Callum Turner, and Paul Dano. Finn writes, directs, and produces. The 1981 Żuławski original sets a steep bar.

Mike Flanagan will [premiere exclusive footage from his *Carrie* series](https://bloody-disgusting.com/tv/3958813/mike-flanagans-carrie-will-debut-exclusive-first-look-at-san-diego-comic-con/) at San Diego Comic-Con later this month on Prime Video.

Linda Cardellini [teased her approach](https://bloody-disgusting.com/tv/3958799/crystal-lake-linda-cardellini-teases-the-shows-deep-dive-into-the-mrs-voorhees-character/) to Pamela Voorhees in the *Friday the 13th* prequel *Crystal Lake*, calling it "really fun"—suggesting the show will dig into her backstory rather than just explain what we already know.

[Casper Kelly's *Buddy*](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3958736/buddy-teaser-trailer-what-if-barney-became-a-sadistic-maniac/) (director of *Too Many Cooks*) arrives August 28. A children's TV mascot—a bright orange unicorn—harbors a dark and violent nature. The teaser doesn't bother with subtlety.

Na Hong-jin's [*Hope*](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3958720/hope-new-trailer-reveals-a-rampaging-monster-tearing-through-a-village/) hits theaters September 9 via NEON. A creature tears through a village in the action-heavy trailer. Cast includes Hwang Jung-min, Hoyeon, Taylor Russell, Michael Fassbender, and Alicia Vikander—details sparse on what they're fighting or why.
