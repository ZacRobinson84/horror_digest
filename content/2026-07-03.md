Dario Argento says [his next film will be the bloodiest of his career](https://www.dreadcentral.com/news/579237/dario-argento-says-next-movie-will-be-the-bloodiest-film-of-my-career/). Details sparse, but Argento at 84 returning to full extremity is worth noting.

Ryan Coogler's [X-Files reboot wrapped its pilot](https://bloody-disgusting.com/tv/3958255/ryan-cooglers-the-x-files-reboot-has-wrapped-production-on-pilot/) with Himesh Patel and Danielle Deadwyler playing new agents. Coogler directing prestige TV about paranoia is an interesting move; the pilot's done, now comes the wait to see if it sticks around.

Hanna Bergholm (Hatching) returns with [Nightborn](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3958240/nightborn-trailer-teases-a-monstrous-newborn-in-shudders-horror-film-from-hatching-filmmaker/), a fable about maternal horror and a very wrong newborn. Premieres at Fantasia on July 22, hits Shudder July 31. The trailer looks properly visceral.

Steven Spielberg is producing a [feature adaptation of The Mandela Catalogue](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3958249/steven-spielberg-to-produce-the-mandela-catalogue-movie/), the viral YouTube analog horror series. Scott Stuber and Amazon MGM involved. Hollywood's continuing its hunt for the next Backrooms; whether YouTube horror translates to feature length remains an open question.

Zach Lipovsky and Adam Stein (Final Destination Bloodlines) are back with [Freaks Part II](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3958220/freaks-part-ii-first-look-teases-gory-sequel-from-final-destination-bloodlines-directors/), their sequel to the 2018 mutant thriller. Closing out Fantasia this year.

Fantasia's final programming wave dropped, headlined by [Junction Row](https://bloody-disgusting.com/exclusives/3958129/junction-row-trailer-fantasia-2026/) with Katharine Isabelle and Lovecraftian creatures, and [Her Private Hell](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3958141/her-private-hell-fantasia-2026/) as the opening film. Festival runs July 16–August 2 in Montreal.

Capcom moved [Onimusha: Way of the Sword](https://bloody-disgusting.com/video-games/3958195/capcom-bumps-onimusha-way-of-the-sword-up-to-september-4-release/) up from September 25 to September 4 across PS5, Xbox Series, Switch 2, and PC.
