Na Hong-jin (The Wailing) is making his creature feature debut with [Hope](https://bloody-disgusting.com/exclusives/3966143/gory-new-hope-images-tease-brutal-creature-feature-from-the-wailing-director-ahead-of-release/), hitting theaters September 9. Neon's been teasing it with gory stills.

Dave Boyle (Never After Dark) is directing a [Pulse remake](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3966197/kiyoshi-kurosawas-pulse-to-get-remake-from-never-after-dark-director/) for Screen Gems and Spyglass, with Parker Finn producing. Kurosawa's original is still the standard for online horror.

Victor Miller, who [wrote Friday the 13th](https://www.dreadcentral.com/news/587319/breaking-friday-the-13th-writer-victor-miller-has-died-at-86/), died Monday at 86. He created the template that most slashers have been copying for half a century.

Adam Wingard's [Onslaught](https://bloody-disgusting.com/reviews/3966126/onslaught-review-adam-wingards-gory-action-throwback-peaks-too-soon/) is a grindhouse-style action film set in The Guest universe, trading kaiju for B-movie gorehound aesthetics. Early word is it front-loads the best bits.

Anthony Cousins (Frogman) is expanding that cult hit's world with [The Kentucky Goblins](https://bloody-disgusting.com/exclusives/3966158/the-kentucky-goblins-frogman-director-expands-the-films-universe-with-new-found-footage-movie-exclusive/), a found footage sequel set in the same folklore-horror universe.

Ryuhei Kitamura (Godzilla: Final Wars) delivered [Labyrinth](https://www.dreadcentral.com/reviews/587040/labyrinth-godzilla-final-wars-director-ryuhei-kitamura-delivers-a-mildly-satisfying-saw-replica-frightfest-review/) at FrightFest—a Saw replica that lands better than most, though it breaks no new ground.

David Dastmalchian stars in the paranoid thriller [Sender](https://rue-morgue.com/exclusive-photos-david-dastmalchian-in-horror-film-sender-heading-for-sitges/), which already took awards at SXSW and is heading to Sitges.
