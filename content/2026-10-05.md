[V/H/S/MIXTAPE](https://dailydead.com/beyond-fest-2026-review-v-h-s-mixtape-keeps-the-franchise-rocking) premières on Shudder October 9. The anthology leans hard into audio horror—segments built around 80s metal videos, apocalypse compositions, songs made by the dead—with directors including members of Wu-Tang Clan. It's less polished than previous entries and more chaotic, which seems to be the point.

[Ernest Dickerson's segment](https://bloody-disgusting.com/exclusives/3970258/ernest-dickersons-v-h-s-mixtape-segment-introduces-tormented-musician-in-eerie-first-look-images-exclusive/) for the anthology, "The Music of Mr. Sze," follows a documentary filmmaker uncovering a reclusive, tormented musician. Details sparse but Dickerson (Bones, Demon Knight) directing keeps it interesting.

Stephen Graham Jones has a sequel to *The Only Good Indians* coming: [*Off The Reservation*](https://bloody-disgusting.com/exclusives/3970444/read-an-exclusive-excerpt-from-off-the-reservation-stephen-graham-jones-sequel-to-the-only-good-indians/). Bloody Disgusting is running an exclusive excerpt.

[*Buddy*](https://ihorror.com/buddy-digital-blu-ray-vhs-october-2026/) hits digital October 13, DVD/Blu-ray December 8. Casper Kelly's film about a possessed children's-TV unicorn made $26 million theatrically. A VHS edition drops in November.

[*Victorian Psycho*](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3970421/victorian-psycho-maika-monroe-is-all-the-rage-on-new-official-poster-for-november-horror-movie/) (Maika Monroe as an eccentric governess) arrives November 13. New poster this week.

[James Wan messaged Annabelle Wallis](https://ihorror.com/james-wan-annabelle-wallis-malignant-watch-this-space/) ("Watch this space") with something brewing—possibly a *Malignant* follow-up or new project. No details yet.

[Lawrie Brewster's *Black Chariot*](https://ihorror.com/black-chariot-trailer-lawrie-brewster-hex-studios/), a black-and-white supernatural noir with Laurence R. Harvey, opens in UK cinemas October 30.
