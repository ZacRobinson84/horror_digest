Eduardo Sanchez and Gregg Hale, the creators of *The Blair Witch Project*, are returning to found footage with [*The Influencer Project*](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3963078/the-influencer-project-found-footage-movie-from-blair-witch-project-creators-releasing-in-october-poster/), hitting theaters October 2. Details sparse, but the premise involves social media.

Parker Finn (*Smile*, *Smile 2*) is remaking Żuławski's *Possession*—the 1981 [Cold War psychological nightmare](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3963054/paramount-sets-summer-2027-release-for-parker-finns-possession-remake/), not the demonic possession angle. Paramount has it scheduled for June 11, 2027. Controversial move, but Finn's earned some credibility.

Alex Phillips, who made *Anything That Moves* a rust-belt giallo, is directing [*Rough on Rats*](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3963030/psychotronic-noir-horror-rough-on-rats-inspired-by-chicagos-infamous-black-widow-killer-announces-cast/), a psychotronic noir inspired by Tillie Klimek, Chicago's Black Widow serial killer. Cast announced but details thin.

Bahman Ghobadi's ecological horror [*Trees Are Watching*](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3963021/psychological-horror-trees-are-watching-starring-eva-green-heads-to-tiff-next-month/) (Eva Green, witchy vibes) premieres at TIFF on September 18. Kurdish auteur tackling woodland dread as allegory.

Netflix's fourth-season *Monster* anthology focuses on [Lizzie Borden](https://bloody-disgusting.com/tv/3963025/monster-the-lizzie-borden-story-teaser-brings-the-infamous-axe-murderer-to-netflix-this-september/) this time, hitting September 17. Ryan Murphy and Ian Brennan continue mining true crime as genre fodder.

[*Crystal Lake*](https://bloody-disgusting.com/tv/3962990/crystal-lake-showrunner-promises-pamela-voorhees-goes-full-friday-the-13th-in-the-prequel-series/), the *Friday the 13th* prequel showing young Pamela and Jason before Camp Crystal Lake, arrives on Peacock this Halloween. Showrunner promises Pamela "goes full Friday the 13th."

A [*Jeff the Killer*](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3963010/jeff-the-killer-horror-movie-in-the-works-based-on-viral-creepypasta/) film is in development from Tongal and director Savanah Moss, following *The Backrooms*' box office wake. Internet creepypasta adaptation cycle continues.
