John Carpenter's [graphic novel debut](https://bloody-disgusting.com/comics/3962432/cathedral-john-carpenters-debut-graphic-novel-now-available/) *Cathedral* is out now via Storm King Comics, with an accompanying soundtrack from Carpenter, Cody Carpenter, and Daniel Davies dropping Friday.

Banjong Pisanthanakun (*The Medium*, *Shutter*) is bringing [*Inherit*](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3962387/inherit-trailer-teases-chilling-demonic-possession-horror-from-the-medium-filmmaker/) to TIFF next month—a demonic possession film with Davika Hoorne as a grandmother under the entity's control.

Shudder acquired [*Bloody Tennis*](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3962396/shudder-acquires-extreme-sports-nightmare-bloody-tennis/), a Greek-German body horror film from Nikias Chryssos marking his English-language debut. Set at an elite tennis academy, details sparse.

[*Clown in a Cornfield 2: Frendo Lives*](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3962361/clown-in-a-cornfield-2-frendo-lives-slasher-movie-sequel-officially-in-development/) is officially in development. The first film pulled $13 million at the worldwide box office last year.

Frictional Games is [remastering *Amnesia: The Dark Descent*](https://bloody-disgusting.com/video-games/3962309/frictional-games-developing-a-remaster-of-amnesia-the-dark-descent-for-later-this-year/) for later this year, per their 2025 annual report.

John Erick Dowdle (*As Above, So Below*) is directing [*Doctor Caligari's Cabinet of Wonders*](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3962406/doctor-caligaris-cabinet-of-wonders-talia-ryder-starring-alongside-michael-shannon/), a *Cabinet of Dr. Caligari* reimagining with Michael Shannon and Talia Ryder.

[*Victorian Psycho*](https://bloody-disgusting.com/exclusives/3962329/victorian-psycho-carves-up-new-november-release-date/) has shifted from September 25 to November 13—a smarter move out of the fall release glut.
