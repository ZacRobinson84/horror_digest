Ryan Murphy's *The Shards*, based on Bret Easton Ellis's prep school thriller, drops on FX and Hulu August 5. The [third trailer](https://bloody-disgusting.com/tv/3961674/the-shards-third-trailer-promises-a-summer-of-backstabbing-and-serial-killing-in-1981/) leans into 1981 paranoia—serial killer, backstabbing, the usual Ellis fare.

Sam Raimi is producing Stephen King's *Desperation*, with *Final Destination Bloodlines* directors Adam B. Stein and Lipovsky set to [helm the adaptation](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3961625/desperation-sam-raimi-and-final-destination-bloodlines-directors-teaming-for-stephen-king-movie/). The 1996 novel is finally getting another crack at cinema.

Hanna Bergholm's *Nightborn*—her follow-up to *Hatching*—is a [study in monstrous motherhood](https://rue-morgue.com/fantasia-26-movie-review-nightborn-proves-mother-knows-best-its-just-natural/) that debuted at Fantasia. Ambitious, unsettling, and out now on Shudder.

A24's *Crystal Lake*, the Friday the 13th prequel, lands on Peacock in October stateside, but [Warner Bros. picked it up for HBO Max](https://bloody-disgusting.com/tv/3961656/crystal-lake-finds-a-home-on-hbo-max-in-europe-the-middle-east-and-africa/) everywhere else—Europe, Middle East, Africa.

Mahershala Ali [has moved on](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3961681/mahershala-ali-confirms-hes-moved-on-from-marvels-long-delayed-blade-reboot/) from Marvel's *Blade* reboot after seven years in development hell. The project appears officially dead.

Eli Roth's *Ice Cream Man* arrives August 7 from his own label, The Horror Section. No press-release fluff here—just Roth making another movie.
