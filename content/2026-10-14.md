Ryan Coogler's [X-Files reboot is officially greenlit at Hulu](https://www.fangoria.com/the-x-files-series-ryan-coogler/), starring Himesh Patel and Danielle Deadwyler. The pilot had already wrapped; this is the series order.

Ethan Hawke and Scott Derrickson are [reteaming for Midnight Radio](https://www.fangoria.com/ethan-hawke-midnight-radio/), a nine-episode horror fiction podcast. Hawke plays the host of a late-night AM conspiracy show who receives an unsettling call one night. Details sparse on what unfolds.

Jacob Chase (Insidious: Out of the Further) is directing [Sleepover for Blumhouse Atomic Monster and Universal](https://www.fangoria.com/sleepover-parker-posey/), with Parker Posey attached. October 8, 2027. Described as a "quintessential sleepover nightmare."

Mike Flanagan's [Carrie finale is bringing prom night chaos](https://www.dreadcentral.com/images/593499/carrie-finale-images-prom-night/). Prime Video has released images; the miniseries wraps soon.

[Hammer Films has acquired Susan Hill's complete literary catalogue](https://rue-morgue.com/hammer-acquires-rights-to-the-woman-in-black-author-susan-hills-complete-literary-works/), meaning more ghost stories beyond The Woman in Black are now in development.

Creepypasta adaptations are coming. [The Third Parent](https://www.dreadcentral.com/trailer/593515/rob-lowe-meets-a-malevolent-entity-in-teaser-for-the-third-parent/) teaser dropped with Rob Lowe facing off against "Tommy Taffy." [Kane Parsons' Backrooms](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3971821/the-third-parent-teaser-creepypasta-movie/) showed there's an audience for this, though neither Channel Zero nor Slender Man left much of a mark.

New Zealand body-horror comedy [Mum, I'm Alien Pregnant is hitting digital October 24](https://www.fangoria.com/mum-im-alien-pregnant-trailer/). Pregnancy horror with practical gore and the kind of irreverent tone the country does well.

Z Nation is [returning as Z Nation: Resurrection](https://bloody-disgusting.com/tv/3971601/z-nation-resurrection-the-zombie-series-is-coming-back-to-life-with-brand-new-sequel-series/), a sequel series to The Asylum's original run. Black Summer prequel already expanded the universe; this continues it.
