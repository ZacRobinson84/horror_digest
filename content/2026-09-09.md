Julius Avery (Overlord, The Pope's Exorcist) is [directing Crush](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3966929/crush-sarah-catherine-hook-starring-in-horror-movie-about-a-woman-being-crushed-by-a-snake/), a survival thriller for 20th Century Studios about a woman in the Everglades waking to a python. Sarah Catherine Hook (The White Lotus) leads.

Spencer Lackey, a YouTube horror creator, will [write and direct Lust](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3966936/viral-horror-creator-spencer-lackey-to-direct-lust-for-blumhouse-atomic-monster-and-neon/) for Neon, Blumhouse Atomic Monster, and Spooky Pictures. Another swing at finding the next indie-to-studio pipeline success.

M. Night Shyamalan teamed with Nicholas Sparks on [Remain](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3966863/remain-teaser-trailer-director-m-night-shyamalans-supernatural-romance-stars-jake-gyllenhaal/), a supernatural romance with Jake Gyllenhaal hitting theaters February 5, 2027. Details sparse beyond the teaser.

TIFF 2026 kicks off this week with [five horror premieres worth noting](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3966912/five-horror-premieres-we-cant-wait-to-see-at-tiff-2026/), including Ken Russell's The Devils and limited-run series spotlights on Below, Carrie, and Crystal Lake.

Na Hong-jin's [Hope](https://bloody-disgusting.com/reviews/3966895/hope-review-na-hong-jins-ambitious-alien-saga-starts-with-a-propulsive-bang/) runs 160 minutes of propulsive alien action with minimal breathing room. The Wailing director's latest doesn't pause to catch its breath.

Dave Boyle's [Never After Dark](https://bloody-disgusting.com/news/3966326/never-after-dark-bloody-sneaks/) lands September 25. Stephen King calls it "amazing and haunting"; Scott Derrickson says "intelligent, unpredictable, and bone chilling." Free sneak screenings September 16 via Bloody Disgusting and Magnolia.

Cassandra "Elvira" Peterson plays a witch in [R.L. Stine's Pumpkinhead 2](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3966791/r-l-stines-pumpkinhead-2-first-images-of-cassandra-elvira-peterson-in-tubis-halloween-sequel/), premiering on Tubi's Terror on Tubi event this October.
