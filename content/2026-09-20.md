Zach Cregger's [*Resident Evil* opened to $26.3 million Friday](https://ihorror.com/resident-evil-box-office-opening-day-record-zach-cregger/), the franchise's biggest opening day, with a B+ CinemaScore. A24 attached a [teaser for Jeremy Saulnier's *Slayground*](https://ihorror.com/a24-slayground-teaser-jeremy-saulnier-resident-evil/) to screenings—security camera footage from a haunted house, theater-only, no release date announced.

Shudder picked up [*The Glorious Dead* from The Adams Family](https://www.fangoria.com/shudder-acquires-the-adams-familys-the-glorious-dead-exclusive/) out of Fantastic Fest, their fourth collaboration with the indie outfit. Ilja Rautsi's Finnish [*Red Snow*—a vampire-Santa comedy](https://ihorror.com/red-snow-shudder-december-15-ilja-rautsi-vampire-santa/)—premieres at Fantastic Fest tomorrow and hits Shudder December 15.

Samjad's [*Half*](https://bloody-disgusting.com/reviews/3968342/half-review-vampire-twins-paint-the-desert-red-in-gory-revenge-actioner-tiff-2026/) plays TIFF as a vampire revenge actioner with Very Tri Yulisman (*The Raid 2*, *The Night Comes for Us*) handling martial arts choreography. Cameron Dodd's [*The Cat and the Hatchet*](https://bloody-disgusting.com/the-further/3968369/the-cat-and-the-hatchet-trailer-twisted-horror-take-on-the-cat-in-the-hat-is-coming-soon/) is the latest public-domain horror short, reimagining *The Cat in the Hat* as a slasher.

Michael Dougherty's [*Trick 'r Treat: Witching Hours*](https://rue-morgue.com/sam-returns-in-trick-r-treat-witching-hours/) graphic novel anthology gets wide release this October. Concrete Burial, a [first-person Brutalist horror crawler inspired by Control and Philip Jackson sculptures](https://bloody-disgusting.com/video-games/3968398/first-person-horror-adventure-concrete-burial-out-now-on-steam-trailer/), is live on Steam with a 20% launch discount.
