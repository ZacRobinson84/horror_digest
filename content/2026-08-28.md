Nick Cutter's *The Troop* is getting adapted at Paramount Primal with Chris Basler writing. The 2014 novel about a troop of boys enduring something very wrong on an island has been in development hell for years; [this time it might stick](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3965721/the-troop-nick-cutters-horror-novel-getting-film-adaptation-from-paramount-primal/).

Drew Hancock (*Companion*) is directing *Seasons* for Amazon MGM Studios with Elizabeth McGovern, Jack Reynor, Lily James, and Ron Perlman. Details are [sparse](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3965643/elizabeth-mcgovern-joins-horror-movie-seasons-from-companion-director/), but Hancock's debut suggests he knows how to build dread.

*Effigy*, directed by Myles McEwen and Ripley Stevens, wrapped in Australia. Set in 1989 England, it follows a family's violent haunting triggered by unearthing a mysterious effigy doll. [First images](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3965699/effigy-first-look-images-australian-supernatural-horror-movie-is-set-in-1989/) look properly unsettling.

*Holstin*, the long-gestating psychological survival-horror from Team17 and Sonka, finally has a 2027 window for PC and consoles—including Game Pass day one. [Four years](https://bloody-disgusting.com/video-games/3965655/holstin-targets-2027-release-for-pc-and-consoles-trailer/) since the initial reveal.

Indie studio 906 Games showed *Cutout* at Gamescom: a side-scrolling action-horror set in a hand-crafted paper-and-cardboard grimoire. [Trailer](https://bloody-disgusting.com/video-games/3965661/papercraft-action-horror-title-cutout-unveiled-at-gamescom-trailer/) available for anyone who digs tactile aesthetics applied to the grotesque.

Marvel's *Midnight Universe* launches in October with horror reimaginings of Spider-Man, the Fantastic Four, and the X-Men. [Official reveal](https://bloody-disgusting.com/comics/3965682/marvel-comics-opens-the-terrifying-midnight-universe-with-an-official-reveal-trailer/) dropped this week.

David Kittredge [spent years writing about John Boorman's *Exorcist II*](https://ihorror.com/david-kittredge-boorman-and-the-devil-interview/), the film that's been a punchline since 1977, and has a thoughtful defense of risk-taking in cinema that's worth the read.
