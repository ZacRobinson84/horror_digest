Jim Krut, who played the iconic "Helicopter Zombie" in George A. Romero's *Dawn of the Dead*, [has passed away](https://bloody-disgusting.com/news/3961978/memorable-dawn-of-the-dead-zombie-actor-jim-krut-has-passed-away/). Tom Savini announced the death on Instagram.

Justin Hardy, filmmaker and son of *The Wicker Man* director Robin Hardy, [died at 61](https://rue-morgue.com/filmmaker-justin-hardy-son-of-robin-hardy-director-of-the-wicker-man-dead-at-61/). His death comes weeks before the premiere of his much-anticipated sequel to his father's 1973 folk horror classic.

Takashi Miike's [*Bad Lieutenant: Tokyo*](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3961870/bad-lieutenant-tokyo-first-look-takashi-miikes-film-stars-shun-oguri-lily-james-liv-morgan/) teaser is out. A standalone sequel to Abel Ferrara's 1992 *Bad Lieutenant*, it stars Shun Oguri, Lily James, and Liv Morgan.

Adam Egypt Mortimer's supernatural horror [*Pathetic Fallacy*](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3961911/kid-cudi-and-jessica-rothe-to-star-in-psychedelic-horror-pathetic-fallacy-from-adam-egypt-mortimer/) has wrapped. Kid Cudi, Jessica Rothe, Travis Fimmel, and Natasha Lyonne star in what's described as a psychedelic tale of love and death.

Fun City Editions is giving the rare 1982 Euroshocker *Der Fan* (aka *Trance*) [its first U.S. theatrical release](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3961925/cult-horror-der-fan-gets-new-trailer-ahead-of-first-ever-u-s-theatrical-release/) in a 4K restoration. It premieres at Metrograph in NYC on August 28.

Shudder's cult series *The Creep Tapes* [returns for season three on September 15](https://bloody-disgusting.com/tv/3961906/peachfuzz-is-back-the-creep-tapes-sets-season-three-premiere-next-month/). Mark Duplass and Patrick Brice wrote and executive produce.

V/H/S gets musical with [*V/H/S/MIXTAPE*](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3961847/v-h-s-mixtape-announced-for-october-2026-premiere-on-shudder/), premiering October 9 on Shudder. Directors include Ernest Dickerson, Flying Lotus, Ghost's Tobias Forge, and others exploring music and sound as sources of terror.
