Rue Morgue [traces the lineage](https://rue-morgue.com/deadly-and-digital-how-early-internet-horror-paved-the-way-for-youtubes-hollywood-takeover/) of early internet horror—creepypastas, ARGs, found-footage precursors—through to recent YouTube acquisitions like *Backrooms* and *Obsession*. The argument: that era rewired how horror reaches audiences and what audiences expect from it.

Ontario stoner/doom band This Love Is Drone released a [track and video](https://rue-morgue.com/ontarios-this-love-is-drone-pays-homage-to-a-jack-hill-classic-with-im-a-spider-baby/) riffing on Jack Hill's *Spider Baby*. Details sparse, but the Sudbury outfit clearly knows their cult deep cuts.
