Mike Flanagan's *The Exorcist: Martyrs* [officially titled and dated](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3970777/the-exorcist-martyrs-mike-flanagans-exorcist-movie-gets-official-title-plot-details-and-first-look-images/) for March 12, 2027. Scarlett Johansson plays an NYPD detective hunting an executed killer (John Leguizamo) connected to demonic possession. The teaser suggests Flanagan's leaning into procedural dread rather than pure exorcism spectacle.

Joshua Giuliano's *River*, [acquired by Universal and Blumhouse](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3970823/slasher-movie-river-gets-r-rating-for-strong-bloody-violence-and-grisly-images/), got an R rating for "strong bloody violence" and "grisly images." Festival circuit darling hitting theaters January 15, 2027. Jane Levy and Jessica Rothe lead.

*Silver Pines*, a 2D side-scrolling survival horror from Wych Elm Games, [trades fixed cameras for lateral scrolling](https://bloody-disgusting.com/video-games/3970814/silver-pines-review-a-stunning-dream-like-2d-survival-horror-game-inspired-by-twin-peaks/) while staying true to the genre's DNA. *Twin Peaks* vibes, painterly presentation.

*New Group*, from director Yûta Shimotsu (*Best Wishes to All*), hits [Shudder November 6](https://rue-morgue.com/shudder-sets-a-date-drops-the-trailer-for-new-group-j-horror-weirdness-from-best-wishes-to-all-director/). Conformity-as-body-horror premise. A high school student watches her world collapse into a cult-like hive mentality.

A fan [decompiled Hideo Kojima's P.T.](https://rue-morgue.com/fan-decompiles-hideo-kojimas-p-t-into-a-native-modernized-rebuild/) into native, modernized code—the vanished *Silent Hills* teaser rebuilt from scratch as an open-source rebuild.

Ryan Murphy's next series, [*Brides of Blood*](https://rue-morgue.com/ryan-murphy-reveals-new-vampire-series-titled-brides-of-blood/), focuses on Dracula's brides. Cameras roll in Europe this December.

Severin announced [*Morbid Pleasures: A Compendium of Grand Guignol & the Grand Guignolesque*](https://rue-morgue.com/severin-announces-a-huge-grand-guignol-the-grand-guignol-esque-12-disc-set-packed-with-morbid-delights/), a 12-disc deep dive into Paris' Grand Guignol theater and its influence. Billed as the company's most monumental collection to date.
