Yann Demange directing a [zombie film from the Train to Busan director](https://rue-morgue.com/fantasia-26-movie-review-train-to-busan-director-delivers-more-zombie-excitement-in-colony/) — *Colony* premiered at Fantasia with energetic staging and committed zombie work.

Nick Cutter's *The Troop* is [getting adapted at Paramount Primal](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3965721/the-troop-nick-cutters-horror-novel-getting-film-adaptation-from-paramount-primal/) with Chris Basler writing. The 2014 novel's been in development limbo for years; details on the story remain thin.

Australian supernatural film *Effigy*, [wrapped and set in 1989 England](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3965699/effigy-first-look-images-australian-supernatural-horror-movie-is-set-in-1989/), follows a family enduring a haunting triggered by unearthing a mysterious doll. Directed by Myles McEwen and Ripley Stevens.

David Kittredge spent years inside *Exorcist II: The Heretic*. [His documentary defends Boorman's 1977 punchline](https://ihorror.com/david-kittredge-boorman-and-the-devil-interview/) with the argument that risk-taking produces both failures and *Excalibur*.

*Ever After*, a fairy tale horror from Laszlo Illes, [drops September 4th](https://dailydead.com/watch-an-exclusive-flamethrower-clip-from-ever-after/) with an exclusive flamethrower clip already circulating.

Jane Schoenbrun's *Teenage Sex and Death at Camp Miasma* is in theaters now. [Actor Jack Haven talked about playing Little Death](https://dailydead.com/playing-little-death-an-interview-with-teenage-sex-and-death-at-camp-miasmas-jack-haven/) and the film's riff on slasher tropes.
