Zach Cregger's next is [sci-fi thriller *The Flood*](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3969717/jennifer-lawrence-to-star-in-zach-creggers-sci-fi-movie-the-flood/) with Jennifer Lawrence attached. Amblin and New Line, August 2028. Plot details sparse but the pairing's worth watching.

Carlota Pereda's English-language debut, [*Found Alive*](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3969651/psychological-thriller-found-alive-from-piggy-director-unveils-first-look-ahead-of-sitges-debut/), opens Sitges this year with Chloë Grace Moretz as a former captive. Pereda made *Piggy* work, so there's reason to care.

[*Ghost in the Cell*](https://bloody-disgusting.com/reviews/3969579/ghost-in-the-cell-review-joko-anwar-delivers-gory-fun-prison-horror-in-his-best-yet/), Joko Anwar's prison horror, plays brutal and fun. Out October 2 in the US—reviewer called it his best yet.

Curry Barker's follow-up to *Obsession*, [*Anything But Ghosts*](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3969684/curry-barkers-anything-but-ghosts-sets-2027-release-date/), leans horror-comedy and lands May 7, 2027 from Focus Features. Barker co-wrote with Cooper Tomlinson and the two star.

[*Five Nights at Freddy's 3*](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3969676/five-nights-at-freddys-3-matthew-lillard-filming-start/) is moving forward with Matthew Lillard attached. Early script's done, shooting starts 2027. He called it "next-level."

Tommy Wiseau turns up in [*Silt*](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3969667/tommy-wiseau-disturbs-in-lakeside-cosmic-horror-movie-silt-trailer/), a lakeside cosmic horror from Vernon Davis. Trailer's out; limited theatrical run this fall.

Mike Flanagan's [*Carrie*](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3969664/the-exorcist-reboot-mike-flanagan-future-update/) for Prime (one season ordered so far) has Flanagan hoping it'll "invigorate" the fanbase. The original script's his starting point.
