Hangtime International Pictures and Severin Films are [remaking Lucio Fulci's *The Beyond*](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3971014/lucio-fulci-horror-classic-the-beyond-getting-a-fresh-remake-from-severin-films/), the 1981 Italian splatter classic, as the first in a slate of planned remakes. Details sparse beyond the announcement.

[*Clive Barker's Hellraiser: Revival*](https://bloody-disgusting.com/video-games/3970960/clive-barkers-hellraiser-revival-has-such-sights-to-show-you-today-on-pc-consoles-trailer/) is out today on PC, PlayStation 5, Xbox Series, and Switch 2. Doug Bradley returns as Pinhead; the studio rebuilt his voice from memory since no one documented how it was supposed to sound in the original films.

Mike Flanagan's [*Carrie* adaptation drops all eight episodes on Prime Video](https://ihorror.com/carrie-prime-video-all-eight-episodes-mike-flanagan/), with the prom sequence reportedly the most complicated thing Flanagan has ever shot. [*The Exorcist: Martyrs*](https://rue-morgue.com/universal-reveals-the-exorcist-martyrs-and-unveils-eerie-first-look/), his next project, arrives March 2027 with Scarlett Johansson as a haunted NYPD detective.

László Nemes, the Oscar-winning director of *Son of Saul*, is developing [*Sickert*](https://ihorror.com/sickert-laszlo-nemes-jack-the-ripper-series/), a limited series centering painter Walter Sickert as a suspect in the 1888 Whitechapel murders.

Del Toro's [*Pan's Labyrinth* 3D conversion](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3970980/over-1000-human-artists-turned-pans-labyrinth-into-a-brand-new-3d-theatrical-experience/) hits theaters with over 1,000 artists handling the frame-by-frame work—no AI involved, as the director has made emphatic.

Gustaf Skarsgård makes his directorial feature debut with [*The Cannibal*](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3970866/the-cannibal-horror-movie-ingmar-bergman/), a psychological horror exploring Ingmar Bergman's demons through a screenwriter's descent. Peter Birro wrote it.

Ron Perlman and Theo Rossi's Montana-set horror film [*Renew*](https://ihorror.com/renew-wraps-ron-perlman-theo-rossi-tommy-denucci/) has wrapped filming under Tommy DeNucci's direction.
