1408 sequel incoming with [Cusack, Jackson, and Hafstrom returning](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3975061/1408-stephen-king-sequel-elijah-wood-john-cusack/), plus Natalia Dyer and Elijah Wood joining. Hafstrom's still in the director's chair for another trip to the haunted room.

Blumhouse Atomic and James Tynion IV's Tiny Onion signed a first look deal. First up: [a film and animation adaptation of *Exquisite Corpses*](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3975057/blumhouse-atomic-developing-serial-killer-battle-royale-comic-exquisite-corpses-for-film-and-animation/), the serial killer battle royale comic. No plot details yet.

Paul Giamatti and Simon Pegg in [*Boutique*](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3975049/paul-giamatti-horror-movie-boutique-simon-pegg/), a murderous seaside town thing. Pegg's an interesting pull for straight horror.

Rosamund Pike producing and starring in [*Feed*](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3974952/feed-rosamund-pike-starring-in-body-horror-movie-inspired-by-hatching/), body horror from Leslye Headland (*Russian Doll*). Inspired by *Hatching*—a mother, a tween gymnast, and a collagen supplement gone wrong.

[*Paranormal Activity* shifted to October 2027](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3975040/paranormal-activity-movie-release-date-delay-october-2027/), landing back on the Halloween calendar. Six-month delay from the May slot.

[Ben Leonberg adapting the horror novella *Look Out*](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3975015/good-boy-filmmaker-to-adapt-horror-novella-look-out/) after *Good Boy*. From the Vertigo/AWA "Future of Fear" partnership.

[David Lynch's *Hyena* recasted its two leads](https://rue-morgue.com/david-lynchs-hyena-recasts-its-two-leads/) at AFM. One of Lynch's final producing credits; details on replacements sparse.

[Letitia Wright in the evil-child supernatural pic *In Her Shadow*](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3974958/letitia-wright-starring-in-evil-child-horror-movie-in-her-shadow/), directed by Rowan Athale (*Gangs of London*). From Tea Shop Productions, the *Obsession* outfit.
