[Screaming Mad George](https://www.dreadcentral.com/news/585293/society-and-nightmare-on-elm-street-effects-legend-screaming-mad-george-has-died-at-69/), the practical effects visionary behind *Society* and *A Nightmare on Elm Street*, died at 69. His work defined a generation of practical body horror.

[Hayden Panettiere](https://bloody-disgusting.com/news/3963821/scream-franchise-star-hayden-panettiere-has-passed-away-at-36-years-old/), who played Kirby Reed in *Scream 4*, has died at 36.

[Jane Schoenbrun](https://www.dreadcentral.com/news/585273/camp-miasma-director-jane-schoenbrun-wants-to-take-a-stab-at-a-nightmare-on-elm-street/) (*Camp Miasma*) is in the running to direct the next *A Nightmare on Elm Street* reboot. Details sparse, but Schoenbrun's sensibility could make this interesting.

[*The Blood of Dawnwalker*](https://bloody-disgusting.com/video-games/3963773/new-trailer-for-the-blood-of-dawnwalker-covers-game-mechanics-story-and-more-watch/) drops in less than a month on console and PC. New trailer covers mechanics and story.

Netflix is [shutting down Night School Studio](https://bloody-disgusting.com/video-games/3963766/unhinged-developer-night-school-to-be-shut-down-by-netflix/), the developer behind *Unhinged*, just six weeks after the game's launch. Also closing Helsinki's Moonloot.
