Colin Minihan (Grave Encounters) is directing [Stung](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3967259/stung-odeya-rush-and-more-join-justin-long-in-killer-bee-horror-movie/), a killer bee survival film wrapped and starring Justin Long, Iris Apatow, and Odeya Rush. Set in suburban paranoia, it follows four people trapped by a swarm.

Drew Hancock's [Seasons](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3967251/seasons-companion-directors-new-reddit-horror-movie-gets-2027-release-date/) lands November 12, 2027—his follow-up to Companion with Jack Reynor and Lily James facing ancient spirits. Amazon MGM Studios backing.

[River](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3967240/river-new-images-of-jessica-rothe-and-jane-levy-in-brutal-slasher-movie/) hits Toronto this week: Joshua Giuliano's directorial debut with Jane Levy and Jessica Rothe as estranged siblings returning home after their father's death. Shudder and IFC are handling it.

Hammer Films' [Ithaqua](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3967180/ithaqua-trailer-hammer-films-unleashes-its-first-new-monster-in-over-60-years/) trailer arrived—fourteen years in development, it's the studio's first original monster in over six decades under new ownership.

Jonathan King's [Black Sheeps](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3967219/black-sheeps-first-look-sequel-to-horror-comedy-black-sheep-will-world-premiere-this-october/) is premiering at Sitges this October, twenty years after the wacky 2006 zombie comedy original.

Rocko Zevenbergen's [Flapjax](https://bloody-disgusting.com/exclusives/3967099/flapjax-exclusive-teaser-serves-up-body-horror-and-campy-nickelodeon-style-nostalgia/)—body horror fast-food sci-fi comedy with Yuzna energy—debuts at Fantastic Fest September 17–24.

[Whalefall](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3967228/whalefall-trailer-what-if-an-entire-horror-movie-took-place-inside-a-sperm-whale/) traps Austin Abrams alive inside a sperm whale for its entire runtime. The premise alone is enough.
