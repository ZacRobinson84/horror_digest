Katsuhiro Satô, director of *Noroi*, has [another found footage film in the can](https://bloody-disgusting.com/reviews/3961750/kinki-review-the-director-of-noroi-returns-with-another-found-footage-triumph-nyaff-2026/) with *Kinki*, which played NYAFF 2026. If you've exhausted the obvious found footage canon and want something that actually lands, this is the move.

Hanna Bergholm won the Cheval Noir at Fantasia for *Nightborn*, her latest venture into [maternal horror territory](https://rue-morgue.com/exclusive-interview-mommy-fearest-hanna-bergholm-on-the-maternal-mayhem-of-nightborn/). Fresh interview with Bergholm on what makes the film tick.

Steven Fierberg's *The Spirit Lock* premiered its [exclusive trailer](https://rue-morgue.com/exclusive-trailer-premiere-trapped-by-the-spirit-lock/) at Rue Morgue—couple fighting over a possessed child with help from an ancient pagan device. Premise reads clean; execution TBD.

Blumhouse and Atomic Monster are spinning off *M3GAN* into *Soulm8te*, an R-rated AI thriller. [Sullivan and Rysdahl discuss](https://www.fangoria.com/soulm8te-lily-sullivan-david-rysdahl/) algorithms, erotica, and intimacy in the film.

Aaron Bennett's *Jokers Wild* fan-film universe continues with *Jokers Wild: Lazarus Risen*, shifting focus to Harley Quinn. [Lauren Alenia, a longtime Harley cosplayer](https://ihorror.com/lauren-alenia-promises-a-darker-more-disturbing-harley-quinn-in-jokers-wild-lazarus-risen/), steps into the role and promises something darker than the usual interpretation.
