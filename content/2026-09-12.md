[V/H/S: SCP](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3967426/v-h-s-scp-sets-2027-theatrical-relase-as-a24-boards/) gets its first theatrical release next year with A24 now producing. Framed as "recovered field documentation," the next V/H/S installment drops in 2027—a step up from the anthology's streaming-only history.

Joshua Giuliano's feature debut [River](https://bloody-disgusting.com/reviews/3967317/river-review-jane-levy-makes-a-ferocious-horror-return-in-throwback-slasher-tiff-2026/) played TIFF Midnight Madness to strong reviews. Jane Levy returns to horror in this throwback slasher opposite Jessica Rothe; Shudder will release it.

[Stuffed](https://bloody-disgusting.com/reviews/3967419/stuffed-review-jodie-comer-harry-melling-find-love-in-macabre-taxidermy-musical-tiff-2026/), a taxidermy-themed musical from debut director Theo Rhys, premiered at TIFF with Jodie Comer and Harry Melling. A morbid odd-couple romance that leans into death and decay.

Steven Spielberg's Amblin is producing [The Mandela Catalogue](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3967402/the-mandela-catalogue-steven-spielberg-produced-horror-movie-will-release-in-summer-2028/), adapting the viral YouTube series. Amazon MGM and United Artists on board; summer 2028 theatrical date set.

[Wrong Number](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3967312/wrong-number-kid-cudi-directing-and-starring-in-stalker-horror-movie/), Kid Cudi's directorial follow-up, reunites him with Barbie Ferreira for a stalker thriller. Cudi promises blood.

Ken Russell's [The Devils](https://bloody-disgusting.com/reviews/3967266/the-devils-review-ken-russells-masterpiece-has-never-been-more-relevant-or-beautiful-tiff-2026/), newly restored, screened at TIFF to praise as both culturally urgent and visually stunning. A reappraisal of his most censored work feels timely.

[Black Zombie](https://dailydead.com/interview-discussing-the-origins-of-the-zombie-with-black-zombies-maya-annik-bedward/), Maya Annik Bedward's documentary on the zombie's Haitian origins and pop-culture drift, offers a necessary correction to decades of George A. Romero fixation. Worth your time if you care how we got here.
