Mike Flanagan's [*Carrie* adaptation](https://bloody-disgusting.com/tv/3960919/carrie-seeks-friends-in-first-teaser-for-mike-flanagans-stephen-king-series-for-prime-video/) lands on Prime Video October 7 as an eight-episode limited series. The teaser positions it as a loneliness story rather than pure telekinetic rampage, which could work or could sand down the whole point—details sparse on execution.

Ryan Gosling is [officially Ghost Rider](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3960924/its-official-ryan-gosling-playing-ghost-rider-in-mcu-movie-being-directed-by-shawn-levy/) in an MCU film directed by Shawn Levy. This is Marvel doing Marvel things, but Gosling has wanted the role for years, so at least there's genuine interest on his end rather than just casting spreadsheet logic.

Jenn Wexler's [*The Last Temptation of Becky*](https://www.dreadcentral.com/news/582364/jenn-wexler-and-lulu-wilson-on-punk-energy-fighting-nazis-in-the-last-temptation-of-becky-interview/) premiered at SDCC. Wexler and Lulu Wilson discuss the film's punk ethos and its Nazi antagonists—sounds like the spiritual sequel to *Becky* actually has something to say rather than just repeating the gimmick.

Fantasia had two standouts: *Unholy Night*, which [takes the Evil Dead formula](https://www.dreadcentral.com/reviews/580183/unholy-night-tender-and-gnarly-in-equal-measure-fantasia-review/) somewhere tender enough to distinguish itself, and Kasper Kalle's [*No Rest for the Wicked*](https://www.dreadcentral.com/reviews/582571/no-rest-for-the-wicked-2026/), which pairs queer romance with genuine horror—described as *Call Me By Your Name* with actual teeth.
