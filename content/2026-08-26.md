Nicolas Winding Refn returns with [Her Private Hell](https://bloody-disgusting.com/home-video/3965258/her-private-hell-arrives-on-digital-today-ahead-of-november-physical-media-release/), his first film in a decade, now on digital platforms with physical media coming in November. Details sparse, but Refn's track record alone warrants attention.

Richard Kelly is writing [Donnie Darko: The Philosophy of Time Travel](https://bloody-disgusting.com/books/3965266/donnie-darko-the-philosophy-of-time-travel-richard-kellys-official-sequel-novel-coming-soon/), an 800-page sequel novel for the 25th anniversary. Kelly's also developing additional films in the universe.

[It Ends](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3965245/it-ends-final-trailer-hits-the-open-road-of-existential-horror/) expands nationwide August 28—Alexander Ullom's feature debut about recent grads trapped on a never-ending highway surrounded by cosmic horrors. The premise lands somewhere between road-trip dread and cosmic nihilism.

Yeon Sang-ho's [Colony](https://dailydead.com/review-colony-unleashes-the-horrors-of-hive-mind-zombies/) hits theaters August 28, pushing past Train to Busan into hive-mind zombie territory. The review calls it an evolution, not a retread.

[Backrooms](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3965303/liminal-horror-hit-backrooms-creeps-onto-hbo-max-next-month/) lands on HBO Max September 25. Kane Parsons' liminal horror film with Chiwetel Ejiofor.

Adam Wingard's [Onslaught](https://bloody-disgusting.com/interviews/3965269/adam-wingard-delivers-an-onslaught-if-the-blood-is-spraying-its-gonna-be-exciting/) (September 4) reunites him with the You're Next/The Guest universe. An action-slasher, not a franchise entry—Wingard's first original project in years. Free sneak screenings start August 27.

Dolly Parton [died Tuesday at 80](https://ihorror.com/dolly-parton-buffy-the-vampire-slayer-producer/). She was an uncredited producer on Buffy the Vampire Slayer—one of those hidden-in-plain-sight pieces of pop culture history.
