Jeremy Saulnier's [Slayground](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3968479/slayground-teaser-trailer-a24-and-jeremy-saulnier-unleash-a-haunted-attraction-horror-movie/) for A24 dropped a teaser—security-cam footage of a Halloween haunted house with a countdown clock. Green Room's return to horror after a decade.

[They Follow](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3968573/it-follows-2-they-follow-filming-wraps/) wraps filming. David Robert Mitchell's It Follows sequel has Maika Monroe back as Jay, joined by Naomi Ackie and Jackie Earle Haley.

Zach Cregger's [Resident Evil](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3968487/zach-creggers-resident-evil-debuts-with-over-100-million-at-the-worldwide-box-office/) opened to over $100 million worldwide, breaking franchise records. The reboot apparently works.

Mike Flanagan's [Carrie](https://bloody-disgusting.com/news/3968533/carrie-relifts-off-in-all-new-poster-for-prime-video-stephen-king-reboot/) lands on Prime Video October 7. New poster shows Summer H. Howell's Carrie levitating—premiered at Toronto and Fantastic Fest.

Adam Egypt Mortimer's [Pathetic Fallacy](https://bloody-disgusting.com/reviews/3968594/pathetic-fallacy-review-scott-mescudi-anchors-trippy-cosmic-horror-odyssey-through-love-and-loss-fantastic-fest-2026/) screens at Fantastic Fest with Scott Mescudi in his first lead role. Shot-on-film cosmic psychedelia tangled with grief and rage.

[In a Violent Nature 2](https://bloody-disgusting.com/reviews/3968571/in-a-violent-nature-2-review-slasher-sequel-delivers-incredible-kills-but-loses-killer-pov-fantastic-fest-2026/) premiered at Fantastic Fest as a work print. The sequel amps up kills but ditches the original's killer POV, with Ry Barrett's undead Johnny still carving through a summer camp.

[Hell House LLC](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3968628/hell-house-llc-theatrical-screenings-10th-anniversary/) hits theaters nationwide this October for its 10th anniversary—the first theatrical run for the cult found-footage film.
