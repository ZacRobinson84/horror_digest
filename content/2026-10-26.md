Peacock's _Crystal Lake_ fields [six actors playing Jason across its decades-spanning prequel](https://bloody-disgusting.com/editorials/3973150/meet-the-six-actors-who-brought-jason-voorhees-to-life-in-peacocks-crystal-lake/), a first for the franchise. Details sparse on how they're deployed.

[_Rubberhead_, a documentary on practical effects artist Steve Johnson](https://bloody-disgusting.com/reviews/3973286/rubberhead-review-steve-johnson-documentary-is-an-honest-tribute/), arrives as a genuinely wild tribute to someone actually worth the attention—not Rick Baker or Nicotero, but the sculptor/engineer behind the goblins and gore. Filmmaker Nick Taylor's take reads as honest rather than hagiographic.

_Clayface_ topped the box office with $43M worldwide. [The conversation around it](https://www.dreadcentral.com/box-office/595235/clayface-box-office-budget-success/) isn't whether it won or lost, but whether an R-rated DC body horror film counts as a win at all. It's still odd to see the genre muscle the blockbuster space.

[Claudio Simonetti's Goblin performed Argento's _Tenebre_ score live](https://rue-morgue.com/review-claudio-simonettis-goblin-melts-faces-at-tenebre-screening/) at the Somerville Theatre in Boston, the fourth stop on a month-long North American tour. If you're near a date, go.

[Glass Eye Pix turns 40 with a seven-film retrospective at IFC Center (Oct 28) followed by a one-day con in Manhattan (Nov 7)](https://ihorror.com/glass-eye-pix-40-ifc-center-optic-nerve-gep-con/). Larry Fessenden's outfit has quietly been the most consistent independent horror production house for four decades.

[_Trick 'r Treat_ returns to Regal on Halloween with the original Sam short restored in 4K](https://www.dreadcentral.com/news/595244/trick-r-treat-regal-halloween-seasons-greetings/). It's the 4K restoration angle that matters here—seeing that short theatrically, properly restored, actually earns the hype.

[James Tynion IV's _Exquisite Corpses_ comic miniseries pits iconic killers in a sadistic battle royale descending on an American town](https://bloody-disgusting.com/comics/3972835/james-tynion-ivs-exquisite-corpses-is-a-gory-halloween-epic-packed-with-iconic-killers/). Tynion's been on a horror streak since _Something Is Killing the Children_; this sounds properly ambitious.
