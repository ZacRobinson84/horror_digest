Mike P. Nelson's [Beware Boiúna](https://bloody-disgusting.com/reviews/3967900/beware-boiuna-review-giant-snake-horror-slithers-to-the-top-of-2026s-creature-feature-food-chain-fantastic-fest-2026/) lands at Fantastic Fest as a better argument for the creature feature subgenre than the actual Anaconda reboot—sweaty Amazon thrills and mean kills without the franchise baggage.

[V/H/S/MIXTAPE](https://ihorror.com/vhs-mixtape-first-poster-shudder-october-9/) drops October 9 on Shudder with segments from RZA, Ernest Dickerson, Renee Zhan, and GWAR. The annual autumn tradition continues.

Mike Flanagan is [writing a new Mist adaptation](https://rue-morgue.com/mike-flanagan-confirms-hes-writing-a-new-adaptation-of-the-mist/), departing from Frank Darabont's 2007 film from page one, with the script due by Halloween.

Zach Cregger's [Resident Evil](https://www.fangoria.com/resident-evil-zach-cregger-review/) follows the survival horror formula—slow build to the first zombie, then the gloves come off. It works.

[99 Nights in the Forest](https://ihorror.com/99-nights-in-the-forest-movie-sam-raimi-josh-cooley-20th-century/), the Roblox survival game, is becoming a live-action film at 20th Century Studios with Sam Raimi producing and Toy Story 4's Josh Cooley writing.

Martin Scorsese [joined Eva Green's Trees Are Watching](https://ihorror.com/trees-are-watching-martin-scorsese-eva-green-tiff/) as executive producer one day before its TIFF premiere—a psychological horror from Bahman Ghobadi.

Angel Melanson, formerly of Fangoria, [moves to Shudder](https://ihorror.com/angel-melanson-shudder-director-of-programming/) as Director of Programming.
