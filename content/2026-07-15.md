Rebekah McKendry's vampire revenge tale [Sundown](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3959349/first-look-at-rebekah-mckendrys-vampire-horror-sundown-teases-brutal-family-revenge-tale/) premieres at Locarno on August 13. Olwen Fouéré leads a family with fangs and a score to settle.

[Rubberhead](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3959403/rubberhead-documentary-spotlights-the-wild-life-work-of-sfx-raconteur-steve-johnson-trailer/), a documentary on creature effects maestro Steve Johnson, hits Fantasia this month. Johnson's work on *Fright Night*, *A Nightmare on Elm Street 4*, and *Poltergeist II* anchors a portrait of an artist who was candid about both his craft and his demons.

[Ferine](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3959340/ferine-official-teaser-trailer-carolyn-bracken-undergoes-an-animalistic-transformation/) arrives at Fantasia with Carolyn Bracken as an art collector whose rational mind wars with an animal instinct she can't control. Italian production, primal body horror.

Yeon Sang-ho's [Train to Busan](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3959405/train-to-busan-watch-the-brand-new-10th-anniversary-theatrical-release-trailer/) returns to U.S. theaters August 14 for its 10th anniversary. Ahead of his new film *Colony* (also August 28), the zombie thriller gets a theatrical lap.

[The Shards](https://bloody-disgusting.com/tv/3959330/the-shards-trailer-ryan-murphy-and-bret-easton-ellis-take-you-back-to-1981-this-summer/), Ryan Murphy and Bret Easton Ellis's adaptation of the 2023 prep school thriller, premieres on Hulu and FX August 5. Serial killer stalks L.A. teens in 1981.

Josh Hartnett anchors Netflix's sea creature series [Below](https://bloody-disgusting.com/tv/3959364/below-first-look-images-josh-hartnett-stars-in-netflixs-sea-creature-series/), arriving in October from *Umbrella Academy* creator Jesse McKeown. A grizzled fisherman confronts something in the deep.

[Evil Dead Burn](https://ihorror.com/evil-dead-burn-ignites-the-box-office-with-40-million-worldwide-opening/) opened to $40 million globally. Franchise holds.
