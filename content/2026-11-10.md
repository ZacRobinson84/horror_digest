Paranormal investigator Jay Alani adapts one of his own case files in [Numinous](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3975302/numinous-first-look/), directing and starring. Opens with police discovering a gutted structure and a body in remote Himachal Pradesh; details sparse beyond that.

Original *Return of the Living Dead* cast members have [publicly condemned an AI-generated sequel](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3975227/return-of-the-living-dead-aftermath-backlash-ai-linnea-quigley/). The backlash underscores the creative bankruptcy now associated with generative AI in film.

[Vera Farmiga returns to horror](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3975188/vera-farmiga-returns-to-horror-in-supernatural-movie-inferno/) in *Inferno*, a supernatural serial killer film directed by Bryan Hill ("Titans"). She'll also produce. Based on a rumored Scotland Yard unit investigating the occult.

Trailer drops: South Korean body-horror piece [*The Fin*](https://www.fangoria.com/the-fin-trailer-exclusive-south-korean-horror/) trades on post-industrial decay and political paranoia. [*Dead of Night*](https://bloody-disgusting.com/exclusives/3975133/dead-of-night-trailer-chris-jericho-produced-this-horror-movie-in-theaters-november-13/) (executive produced by Chris Jericho, inspired by the Night Stalker murders) hits theaters November 13. [*Unholy Night*](https://bloody-disgusting.com/exclusives/3975126/unholy-night-exclusive-trailer-dead-family-members-steal-christmas-in-shudders-holiday-horror-movie/) is a Christmas-set zombie comedy for Shudder. [*Cheer Bloody Murder*](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3975169/cheer-bloody-murder-trailer-scout-taylor-compton/) plays slasher tropes against cheerleading.

[*Witchcraft: The Lanterne of Light*](https://rue-morgue.com/witchcraft-the-lanterne-of-light-wraps-production/) (entry eighteen in the long-running occult franchise) wrapped production at Henry River Mill Village, the North Carolina ghost town used for *The Hunger Games*' District 12.

[Tyrus Pictures is developing a feature adaptation](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3975175/horror-manga-noah-of-the-blood-sea-feature-adaptation-to-unleash-terror-aboard-luxury-cruise-ship/) of the Japanese horror manga *Noah of the Blood Sea*, targeting 2027 production start. A family's luxury cruise turns monstrous.

Games: [*Wilderdark*](https://bloody-disgusting.com/video-games/3975277/dinosaur-survival-horror-game-wilderdark-launches-on-steam-today-trailer/) (dinosaur survival horror) launched on Steam today with a 20% discount through November 17. [*Where Dolls Hang*](https://rue-morgue.com/where-dolls-hang-gets-new-gameplay-trailer-showing-off-its-doll-haunted-island/), inspired by Mexico's real Isla de las Muñecas, got a fresh gameplay trailer.

**Worth reading in full:** [Ken Russell's *The Devils* (NC-17 Director's Cut) is getting a 4K Ultra HD release](https://bloody-disgusting.com/home-video/3975236/nc-17-directors-cut-of-ken-russells-the-devils-comes-home-with-4k-ultra-hd-physical-media-release/) — the film has already made $4 million in its theatrical run.
