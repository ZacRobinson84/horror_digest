Zach Cregger's Resident Evil opened to $108.3 million worldwide, the biggest start in franchise history. The film hit $60 million domestic alone—a record for the series.

Jeremy Saulnier's next for A24 is [Slayground](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3968479/slayground-teaser-trailer-a24-and-jeremy-saulnier-unleash-a-haunted-attraction-horror-movie/), a haunted attraction horror film. Teaser is live.

[River](https://dailydead.com/fantastic-fest-2026-review-river-is-an-artful-and-barbarous-slasher-debut/) sold at Fantastic Fest for $10 million—Universal and Blumhouse Atomic Monster picking up Joshua Giuliano's slasher debut. The film premiered to strong reception on the fest circuit.

Shudder acquired [The Glorious Dead](https://ihorror.com/the-glorious-dead-shudder-adams-family-2027/), an apocalyptic small-town horror film from the Adams family collective (John Adams, Toby Poser), for a 2027 release.

Hell House LLC hits [theaters nationwide](https://dailydead.com/horror-highlights-slayground-hell-house-llc-hangwater-tx/) for the first time, ten years after its original release, via Terror Films Releasing.

Barbara Crampton faces [The Kentucky Goblins](https://www.fangoria.com/kentucky-goblins-barbara-crampton/) in a new found-footage creature feature—described as a festive affair.

Hideo Kojima updated OD at Tokyo Game Show. [Production is progressing smoothly](https://bloody-disgusting.com/video-games/3968405/hideo-kojima-updates-od-development-during-tokyo-game-show-production-is-progressing-smoothly-video/), with Xbox backing the horror game after Sony's recent Physint deal fell through.

**Worth reading in full:** [The Cycle](https://dailydead.com/fantastic-fest-2026-review-the-cycle-is-a-patient-creature-feature-that-pays-off/) at Fantastic Fest—Jordan Downey's patient creature feature with practical effects.
