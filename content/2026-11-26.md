Takashi Miike's [Bad Lieutenant: Tokyo](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3976904/bad-lieutenant-tokyo-release-date-lily-james-takashi-miike/) lands March 5, 2027 via NEON. Shun Oguri in the title role; details sparse on how this one departs from Ferrara's NYC original.

Hideki Kamiya wants to [make a new Castlevania game](https://bloody-disgusting.com/video-games/3976916/devil-may-cry-director-hideki-kamiya-wants-to-make-a-new-castlevania-game-video/). The Devil May Cry and original RE2 director floated the idea in a recent Q&A after Konami's Belmont's Curse revival earlier this year.

[Godzilla Minus Zero](https://bloody-disgusting.com/news/3976898/godzilla-minus-zero-and-backrooms-among-top-20-oscar-finalists/) made the Visual Effects Oscar shortlist for the 99th Academy Awards, alongside Backrooms. Its predecessor became the first Godzilla film to win the category.

Amazon is reportedly adapting Christopher Buehlman's [Between Two Fires](https://www.dreadcentral.com/comics-books/598901/between-two-fires-amazon-adaptation-report/), a medieval horror novel. Details on the project remain thin.

Osgood Perkins' [The Young People](https://rue-morgue.com/the-young-people-earns-r-rating-for-violence-and-gore/) for NEON earned an R rating for violence and gore. No release date yet, but the MPAA rating suggests the bloodletting won't be subtle.

Solo developer DustyEffsky's [DEAD COPIES](https://bloody-disgusting.com/video-games/3976922/dystopian-dark-comedy-title-dead-copies-sees-you-using-dead-bodies-to-solve-puzzles-trailer/) arrives on Steam March 9—a dark-comedy puzzle game where you solve problems using literal corpses in a dystopian industrial setting.
