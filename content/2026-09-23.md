David Robert Mitchell wrapped [*They Follow*](https://rue-morgue.com/they-follow-wraps-filming-on-the-it-follows-sequel/), his sequel to *It Follows*, with Maika Monroe returning and Naomi Ackie and Jackie Earle Haley joining the cast. No release window yet.

Roy Lee, who produced Zach Cregger's *Resident Evil*, is [developing a *Silent Hill* reboot](https://ihorror.com/silent-hill-reboot-roy-lee-resident-evil-producer/). No studio, writer, or format announced.

Robert Eggers' next is [*Romeo and Juliet*](https://ihorror.com/robert-eggers-romeo-and-juliet-focus-features-werwulf/) at Focus Features—a Shakespeare adaptation, not the horror pivot some might've guessed. Shoots after *Werwulf* opens Christmas.

Joko Anwar's [*Ghost in the Cell*](https://www.dreadcentral.com/reviews/589514/ghost-in-the-cell-review-joko-anwars-latest-is-expectedly-gnarly-surprisingly-heart-warming-fantastic-fest/) premiered at Fantastic Fest as a horror-comedy about friendship and capitalism wrapped in Indonesian body horror. Anwar's balancing gnarly practical effects with genuine sweetness.

Jessica Biel is producing [a *Coffin Moon* series](https://ihorror.com/coffin-moon-series-paramount-jessica-biel-keith-rosson/) at Paramount, adapting Keith Rosson's vampire novel set in the '70s. Stephen King praised the book.

Jane Schoenbrun's meta slasher [*Teenage Sex and Death at Camp Miasma*](https://ihorror.com/teenage-sex-and-death-at-camp-miasma-mubi-streaming-october-2-vhs/) hits MUBI October 2, same night its VHS tour kicks off in theaters.

James Cullen Bressack shot [*I Have Proof*](https://ihorror.com/i-have-proof-was-recorded-over-somebody-elses-family-tapes/) on secondhand VHS-C tapes other people had already used—fragments of their original recordings sometimes survived between takes, leaving other people's memories literally inside the finished film.
