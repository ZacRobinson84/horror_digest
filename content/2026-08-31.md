Casper Kelly's *Buddy*, a Barney-meets-body-horror concept, [doubled its box office projections](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3965838/casper-kellys-buddy-doubles-box-office-projections-with-5-3-million-opening/) with a $5.3 million opening weekend, landing at #6 against bigger competition. Indie horror doing indie horror things.

Ridley Scott [called *Alien: Romulus* just "okay"](https://www.dreadcentral.com/news/586912/ridley-scott-says-alien-romulus-was-just-okay-is-rewriting-the-next-alien/) and is rewriting the next *Alien* prequel himself. The third prequel, long dormant, is apparently back on.

Phil Tippett's studio [is not shutting down](https://bloody-disgusting.com/the-further/3965861/tippett-studio-releases-official-statement-about-the-companys-future-not-shutting-down/), despite weekend reports saying otherwise. The VFX legend behind *Jurassic Park*, *Star Wars*, and *Mad God* clarified the company's future in an official statement.

*Ever After*, a twisted fairy-tale horror from Laszlo Illes, opens September 4 and [features Bonnie Aarons and Mark Sheppard](https://ihorror.com/interview-bonnie-aarons-enters-a-twisted-fairy-tale-in-ever-after/) among its ensemble. Details sparse but the cast suggests it's not taking itself too seriously.

Julio Román's *Soul Snatchers*, a Puerto Rican production, [treats theology as actual material](https://ihorror.com/soul-snatchers-has-more-theology-than-its-104-minutes-can-hold/) rather than window dressing for demons throwing furniture. The 104 minutes apparently strain under the weight of what it's trying to say.

David Gregory of Severin Films [would rather restore Lucio Fulci's *Bloody Moon*](https://ihorror.com/david-gregory-would-rather-restore-bloody-moon-than-citizen-kane/) than *Citizen Kane*. Said at Severin's 20-year anniversary event in LA, which speaks to where his priorities (and ours) should be.
