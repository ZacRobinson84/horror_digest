Leslye Headland (The Acolyte) is directing [Feed](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3974952/feed-rosamund-pike-starring-in-body-horror-movie-inspired-by-hatching/), a body horror film starring and produced by Rosamund Pike, inspired by Hatching. Daniella Isaacs wrote the script.

Freddie Prinze Jr. wrapped principal photography on [Killing Time](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3974947/killing-time-freddie-prinze-jr-starring-in-psychological-home-invasion-horror-movie/), a psychological home invasion thriller. It's being described as a reimagining of Who's Afraid of Virginia Woolf? with horror elements.

[Godzilla Minus Zero](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3974858/godzilla-minus-zero-epic-final-trailer-unleashes-king-ghidorah/) is in theaters now. The final trailer confirms King Ghidorah as Godzilla's antagonist in Takashi Yamazaki's sequel to Godzilla Minus One.

Kiernan Shipka stars in [Hyena](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3974896/hyena-movie-david-lynch-kiernan-shipka/), a psychological thriller executive produced by David Lynch and directed by Scott Coffey. Edgar Ramirez and Rosemarie DeWitt also star.

Nicholas Galitzine is in final talks to play Ichabod Crane opposite Sydney Sweeney in [Hollow](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3974838/nicholas-galitzine-to-play-ichabod-crane-opposite-sydney-sweeney-in-hollow/), a new take on The Legend of Sleepy Hollow based on an upcoming 2027 novel.

Yellowjackets returns for [its fourth and final season](https://bloody-disgusting.com/tv/3974788/yellowjackets-season-4-trailer/) on November 20, with the survivors finally heading home to Wisayok. The trailer suggests a reckoning waiting for them there.

Netflix's [Other Worlds: A Stranger Things Adventure](https://bloody-disgusting.com/tv/3974935/other-worlds-a-stranger-things-adventure-netflix-announces-new-live-action-spinoff-series/) is a live-action spinoff set in 1950s Marion, Nevada, premiering in 2028. It's the first of a rumored multi-project Stranger Things expansion.
