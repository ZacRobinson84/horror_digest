[Spielberg producing The Mandela Catalogue](https://ihorror.com/the-mandela-catalogue-movie-steven-spielberg-analog-horror/) as a feature film. The analog horror series moves off YouTube into theatrical territory. Details sparse on the adaptation itself, but the pairing is genuinely odd — he's aware of what made it work.

[Remothered games getting Unreal Engine 5 remasters](https://bloody-disgusting.com/video-games/3958264/remastered-editions-of-remothered-tormented-fathers-and-remothered-broken-porcelain-announced-trailer/) for PS5, Xbox Series, and Switch 2. Stormind Games rebuilding the first two entries while still working on Red Nun's Legacy.

[Frictional Games delays ONTOS to 2027](https://bloody-disgusting.com/video-games/3958258/sci-fi-horror-mystery-title-ontos-pushed-to-2027-trailer/). The Amnesia developers call it their most ambitious project yet — worth the wait if it's actually ambitious and not just longer.

[Steelkrill Studio's Where Dolls Hang embeds full-length films in-game](https://bloody-disgusting.com/video-games/3958261/where-dolls-hang-lets-players-watch-full-length-movies-in-game/). The psychological survival horror lets you discover and watch complete horror features directly in the world, echoing The Darkness's trick with To Kill a Mockingbird.
