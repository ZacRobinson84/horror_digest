Rob Savage's *Other Mommy*, adapted from Josh Malerman's novel, arrives this Halloween with Jessica Chastain in the lead. Poster's out, tickets selling.

Universal and Blumhouse Atomic Monster are [acquiring *River*](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3967572/river-universal-blumhouse-atomic-monster-closing-deal-to-acquire-jane-levy-jessica-rothe-slasher-movie/), a slasher that premiered at Toronto with Jane Levy and Jessica Rothe. The film was originally backed by Shudder and Independent Film Company.

Guillermo del Toro wants to adapt [three H.P. Lovecraft stories for television](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3967553/guillermo-del-toro-h-p-lovecraft-adaptation-future/), moving past his long-stalled *At the Mountains of Madness* feature. Details sparse on which tales or timeline.

Justin G. Dyck (*Anything for Jackson*) is directing *Casket Girls*, a New Orleans vampire thriller with [Gabrielle Union investigating a supernatural case](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3967524/gabrielle-union-to-battle-new-orleans-vampires-in-casket-girls-first-look/) that pulls her into the city's undead underworld. Mike Colter, Matt Frewer, and Raúl Castillo also star.

Agata Trzebuchowska (*Ida*) makes her feature debut with [*Sick*](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3967538/coming-of-age-horror-sick-acquired-ahead-of-fantastic-fest-premiere/), a coming-of-age horror that Pluto Film acquired ahead of its Fantastic Fest premiere. Described as an unsettling look at growing up.

Brad Anderson is developing *Session 1*, [a prequel to his 2001 cult film *Session 9*](https://rue-morgue.com/brad-anderson-is-developing-a-session-9-prequel-called-session-1/). He and writer Stephen Gevedon announced the project at a 25th-anniversary screening. The rights holder has already rejected the idea once.

Takashi Miike's [*Bad Lieutenant: Tokyo*](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3967460/bad-lieutenant-tokyo-trailer-lily-james-and-shun-oguri-star-in-takaski-miikes-yakuza-thriller/) premiered at Toronto. Lily James and Shun Oguri star in NEON's yakuza thriller.
