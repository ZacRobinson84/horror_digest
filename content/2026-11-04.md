Paul W.S. Anderson returns to cosmic horror with [adaptations of *Nether Station* and *Nether Vortex*](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3974444/nether-station-nether-vortex-paul-w-s-anderson/), his first space-horror project since *Event Horizon*. Details sparse, but the pairing suggests he's committing to the subgenre rather than dipping a toe in.

A [fan-made short called *The Cat and the Hatchet*](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3974581/the-cat-and-the-hatchet-hits-1-6-million-views-and-a-feature-film-is-being-crowd-funded/) hit 1.6 million YouTube views in its first week—a warped Dr. Seuss property that's now getting a feature-length crowdfund. The public-domain-character-goes-horror trend keeps finding audiences.

Antenna Releasing picked up [*I Don't Like It Here*](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3974512/antenna-releasing-acquires-grieve-filmmaker-robbie-smiths-brutal-horror-film-i-dont-like-it-here/), a 66-minute punisher from Robbie Smith (Heavy Heavy Low Low's frontman), shot in the California desert for under $20K. Lean, mean, and apparently effective.

[*The Invited*](https://bloody-disgusting.com/news/3974515/wendy-webb-books-franchise-tv-show-the-invited/), a supernatural series based on Wendy Webb's novels, is in development with Jeff Howard (*Oculus* co-writer) on the adaptation. The streaming era's turned Webb's work into TV-ready material.

Zach Cregger wants [to stay involved with *Resident Evil*](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3974433/resident-evil-zach-cregger-future-plans-oversee/) beyond his feature, hoping to "have a hand" in the franchise's direction. Reasonable ask given what he did with the IP.
