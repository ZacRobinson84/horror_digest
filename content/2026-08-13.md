Ken Russell's [restored *The Devils* Director's Cut](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3963236/ken-russells-the-devils-directors-cut-rated-nc-17-for-sexual-content-and-graphic-nudity/) arrives in theaters this October rated NC-17—the first time the 1971 film has been remastered and released theatrically in full. Warner Bros. Clockwork and the BFI are handling the fall rollout.

Justin Seaman's *[The Barn Part III](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3963355/the-barn-part-iii-trailer-goes-heavy-on-the-halloween-monsters-and-practical-effects/)* screens at Popcorn Frights tomorrow ahead of its release, packing the film with practical creature work and a cast of horror fixtures assembled for what's positioned as the final chapter of the Halloween creature trilogy.

*[Gator Face](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3963368/gator-face-trailer-a-reptilian-cult-feeds-its-victims-to-ravenous-alligators-this-september/)* wades in September 15 on digital—an alligator creature feature from Epic Pictures' DREAD label featuring actual reptiles and a Rudyard Kipling poem that apparently anchors the film's imagery.

A documentary [*Black Zombie*](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3963317/black-zombie-trailer-chronicles-historical-origins-of-zombie-cinema-in-new-documentary/) from Maya Annik Bedward excavates the Haitian origins and Black spiritual traditions that were distorted in early zombie cinema, opening September 11 in NYC and LA through Kino Lorber.

Casper Kelly's *[Buddy](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3963225/casper-kellys-buddy-is-rated-r-for-bloody-violence-involving-children/)* hits theaters August 28—a riff on children's TV that turns murderous, carrying an R rating for "bloody violence, some drug content and language – all involving children."

Mike P. Nelson's *[Beware Boiúna](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3963303/beware-boiuna-official-posters-devour-jimothy-and-all-your-favorite-viral-animals/)* gets its trailer tomorrow from Lionsgate. The giant snake film has already deployed a fun series of character posters where it devours internet-famous animals instead of listing cast.

Bill Moseley and Sean Gunn [lead *Cotton Eye Joe*](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3963240/bill-moseley-and-sean-gunn-lead-slasher-movie-cotton-eye-joe-as-filming-begins/), a folk-song-turned-slasher now filming in Illinois. Details sparse beyond the premise: four friends stranded on a deserted road.
