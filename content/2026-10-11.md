Toho's [*Godzilla Minus Zero*](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3971329/godzilla-minus-zero-new-trailer-brings-maximum-punishment/) hits November 6—the first Japanese production shot for IMAX. The [Dread Central review](https://www.dreadcentral.com/reviews/593001/godzilla-minus-zero-review-big-monstrous-and-confused/) calls it visually dazzling but sluggish, hamstrung by repetitive emotional padding between monster battles.

Michael Dougherty says [*Trick 'r Treat 2*](https://ihorror.com/trick-r-treat-2-script-dougherty-witching-hours/) has a finished script and he's ready to shoot. He's traced Sam's origin to a childhood Halloween mugging, which either deepens the character or misses the point—details sparse.

Mike Flanagan's [*The Exorcist: Martyrs*](https://rue-morgue.com/mike-flanagan-unleashes-a-blood-scrawled-first-trailer-for-the-exorcist-martyrs/) trailer is out with Scarlett Johansson hunting a satanic killer's legacy. [Dread Central's theory](https://www.dreadcentral.com/news/593032/exorcist-martyrs-julie-kinderman-theory/) flags a possible *Exorcist III* connection buried in the footage.

[In a Violent Nature: Part 2](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3971270/in-a-violent-nature-part-2-brings-johnny-back-for-a-bloody-valentines-release/) arrives February 12, 2027—Ry Barrett's Johnny announced the date at NYCC. A summer camp sequel landing on Valentine's weekend is either marketing or genuinely funny.

Guillermo del Toro converted [*Pan's Labyrinth*](https://ihorror.com/pans-labyrinth-3d-20th-anniversary-re-release/) to 3D with 1,300 artists and no AI upscaling. It's in theaters now from Cineverse and Fathom.

The new [*Paranormal Activity*](https://ihorror.com/paranormal-activity-2027-original-house-ian-tuason/) (the eighth) shoots in the original 2007 house. Ian Tuason directs for Blumhouse and Paramount—whether this is a return to form or franchise fatigue in haunted-house drag remains to be seen.
