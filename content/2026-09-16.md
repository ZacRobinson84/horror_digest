**Clayface** gets an R rating for strong bloody violence, gore, language, nudity, and brief drug use—so DC's leaning into the body horror angle here.

French feature **Sunnyside** imagines a world where [humans are farmed for food](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3967826/sunnyside-movie-french-cannibals/), dressed up as a response to overpopulation. The Coven (Terrifier 3 sales) has boarded it.

**Black Sheeps**, Jonathan King's sequel to his 2006 cult splatter-comedy, is [20 years later and brings back the killer sheep](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3967799/black-sheeps-trailer-20-years-later-the-killer-sheep-horror-movie-gets-a-bonkers-sequel/). Wētā Workshop handling creature work again.

**The Night Driver** (Jeremy Weiss) [premieres at Screamfest](https://ihorror.com/night-driver-screamfest-2026-opening-night-sean-cunningham/) October 6. Josh Lucas, David Arquette, Alyssa Milano in a road thriller with a demented hitchhiker. Sean Cunningham producing.

Paolo Strippoli's **The Spiral** (TIFF) is a Faustian family nightmare where [a mother confesses to a series of twisted bargains](https://bloody-disgusting.com/reviews/3967668/the-spiral-review-a-wickedly-entertaining-faustian-family-nightmare-tiff-2026/). Strippoli directed A Classic Horror Story.

**Brooklyn Horror Film Festival** (Oct 15–22 at Nitehawk) [opens with I Love Paris](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3967770/the-brooklyn-horror-film-festival-announces-full-2026-lineup/), a vampire mockumentary. Closes with Kick On, a drug-fueled action horror.

**Widow's Bay** (Apple TV) swept the Emmys with [14 wins including Outstanding Comedy Series](https://bloody-disgusting.com/tv/3967632/widows-bay-takes-home-record-breaking-14-emmy-awards-including-outstanding-comedy-series/), cementing that horror-comedy crossover appeal.

Mike Flanagan's **Dark Tower** adaptation [is still active](https://bloody-disgusting.com/tv/3967771/the-dark-tower-reboot-mike-flanagan-update-stephen-king/), despite years of radio silence. Flanagan confirmed it's "very much" in development.
