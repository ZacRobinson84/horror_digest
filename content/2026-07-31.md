[Boorman and the Devil](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3961548/exorcist-ii-documentary-boorman-and-the-devil-trailer/) arrives August 28 in NYC. The documentary charts how John Boorman, a legitimately great director, made one of cinema's most reviled films with *Exorcist II: The Heretic*. New trailer out now.

[Stephen King and Sam Raimi are finally collaborating](https://www.dreadcentral.com/news/583360/the-directors-behind-final-destination-bloodlines-land-stephen-kings-desperation-from-sam-raimi/) on *Desperation*, with the *Final Destination: Bloodlines* directing team attached. Details sparse, but the pairing alone is worth noting.

[Lee Cronin to direct a competing *A Nightmare on Elm Street*](https://www.dreadcentral.com/news/583350/warner-bros-is-reportedly-racing-paramount-to-make-its-own-a-nightmare-on-elm-street-movie-lee-cronin-to-direct/) for Warner Bros. while Paramount races ahead with its own take. The rights situation is messy; worth watching how this resolves.

[Eli Roth's *Ice Cream Man*](https://www.dreadcentral.com/interviews/583028/eli-roth-gives-the-scoop-on-his-gruesome-ice-cream-man-theres-not-going-to-be-anything-quite-like-it-in-the-theater/) is coming. Roth claims it's going to be gruesome in ways we haven't seen before. Interview covers the project in more depth.

[*Nightborn*](https://bloody-disgusting.com/exclusives/3961422/theres-something-very-wrong-with-the-newborn-in-nightborn-exclusive-clip/) hits Shudder July 31. Hanna Bergholm (*Hatching*) directs; Seidi Haarla and Rupert Grint star in this dark fable about a very wrong newborn. New clip available.

[Michael Dougherty restored his Sam short *Season's Greetings*](https://www.dreadcentral.com/news/583280/michael-dougherty-shares-stunning-new-look-at-sam-in-newly-restored-seasons-greetings-short/). The pre-*Trick 'r Treat* origin of the character is getting a fresh look. Dougherty shared new imagery.
