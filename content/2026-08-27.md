Felix Barrett is adapting [Paranormal Activity for the stage](https://bloody-disgusting.com/interviews/3965407/director-felix-barrett-on-adapting-paranormal-activity-broadway/), with the theater director behind The Drowned Man bringing the found-footage franchise to Broadway. The six-part limited series will expand the property beyond film, though details on casting and format remain sparse.

Stephen King's [Crouch End is coming to Prime Video](https://bloody-disgusting.com/tv/3965594/crouch-end-stephen-kings-cthulhu-mythos-short-story-getting-prime-video-series-adaptation/) as a six-part limited series. The 1980 Lovecraftian short story from *New Tales of the Cthulhu Mythos* follows a couple lost in a nightmarish version of London.

Samara Weaving and Nicholas Braun are [starring in Hivemind](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3965452/samara-weaving-nicholas-braun-to-star-in-killer-wasp-thriller-hivemind-from-eli-director/), a killer wasp thriller from Ciarán Foy (*Eli*), currently shooting with Prime Video set to stream it internationally.

[Pendulum](https://www.dreadcentral.com/trailer/586779/pendulum-trailer-pits-joseph-gordon-levitt-against-a-terrifying-supernatural-threat/) pairs Joseph Gordon-Levitt with a supernatural threat in Mark Heyman's directorial debut (Heyman co-wrote *Black Swan*). Aronofsky produces; Vertical releases it January 1, 2027.

Konami wants [annual Silent Hill releases](https://bloody-disgusting.com/video-games/3965498/konami-aims-to-make-annual-release-of-new-silent-hill-games/), with franchise producer Motoi Okamoto stating the publisher plans yearly entries and wants to expand beyond the American town setting.

[Frictional Games showed gameplay](https://bloody-disgusting.com/video-games/3965487/disturbing-new-gameplay-trailer-released-for-ontos-watch/) for ONTOS at Gamescom—a psychological horror game starring Stellan Skarsgård, set for 2027 release.

[Penny Lane Is Dead](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3965446/ozploitation-horror-penny-lane-is-dead-gets-bloody-character-posters-ahead-of-friday-release/) hits Shudder August 28. This ozploitation throwback set in 1986 Australia centers on a teenager's college celebration spiraling into violence.
