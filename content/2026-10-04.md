Cullen Bunn and Marika Cresta reunite for [a new haunted house comic](https://rue-morgue.com/cullen-bunn-and-marika-cresta-reunite-for-new-horror-comic-we-cannot-remain/) called "We Cannot Remain." The *Deluge* team is back with unhoused survivors trapped in a building that won't let them leave. Ignition Press, January.

Matt Wagner and Kelley Jones's [*Dracula* graphic novel saga](https://rue-morgue.com/dracula-book-iv-the-undead-kickstarter-passes-125000-in-its-first-days/) hit its $666 funding goal and then some—$125K and 1,400 backers in the first days. "Book IV: The Undead" is the concluding chapter.

Richard Kelly [says he's unsatisfied with both existing cuts](https://ihorror.com/richard-kelly-third-donnie-darko-cut-killing-moon/) of *Donnie Darko* and wants to make a third. He'd restore "The Killing Moon" to its original place in the film.

Kid Cudi [posted stills from *Wrong Number*](https://ihorror.com/wrong-number-first-look-kid-cudi-barbie-ferreira/), the stalker thriller he wrote, directed, scored, and stars in opposite Barbie Ferreira. Details sparse on plot.

A teaser for Ron Krauss's [*Wounds*](https://ihorror.com/wounds-teaser-jack-kilmer-paris-jackson-screamfest/) shows a gore artist (Jack Kilmer) at an actual murder scene. Paris Jackson co-stars. Premiering at Screamfest October 8, shot on 35mm.

Dark Sky Films puts John McNaughton's [*Henry: Portrait of a Serial Killer*](https://ihorror.com/henry-portrait-of-a-serial-killer-4k-40th-anniversary-dark-sky/) on 4K for its 40th anniversary October 27, with four new documentaries and a one-night Regal screening October 14.
