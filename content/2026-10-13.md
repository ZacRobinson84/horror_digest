Hammer Films [acquired the complete works](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3971502/hammer-films-acquires-rights-to-the-woman-in-black-author-susan-hills-complete-works/) of Susan Hill, expanding beyond *The Woman in Black*. No word yet on what else gets the treatment, but the 2012 film pulled the studio's biggest US opening in its history.

[Ti West directed *Remnants*](https://bloody-disgusting.com/the-further/3971489/remants-ti-west-directed-the-first-immersive-horror-movie-for-the-apple-vision-pro/), billed as the first immersive horror film for Apple Vision Pro—a 27-minute ghost story arriving October 16. Whether spatial horror actually works or just makes you nauseous remains to be seen.

Stephen King [called Mike Flanagan his "best" adapter](https://bloody-disgusting.com/tv/3971541/stephen-king-mike-flanagan-adaptation/) and teased a secret project beyond the four completed adaptations and the Dark Tower series still in development. Details sparse, but if Flanagan's attached, it's worth tracking.

[*In a Violent Nature: Part 2*](https://dailydead.com/horror-highlights-in-a-violent-nature-part-2-r-l-stines-the-babysitter/) lands February 12, 2027. The first one found an audience; this one's already got a release date locked in.

[*River* trailer dropped](https://rue-morgue.com/trailer-arrives-for-survival-slasher-river-starring-jane-levy-and-jessica-rothe/)—faceless-killer survival slasher with Jane Levy and Jessica Rothe, hitting theaters early next year after festival play.

Tubi and R.L. Stine [are adapting *The Babysitter*](https://rue-morgue.com/r-l-stines-teen-thriller-the-babysitter-coming-to-the-screen/), his 1989 novel, aiming for 2027. Extending their existing partnership with a feature.

[*Carrie* fans spotted matching necklaces](https://www.dreadcentral.com/editorials/593312/carrie-necklace-theory-psychic-connection/) in Flanagan's series that could hint at broader psychic connections among characters—or just be clever costume design. Either way, people are reading into it.
