Mike P. Nelson directing the next [Saw film](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3969022/saw-mike-p-nelson-directing-next-installment-in-the-film-franchise/) for Blumhouse Atomic Monster and Lionsgate, with James Wan guiding. Nelson's recent work on creature features suggests a shift away from the torture-porn aesthetics of the later sequels.

[V/H/S/MIXTAPE](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3968880/gwar-demands-you-play-this-v-h-s-mixtape-trailer-at-max-volume/) hits Shudder October 9. The found-footage anthology turns up the volume with Ernest Dickerson, Flying Lotus, Ghost's Tobias Forge, and RZA among the directors—less a gimmick than a genuine reframing of what found-footage horror can do with sound design.

[Pan's Labyrinth](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3968949/pans-labyrinth-20th-anniversary-featurette-explores-why-guillermo-del-toros-masterpiece-couldnt-be-made-today/) back in theaters for its 20th anniversary. A new featurette explores del Toro's practical effects work and argues, credibly, that the film's blend of dark fantasy and taboo-breaking body horror wouldn't get greenlit now.

[Silent Hill: Townfall](https://bloody-disgusting.com/video-games/3968952/silent-hill-townfall-available-now-on-pc-playstation-trailer/) is out now on PS5 and PC. Screen Burn Interactive's take on the franchise, co-published by Annapurna and Konami, centers on a man summoned back to the town—details sparse on what that entails.

Joko Anwar (Satan's Slaves, Impetigore) delivers [Ghost in the Cell](https://www.dreadcentral.com/exclusive/589876/ghost-in-the-cell-exclusive-clip-twists-hands-and-turns-stomachs-in-joko-anwars-latest/), an exclusive clip of which twists hands into shapes hands shouldn't twist. Indonesian horror continues to operate in its own register.

Dark Sky Films acquired U.S. rights to [Sender](https://rue-morgue.com/dark-sky-films-acquires-u-s-rights-to-sender-starring-jamie-lee-curtis-and-david-dastmalchian/), Russell Goldman's paranoid thriller starring Britt Lower and produced by Jamie Lee Curtis. Spring 2027 theatrical release after festival runs.

Tananarive Due's [Mazywood](https://bloody-disgusting.com/reviews/3968978/mazywood-book-review-tananarive-dues-chilling-horror-epic-about-black-hollywoods-haunted-legacy/) is a horror novel about Black Hollywood's haunted legacy. Due remains one of the few writers working who can think and write at equal depth about the genre's mechanics and its cultural weight.
