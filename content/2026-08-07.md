Gareth Edwards [won't direct the next *Jurassic World* film](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3962614/new-jurassic-world-movie-in-development-but-gareth-edwards-will-not-be-directing/) after steering *Rebirth* to $869 million. Details sparse on what's next.

[*Appofeniacs*](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3962611/appofeniacs-trailer-manipulates-reality-as-deepfake-videos-turn-deadly/) arrives October 2—a sci-fi horror satire on deepfakes and AI from music video director Chris Marrs Piliero. Trailer shows the expected carnage.

John David Washington stars in [*Mime*](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3962592/john-david-washington-to-star-in-supernatural-horror-mime-from-producer-scott-derrickson/), a supernatural horror set in a NYC apartment during a heat wave blackout. Scott Derrickson produces. Sony Screen Gems beat out Amazon and Searchlight for it.

Jane Levy and Jessica Rothe lead [*River*](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3962527/jane-levy-jessica-rothe-starring-river-opens-tiff-2026-midnight-madness-genre-slate/), opening TIFF's Midnight Madness program—a survival horror about three estranged siblings.

Blumhouse is [developing a *Puppet Master* reboot](https://www.dreadcentral.com/news/584116/report-blumhouse-developing-puppet-master-reboot-with-possible-tv-series-also-in-the-works/) with a possible TV series alongside it. One of horror's longest-running franchises finally getting another swing at theatrical.

[*Welcome to Elderfield*](https://bloody-disgusting.com/video-games/3962475/cozy-horror-rpg-welcome-to-elderfield-set-to-launch-september-10-trailer/) launches September 10 on Steam—a Lovecraftian cozy horror RPG where you farm and befriend (or hex) locals. It's as it sounds.

Fantasia wrapped with [*I Love Paris*](https://rue-morgue.com/offbeat-vampire-films-i-love-paris-and-no-rest-for-the-wicked-top-fantasia-audience-awards/) and *No Rest for the Wicked* taking audience awards. *I Love Paris* is a mockumentary vampire film where the lead's pursuit of fame and victims are one and the same.
