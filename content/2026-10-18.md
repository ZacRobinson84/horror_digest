Tom Roberts [gave the original *Nosferatu* a new score](https://rue-morgue.com/original-nosferatu-gets-new-score-by-pianist-tom-roberts/) with period-accurate arrangement. A neat complement for the season.

Brad Caleb Kane [discussed the *Crystal Lake* reimagining](https://www.dreadcentral.com/interviews/593589/brad-caleb-kane-on-pamela-voorhees-creative-kills-and-whats-next-for-crystal-lake-interview/), breaking down his take on Jason and Pamela's origin. The show's already [altered the franchise's understanding of Jason](https://www.fangoria.com/crystal-lake-season-1-jason-voorhees-twist/) in significant ways.

*I Love Paris*, a vampire mockumentary with Aminata Thiboult and director Nicky Murphy, premiered at [TADFF](https://bloody-disgusting.com/reviews/3971858/i-love-paris-review-hilarious-vampire-mockumentary-is-one-of-the-years-best-hidden-gems-tadff-2026/) and [Brooklyn Horror](https://www.dreadcentral.com/reviews/594218/i-love-paris-review-we-have-a-new-vampire-icon-on-our-hands-brooklyn-horror-film-fest/). Both outlets called it a standout—fresh, funny, and stylish.

Jill Schoelen returns in [*Ralph's Perfekt Christmas*](https://ihorror.com/ralphs-perfekt-christmas-trailer-jill-schoelen-cineverse/), her first feature in 29 years. It's a Christmas horror comedy hitting theaters October 30.

Idris Elba [co-directed and stars](https://ihorror.com/above-the-below-first-images-idris-elba-lionsgate/) in *Above the Below*, an underwater survival thriller from Lionsgate due December 4.

Telluride Horror Show and Nightmares Film Festival are both running this weekend. Coverage from [Telluride](https://bloody-disgusting.com/the-further/3972357/telluride-horror-show-day-one-dispatch-cozy-campfires-great-horror-and-stunning-views/) and [Nightmares](https://ihorror.com/nightmares-film-festival-sweetest-place-in-horror/) already live.
