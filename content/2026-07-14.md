Paramount's acquired U.S. theatrical rights to a [new Nightmare on Elm Street film](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3957310/freddys-back-new-a-nightmare-on-elm-street-movie-in-the-works-at-paramount/) from the Wes Craven estate. First project under the deal; details on writer and director are sparse.

[Crystal Lake](https://bloody-disgusting.com/tv/3959246/all-eight-episodes-of-crystal-lake-will-premiere-on-october-15th/) premieres all eight episodes on Peacock October 15. The Friday the 13th prequel centers Pamela Voorhees' descent following Jason's drowning, with Linda Cardellini in the role Betsy Palmer originated.

Don Mancini's new [Chucky film](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3959169/brad-dourif-teases-new-chucky-movie-will-be-unlike-anything-fans-expect/) won't continue from where the TV series left off, according to Brad Dourif. He teased it'll take the franchise in an unexpected direction.

[Sundown](https://www.fangoria.com/sundown-first-look-images/), Rebekah McKendry's supernatural survival horror produced by Slash, is in post-production. First-look images show a creature feature with practical creature work intact.

Mike Flanagan's [Carrie miniseries](https://bloody-disgusting.com/tv/3959113/carrie-first-look-images-preview-mike-flanagans-stephen-king-series-for-prime-video/) for Prime Video expands King's story beyond the White household. Summer H. Howell plays Carrie; the adaptation introduces additional telekinetic characters.

Trevor Henderson's [Cartoon Cat](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3959251/cartoon-cat-trevor-hendersons-viral-art-getting-a-horror-film-adaptation-from-tristar-pictures/) is heading to TriStar Pictures. The internet-born horror property follows Siren Head into theatrical development.

[The Devil's Mouth](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3959217/the-devils-mouth-official-poster-kathryn-newton-shark-movie-premieres-this-month/), a shark-attack film with Kathryn Newton, drops on Prime Video July 29.
