Ti West's [*Ebenezer*](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3960764/ebenezer-official-trailer-ti-west-turns-johnny-depp-into-a-christmas-carol-icon-scrooge/) drops November 13 with Johnny Depp as Scrooge. Trailer and poster are out; details sparse beyond the pitch, but West's track record suggests this won't be a straight period piece.

[*Her Private Hell*](https://www.dreadcentral.com/reviews/582324/her-private-hell-nicolas-winding-refn-returns-with-a-gory-beautifully-perverse-genre-bending-thriller-fantasia-review/), Nicolas Winding Refn's return to features after a decade away, premiered at Fantasia with Sophie Thatcher and Charles Melton. Genre-bending thriller built on themes of passion and identity; the gory stuff serves the perversity, not the other way around.

Netflix is [reteaming with Yeon Sang-ho](https://bloody-disgusting.com/tv/3960620/netflix-reteams-with-train-to-busan-director-on-live-action-parasyte-tamiya-series/) (*Train to Busan*, *Parasyte: The Grey*) for live-action *Parasyte: TAMIYA* in 2027. Based on the manga; Yeon co-writes.

Zach Cregger's [*Resident Evil*](https://rue-morgue.com/zach-creggers-resident-evil-trailer-takes-us-to-a-snowy-and-bloody-raccoon-city/) trailer dropped with Austin Abrams as a medical courier trapped in a single night at Raccoon City. Low-angle, handheld approach—nothing like the prior franchise films. Theaters September 18.

Two documentaries on cult 1980s misfires: [*A World War II Fairy Tale*](https://bloody-disgusting.com/exclusives/3960641/new-documentaries-explore-the-cult-legacies-of-the-keep-and-wolfen-watch-the-trailers-now-exclusive/) covers Michael Mann's *The Keep*, arriving October on limited release; paired with one on *Wolfen*. Both acquired by Antenna Releasing.

[Argentinean film *The Dollmaker*](https://rue-morgue.com/argentinean-and-lynchian-horror-film-the-dollmaker-is-coming-to-the-u-s-trailer-poster/) lands stateside after winning major festival awards. Described as Lynchian; trailer and poster now live.

*Pinocchio Unstrung* in the Twisted Childhood Universe is [surprisingly solid](https://dailydead.com/review-there-are-no-strings-to-the-surprisingly-solid-pinocchio-unstrung/). Second entry in what used to be called the Poohniverse; this one actually works.
