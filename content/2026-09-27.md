Robert Eggers' [*Werwulf* drops a second trailer](https://ihorror.com/werwulf-trailer-2-robert-eggers-full-moon/) timed to the full moon, with Aaron Taylor-Johnson howling and Willem Dafoe hunting. Eggers claims it's the darkest thing he's written. There's also [an ARG running through November](https://dailydead.com/watch-the-new-trailer-for-werwulf-plus-take-part-in-the-movies-alternate-reality-game/).

Fede Álvarez is [working on a third *Don't Breathe*](https://www.dreadcentral.com/news/590308/dont-breathe-3-fede-alvarez-announces-new-script-with-jane-levy-and-stephen-lang-returning/) with Jane Levy and Stephen Lang returning. Announced at a tenth-anniversary screening of the first film.

[*Evil Dead Burn* hits HBO Max October 2](https://ihorror.com/evil-dead-burn-hbo-max-october-2-hbo-triple-bill/), followed by an Evil Dead triple bill on HBO the next night.

Tim Waggoner's cult novel [*Like Death* is getting a 20th-anniversary reissue with a never-published sequel](https://rue-morgue.com/tim-waggoners-like-death-rises-again-with-a-sequel-in-tow/) from Crystal Lake Publishing.

Johnny Depp's [*Day Drinker*](https://ihorror.com/day-drinker-trailer-johnny-depp-lionsgate-march-2027/) teaser suggests something inhuman—full moon, growl, wrong eyes. Lionsgate, March 2027.

[*Bloody Tennis*](https://www.fangoria.com/bloody-tennis-horror-womens-elite-athletics/) uses horror to examine institutional abuse in women's elite athletics.
