John Carpenter says [the ending of *The Thing* contains a clue nobody has figured out](https://ihorror.com/john-carpenter-the-thing-ending-clue-academy-museum-gala/) after 44 years of people inspecting eyeballs and breath. He didn't say what it is.

[Ken Russell's *The Devils* is back in theaters](https://www.fangoria.com/ken-russell-the-devils-2026-box-office/) and actually finding an audience—the controversial 1971 horror-religious nightmare getting its proper resurrection run.

Clive Barker says [he's been sitting on a run of *Hellraiser* film ideas](https://ihorror.com/clive-barker-hellraiser-films-ideas-waiting-in-the-wings/) in his notes. The *Hellraiser: Revival* game apparently lit a fire under him.

[Marta Giec's *ÒPI*](https://ihorror.com/opi-review-polish-folk-horror-family-secrets/) is a Kashubian folk horror film about inherited rituals and family secrets—the kind where a grandmother's offhand warning shapes generations. Playing Brooklyn Horror Film Festival.

[*Slayground*](https://www.dreadcentral.com/news/594337/slayground-poster-january-release-date/) hits theaters January 29. A24's teaser poster is all that's out; full trailer tomorrow.

Hunter Schafer confirmed [the untitled A24 vampire film from Arkasha Stevenson](https://ihorror.com/hunter-schafer-a24-vampire-movie-arkasha-stevenson/) is "batshit crazy" and her favorite thing she's shot.
