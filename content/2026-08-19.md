Clive Barker is returning to short fiction with *Library of the Dead*, a [three-book anthology series](https://dailydead.com/clive-barkers-library-of-the-dead-revealed-a-trio-of-new-horror-anthologies-due-out-in-2027/) positioned as a spiritual successor to *Books of Blood*. The project is live on Kickstarter and will roll out across 2027.

Lee Cronin's *The Mummy* star Jack Reynor has joined [*Seasons*](https://www.fangoria.com/seasons-cast-jack-reynor/), the horror film from *Companion* director Drew Hancock based on a viral Reddit post about a couple who bought a ranch. Lily James is already attached.

Adam Egypt Mortimer (*Daniel Isn't Real*) is bringing [*Pathetic Fallacy*](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3964177/pathetic-fallacy-first-image-teases-psychedelic-horror-from-adam-egypt-mortimer/) to Fantastic Fest next month—a psychedelic horror with Kid Cudi, Jessica Rothe, Travis Fimmel, and Natasha Lyonne. First image looks properly unhinged.

[Fantastic Fest's 21st edition](https://dailydead.com/fantastic-fest-announces-2026-lineup-including-resident-evil-beware-boiuna-the-swallow-and-much-more/) (Sept 17–24) is stacked: Zach Cregger's *Resident Evil* adaptation, *Beware Boiúna* opening the fest, and 35 world premieres across the full slate.

*The Creep Tapes* season 3 returns to Shudder September 15 with [more Peachfuzz chaos](https://www.fangoria.com/the-creep-tapes-season-3-trailer/). Duplass and Brice's deranged unicorn mascot continues finding new victims.

Hayden Panettiere, who played Kirby Reed in the *Scream* franchise, [died this past weekend](https://www.dreadcentral.com/news/585685/scream-stars-remember-hayden-panettiere-you-will-be-forever-missed/) at 36. Co-stars including Neve Campbell and Melissa Barrera shared tributes.
