Fede Álvarez has finished the script for [*Don't Breathe 3*](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3969292/dont-breathe-3-jane-levy-and-stephen-lang-will-return-for-upcoming-sequel/), with Jane Levy and Stephen Lang both returning. He announced it at a Beyond Fest screening, so details are sparse, but the third entry is locked in.

[*The Dark Gets In*](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3969006/the-dark-gets-in-exclusive-teaser-follows-a-documentary-team-into-supernatural-horror/) premieres at Beyond Fest on October 2—a documentary team investigates a disappearance and finds something worse. The teaser is out now.

Takashi Yamazaki defended the [R rating on *Godzilla Minus Zero*](https://www.dreadcentral.com/box-office/590356/godzilla-minus-zero-director-explains-historic-r-rating-cutting-scenes-was-just-not-an-option/), the first *Godzilla* film to earn one from the MPA. He said cutting scenes "was just not an option"—the violence was integral.

Stephen King announced [*The Institute* Season 2 lands on MGM+ November 8](https://bloody-disgusting.com/tv/3969339/stephen-kings-the-institute-season-2-sets-november-premiere-date-on-mgm/). King tweeted that "the kids are done running," implying an escalation.

After winning the Cheval Noir at Fantasia, [*No Rest for the Wicked*](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3969340/supernatural-queer-romance-no-rest-for-the-wicked-acquired-for-2027-release/)—a supernatural gothic romance set on the Faroe Islands in 1862—has been acquired by Altered Innocence for a 2027 theatrical release.

Kyle Mangione-Smith's [*Compliance*](https://dailydead.com/fantastic-fest-2026-review-compliance-is-an-uncomfortable-found-footage-conspiracy-thriller/), a found-footage surveillance-state thriller premiered at Fantastic Fest this week. It's polarizing—Letterboxd shows either 1-star hate or 5-star reverence, which suggests something actually divisive made it through.

Na Hong-jin's creature film [*Hope*](https://www.dreadcentral.com/streaming-home-video/590352/hope-neons-mind-blowing-sci-fi-monster-movie-comes-home-october-13/) hits digital October 13 from NEON. If you missed it in theaters, prepare for a war zone.
