Patrick Brice reunites the [Creep](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3974733/psychosexual-horror-violet-from-creep-director-to-star-lili-reinhart-mark-duplass-lukas-gage-and-more/) team with [Violet](https://www.dreadcentral.com/news/596652/lili-reinhart-mark-duplass-and-others-join-psychosexual-horror-film-violet-from-creep-director/), a psychosexual horror pic starring Lili Reinhart, Mark Duplass, Suki Waterhouse, and Lukas Gage. Duplass co-wrote the original Creep, so the pairing feels earned rather than recycled.

Francis Galluppi, [director of Evil Dead Wrath](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3974780/john-constantine-movie-for-dc-being-written-by-evil-dead-wrath-director-francis-galluppi/), has been hired to write DC's new John Constantine movie. First live-action Constantine feature since 2005.

[Maika Monroe tears through](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3974626/maika-monroe-rages-in-victorian-psycho-final-trailer-as-tickets-go-on-sale/) the final trailer for Victorian Psycho, a horror-comedy set in Victorian England. Hits theaters November 13.

[Synapse Films will release](https://rue-morgue.com/synapse-films-will-bring-jeff-liebermans-just-before-dawn-to-4k-following-legal-ownership-resolution/) Jeff Lieberman's Just Before Dawn on 4K following a legal ownership resolution. The survival-horror classic finally gets the physical media it deserves.

Ken Russell's [NC-17 director's cut of The Devils](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3974642/the-devils-directors-cut-home-video-release-ken-russell/) is getting a home video release date tease. Details sparse, but the full announcement is coming.

Sam Raimi and Decentralized Pictures launched the [Sam Raimi Horror Award](https://rue-morgue.com/sam-raimi-and-decentralized-pictures-launch-the-sam-raimi-horror-award-for-emerging-filmmakers/), a $25,000 prize offering funding, mentorship, and distribution to emerging horror shorts. Submissions open soon.

Elena Maganini, [editor of Henry: Portrait of a Serial Killer and the Emmy-winning Dexter pilot](https://ihorror.com/elena-maganini-henry-portrait-serial-killer-dexter-editor-dies/), died October 21 in Burbank at 70.
