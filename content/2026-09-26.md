Melissa Barrera says she'd return to the *Scream* franchise, but [not under Paramount and Spyglass's watch](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3969241/scream-melissa-barrera-franchise-return-reaction/). After her unceremonious exit, she's made clear the studio would need to change hands first.

Mike Flanagan moved his *Carrie* Prime Video series [into the present day](https://bloody-disgusting.com/news/3969223/carrie-reboot-timeline-change-explained-mike-flanagan/) from King's novel, anchoring key thematic elements to contemporary concerns rather than the '70s.

Rebekah McKendry's vampire film *Sundown* [has found a home at Shudder](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3969125/sundown-rebekah-mckendrys-supernatural-horror-movie-finds-a-home-at-shudder/). The film follows three generations of women in a remote cabin seeking revenge, only to uncover something worse.

Robert Eggers' *Werwulf* trailer [drops tomorrow](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3969111/robert-eggers-werwulf-trailer-will-be-unleashed-tomorrow-new-teaser-howls-at-the-moon/). A new teaser arrived today with an atmospheric howl, three months out from release.

Ari Aster's *Scapegoat* cast expands with [Ella Purnell, Caleb Landry Jones, and Samba Schutte](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3969116/scapegoat-ari-aster-ella-purnell-caleb-landry-jones/) joining Scarlett Johansson, Jacob Elordi, and Jude Law. Still no word on what it's actually about.

AMC+ releases [*Yaga*](https://bloody-disgusting.com/tv/3969098/spellbinding-yaga-trailer-reimagines-the-baba-yaga-legend-this-halloween/) October 23, a contemporary mystery thriller that reimagines the Baba Yaga myth with Carrie-Anne Moss and Noah Reid.

Kino Lorber opens E. Elias Merhige's *Begotten* in a [4K restoration](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3969094/disturbing-experimental-horror-begotten-gets-4k-restoration-theatrical-release/) at the IFC Center. The 1991 experimental nightmare—not for the squeamish—gets its theatrical restoration treatment alongside *The Devils*.
