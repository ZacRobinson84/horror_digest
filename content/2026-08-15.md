David Robert Mitchell's [The End of Oak Street](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3963713/friday-august-14-these-8-new-horror-movies-released-this-week/) is in theaters now—dinosaur creature feature from the It Follows director playing as an Amblin throwback. Also out this week: [a Director's Cut of The X-Files: I Want to Believe](https://bloody-disgusting.com/interviews/3963378/chris-carter-the-x-files-i-want-to-believe-directors-cut-interview/) that Chris Carter says is scarier, plus a 10th anniversary theatrical re-release of Train to Busan. Carter [teased a third X-Files film](https://bloody-disgusting.com/interviews/3963378/chris-carter-the-x-files-i-want-to-believe-directors-cut-interview/) in the works.

Yeon Sang-ho's [Colony](https://bloody-disgusting.com/exclusives/3963569/colony-clip-reveals-the-horrific-next-evolution-of-train-to-busan-filmmakers-zombies/), his follow-up zombie film, arrives August 28. An exclusive clip shows an evolution of the infected from his previous work.

[John Carpenter confirmed at Fan Expo Chicago](https://www.dreadcentral.com/news/585234/john-carpenter-just-dropped-another-update-on-the-thing-2-were-working-on-it-now/) that The Thing 2 is actively in development.

Netflix [ordered Sid & Zoey to series](https://www.dreadcentral.com/tv/585160/netflix-orders-sid-zoey-supernatural-romance-about-two-teens-drawn-into-the-dark-secrets-beneath-an-idyllic-mississippi-town/)—an 8-episode supernatural mystery from Jon Kasdan (Willow) with Blumhouse Atomic Monster and Bad Robot producing. It centers on a seductive, amoral teenager and dark secrets in Mississippi.

Shudder is [releasing the pregnancy horror Goody Goody in September](https://www.dreadcentral.com/news/585225/shudder-sets-pregnancy-horror-goody-goody-for-september-release/).

Rick Baker won [Locarno Film Festival's 2026 Vision Award](https://www.fangoria.com/rick-baker-locarno-film-festival-vision-award/) for his decades-long makeup work and multiple Oscars.

[Grimmfest announced its October 2026 lineup](https://www.fangoria.com/grimmfest-2026-lineup/) with features, shorts, UK premieres, and a special scream queen guest.
