Victor Miller, screenwriter of the original Friday the 13th, [has died at 86](https://ihorror.com/victor-miller-friday-the-13th-screenwriter-dies-86/). He created the template that defined the slasher genre for generations.

Zach Cregger's Resident Evil screened for press and audiences this week. [Early reactions focus on resource scarcity](https://ihorror.com/resident-evil-first-reactions/)—Cregger wants viewers mentally tracking every bullet, turning the film into something closer to a game than a movie experience.

Yeon Sang-ho (Train to Busan) is [reworking zombie tropes with Colony](https://rue-morgue.com/exclusive-interview-colony-director-yeon-sang-ho-on-his-new-approach-to-zombies-and-more/). Interview details sparse but worth reading for his approach to the exhausted genre.

Tommy Wiseau's second feature, Big Shark, [arrives on Tubi September 4](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3966337/tommy-wiseaus-big-shark-finally-swims-onto-streaming-this-week/) after sitting in development since 2019. Three firefighters versus a shark attack; details sparse.

A documentary examining John Boorman's much-maligned horror sequel is [making a case for reappraisal](https://bloody-disgusting.com/reviews/3910604/boorman-and-the-devil-bhff-review/). Rare indie effort to rehabilitate something the genre had written off.

Adam Wingard and Simon Barrett reunite for [Onslaught, hitting theaters September 4](https://dailydead.com/onslaught-interviews-with-adria-arjona-adam-wingard-drew-starkey-and-dan-stevens/). Early word: violent and weird in the way their previous work promised.

Stream Train, a six-player co-op survival horror from Half Past Yellow, [launches October 15](https://bloody-disgusting.com/video-games/3966247/social-co-op-survival-horror-game-stream-train-boards-october-15-playtest-sign-ups-now-available-trailer/) with playtest signups live on Steam through September 14.
