Miike's [Bad Lieutenant: Tokyo](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3967460/bad-lieutenant-tokyo-trailer-lily-james-and-shun-oguri-star-in-takaski-miikes-yakuza-thriller/) premiered at TIFF with Lily James and Shun Oguri. Yakuza thriller, not horror proper, but worth the attention.

Zach Cregger's [Resident Evil](https://bloody-disgusting.com/interviews/3967450/resident-evil-practical-effects-interview/) drops Friday with a 2,000-pound practical monster puppet built by SFX legend. Test audiences found it funnier than intended. Film adapts the first thirty minutes or so of the game's setup.

[Behind the Mask II](https://ihorror.com/behind-the-mask-2-nathan-baesel-meta-horror/) is happening. Nearly two decades after the cult first film, Nathan Baesel confirmed the sequel is in development and staying meta.

Korean director Na Hong-jin's alien thriller [Hope](https://www.fangoria.com/hope-korean-alien-movie-box-office/) opened wide in the U.S.—over 1,500 screens, unusual for Korean cinema—and won the box office. Details sparse on plot, but the wide release suggests distributor confidence.

Ron Murphy's [Back in Black](https://dailydead.com/tiff-2026-review-back-in-black-chases-death-with-laughs-and-smiles/) premiered at TIFF. Dark comedy about a town where citizens start dropping dead and someone's making money off it. Trailer Park Boys and Wynonna Earp director, so expect irreverence.

Nicholas Pineda's bodycam found-footage piece [Infirmary](https://ihorror.com/infirmary-trailer-shudder-found-footage-nicholas-pineda/) hits Shudder October 2. Security guard vanishes in an abandoned psychiatric hospital.

Mason Jeffrey Newton-DeRushie's puppet horror-fantasy [Kylgor](https://www.fangoria.com/kylgor-in-the-grisly-abyss-goodbye-monster-fantastic-fest/) screens at Fantastic Fest next week alongside a Fangoria Studios project.
