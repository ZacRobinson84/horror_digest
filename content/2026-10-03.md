Filming wrapped on [Twilight of the Dead](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3970240/twilight-of-the-dead-filming-wraps-on-kate-beckinsale-zombie-movie-based-on-george-a-romero-story/), a final zombie film based on a George A. Romero treatment, with Kate Beckinsale and Betty Gabriel leading. It's being positioned as the closing chapter to Romero's saga, with Greg Nicotero handling effects.

Sam Raimi and Jordan Peele are producing together for the first time on [Portrait of God](https://rue-morgue.com/portrait-of-god-moves-to-lionsgate-as-sam-raimi-and-jordan-peele-produce-together-for-the-first-time/), a religious horror feature that Lionsgate just picked up. Chase Infiniti stars.

Richard Kelly's [Donnie Darko](https://rue-morgue.com/richard-kelly-on-donnie-darko-at-25-and-its-still-expanding-parallel-universes/) is back in theaters at 25, with Kelly hoping to release a third cut after revisiting the film through his new 600-page novel exploring the aftermath of the story.

[The Watcher](https://bloody-disgusting.com/news/3970297/the-watcher-season-2-start-filming-netflix/) season 2 finally started filming four years after Netflix's renewal—one of the longest gaps between consecutive seasons in TV history. Naomi Watts, Bobby Cannavale, Margo Martindale, and Isabel Gravitt are back.

Kid Cudi shared [first images from Wrong Number](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3970344/kid-cudi-shares-first-images-from-stalker-horror-movie-wrong-number/), his second directorial feature, a stalker horror co-starring Barbie Ferreira. Cudi writes, directs, and stars.

[Black Chariot](https://www.dreadcentral.com/trailer/591554/eerie-black-chariot-trailer-rides-in-with-black-and-white-supernatural-noir-tale-exclusive/), a black-and-white supernatural noir, arrives in UK theaters this October.

[Access:Horror](https://www.fangoria.com/access-horror-virtual-lineup/) returns as a month-long virtual festival (October 4–November 1) with 19 international films, built specifically for disabled audiences with accessibility at its center.
