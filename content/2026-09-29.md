Zach Cregger's *Resident Evil* crossed $100 million domestically, the first film in the franchise to do so. The sewer monster was a 2,000-pound puppet requiring four operators—practical work that paid off.

Jordan Downey's [*The Cycle*](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3969422/the-cycle-trailer-stephen-king-calls-this-movie-the-thing-meets-silence-of-the-lambs/) hits Shudder October 23. Stephen King calls it "*The Thing* meets *Silence of the Lambs*," which is either the pitch or the whole review depending on your trust in King's taste.

Olivier Assayas and Kristen Stewart reunite for [*Spiritual World*](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3969430/spiritual-world-kristen-stewart-plays-a-haunted-clairvoyant-in-personal-shopper-directors-new-movie/), described as a "steamier" *Personal Shopper* with Stewart as a clairvoyant plagued by premonitions. Also stars Vicky Krieps and Riley Keough.

Mike Flanagan wants [*Carrie*](https://bloody-disgusting.com/tv/3969414/carrie-reboot-mike-flanagan-three-season-plan-stephen-king/) to run three seasons. After Gerald's Game and Doctor Sleep, he's earned the right to ask. Details sparse on the Stephen King adaptation beyond Flanagan's ambition.

[*It Follows*](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3969387/sequel-they-follow-adds-landman-star-james-jordan-to-cast/) sequel *They Follow* wraps production. James Jordan (Landman, The Endless) joins the cast; the film picks up a decade later. Maika Monroe returns.

[*The Institute*](https://bloody-disgusting.com/tv/3969339/stephen-kings-the-institute-season-2-sets-november-premiere-date-on-mgm/) season 2 premieres November 8 on MGM+. King tweeted that the kids are done running, which tracks.

Lily Gladstone and Kiowa Gordon team up for the [horror western *The Curse of Silver*](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3969359/horror-western-the-curse-of-silver-to-star-lily-gladstone-and-kiowa-gordon/), reuniting with *Quantum Cowboys* director Geoff Marslett.

[*No Rest for the Wicked*](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3969340/supernatural-queer-romance-no-rest-for-the-wicked-acquired-for-2027-release/), a supernatural gothic romance set on the Faroe Islands in 1862, took the Cheval Noir at Fantasia. Altered Innocence acquired it for 2027 theatrical release.
