Joe Wright (Hanna) is directing [Juice](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3958182/joe-wright-to-direct-post-apocalyptic-thriller-juice-adaptation/), a post-apocalyptic thriller adapted by Emmy winner Abi Morgan from Tim Winton's novel. Details sparse beyond the setup—a man recruited into a resistance organization—but Wright's Black Mirror work suggests he can handle the scale.

Zach Cregger and Brian Duffield are [developing Siren Head](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3958091/zach-cregger-and-brian-duffield-teaming-on-siren-head-horror-movie-based-on-viral-sensation/) for Warner Bros., based on Trevor Henderson's viral creature design. It's the obvious play—meme-to-screen after The Backrooms—but Cregger's track record (Weapons, Resident Evil) and Duffield's (Spontaneous) suggest they might actually do something with it.

David Robert Mitchell is [reworking the dinosaur movie](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3958057/the-end-of-oak-street-dinosaur-movie/) with The End of Oak Street, a sci-fi horror set in a residential suburb rather than the usual wasteland. It Follows director plus Abrams' production company equals worth watching. Hits theaters August 14.

[Good Manners](https://bloody-disgusting.com/home-video/3958135/brazilian-werewolf-fable-good-manners-finally-gets-blu-ray-release/), Marco Dutra's 2017 São Paulo–set werewolf fable, is finally getting physical media via Vinegar Syndrome on Blu-ray with a limited slipcover.

Michael Gabriele's [Unholy Night](https://bloody-disgusting.com/exclusives/3957333/unholy-night-exclusive-teaser-and-images-reveal-undead-grandmas-christmas-eve-carnage/) premieres at Fantasia. A Christmas Eve slasher built around an undead grandmother on a murder spree—premise solid enough, and Gabriele's short "Get Away" took gold at the festival before.

Paul Tremblay talks [fighting AI in publishing](https://bloody-disgusting.com/interviews/3957926/paul-tremblay-on-fighting-ai-with-horror-in-new-novel-dead-but-dreaming-of-electric-sheep/) with his new novel Dead But Dreaming of Electric Sheep. A writer using horror to push back on LLMs feels more honest than most AI discourse currently happening.

[Escape From Umbra](https://bloody-disgusting.com/video-games/3958018/psychological-horror-game-escape-from-umbra-launches-july-10-trailer/), a psychological horror from WildSphere, launches July 10 across all current platforms. Moves away from their earlier gore-focused work toward atmosphere instead.
