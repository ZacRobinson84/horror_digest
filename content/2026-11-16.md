Clint Howard's [*Ice Cream Man* sequel is in production](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3975860/another-ice-cream-man-sequel-to-clint-howard-movie-now-in-production/), with PJ Soles (*Halloween*, *The Devil's Rejects*) attached. The 1990s cult item finally gets its follow-up after years of Howard pushing for it.

[*The Caged*](https://bloody-disgusting.com/exclusives/3975730/the-caged-trailer-exclusive/) premiered at Screamfest 2026 and draws from the St. Osyth witch prison in Essex—a genuinely haunted location with historical weight. Getting a theatrical release soon.

Danny Boyle says [Alex Garland finished the script for *28 Years Later 3*](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3975851/28-years-later-part-iii-danny-boyle-says-theyd-have-to-raise-the-money-to-finish-the-trilogy/), and Cillian Murphy is on board, but the money isn't. The second film underperformed at $58 million worldwide, leaving the trilogy's fate uncertain.

Maika Monroe [won't return for *Longlegs 2*](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3975842/longlegs-2-maika-monroe-says-lee-harker-will-not-be-back-in-the-follow-up-movie/), despite earlier interest in reprising Lee Harker. Nicolas Cage and Osgood Perkins are still moving forward without her.

Katie Dippold's *Widow's Bay* earned a [Grammy nomination for David Fleming's score](https://bloody-disgusting.com/tv/3975841/widows-bay-and-stranger-things-5-earn-grammy-nominations/), marking the only horror title in the Best Score Soundtrack category for 2027. The comedy-horror series already claimed 14 Emmys in September.

Indie game *Endacopia*—a [twisted take on children's edutainment](https://bloody-disgusting.com/video-games/3975814/surreal-point-and-click-horror-adventure-game-endacopia-coming-to-consoles-trailer/)—is coming to PS5 and Xbox Series after six Horror Game Awards nominations. Currently on Steam with a demo available.

Chris Carter [wants another *X-Files* project](https://www.dreadcentral.com/streaming-home-video/597825/the-x-files-chris-carter-mulder-scully-return/) with Mulder and Scully—either a movie or limited series—but says he'll wait until Ryan Coogler's Hulu reboot finds its footing first.
