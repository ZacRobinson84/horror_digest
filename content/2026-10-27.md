Dead Man's Bones are [back with their first new music in 17 years](https://bloody-disgusting.com/news/3973430/dead-mans-bones-new-music-return-ryan-gosling-zach-shields/). Ryan Gosling and Zach Shields released "Lost in the Night," a new single with a music video starring Rain Spencer and Noah Jupe. It's been long enough that this barely registers as news anymore, but here we are.

Bill Nighy has [joined Alfonso Cuarón's Apple TV series *Ascension*](https://bloody-disgusting.com/tv/3973397/bill-nighy-joins-apple-tv-cult-horror-series-ascension-from-alfonso-cuaron/), playing a documentarian opposite Caitríona Balfe. The cult horror series is based on Adam Nevill's *Last Days*.

Chris LaMartina, who made *WNUF Halloween Special*, is [taking the seasonal horror concept to New Year's Eve with *Spirit Tasting*](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3973377/wnuf-halloween-special-creator-takes-on-new-years-eve-horror-with-spirit-tasting/). A roadshow tour runs November through early January with cast appearances at select stops.

The canceled *Paranormal Activity* game has [found new life as a free Steam release called *Playable Archive*](https://bloody-disgusting.com/video-games/3973369/darkstone-digital-shadow-drops-playable-archive-for-free-on-steam-trailer/). Developer Brian Clarke polished the scrapped *Paranormal Activity: Threshold* and released it for nothing.

*MADiSON*, the psychological horror game, is [getting a film adaptation](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3973367/madison-the-terrifying-video-game-is-getting-a-horror-movie-adaptation/) from Story Kitchen, the production company behind the *Sonic* films.

Robert Eggers' *Werwulf* is [screening in 35mm at select theaters on Christmas Day](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3973312/werwulf-playing-in-35mm-in-select-theaters-christmas-day-and-tickets-are-on-sale-now/). Tickets are on sale now.

Nikolaj Coster-Waldau will [lead MGM+'s *The Black Tapes*](https://bloody-disgusting.com/tv/3973313/nikolaj-coster-waldau-to-star-in-mgms-the-black-tapes-horror-series/), an eight-episode adaptation of the 2015 horror podcast. The series reunites original podcast creators Terry Miles and Paul Bae.
