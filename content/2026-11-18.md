Steve Miner is [directing a new Friday the 13th film](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3976124/jason-13th-official-movie-will-release-in-theaters-august-13-2027-from-cineverse/) simply titled *Jason*, arriving August 13, 2027 via Cineverse. The 13th official entry marks Miner's return to the franchise after directing parts 3 and the Final Chapter.

Robert Eggers' *Werwulf* prioritized practical creature work for Aaron Taylor-Johnson's [transformation sequence](https://rue-morgue.com/werwulf-team-details-its-brutal-werewolf-transformation/). The director and creature designer David White built a custom rig to maximize the on-screen brutality, departing from the usual digital polish.

Ilja Rautsi, writer of *Hatching*, makes his directorial debut with [*Red Snow*](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3976067/vampire-santa-terrorizes-a-family-in-bloody-red-snow-trailer/)—a Finnish horror-comedy about a vampire posing as Santa. It hits Shudder December 11.

*The Third Parent*, adapted from the Tommy Taffy creepypasta, earned an R rating for [disturbing bloody violence and gore](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3976146/tommy-taffy-horror-movie-the-third-parent-gets-r-rating-for-disturbing-bloody-violence-and-gore/). The film arrives this winter with Crispin Glover and Rob Lowe.

Joe Bob Briggs and distributor Bob Berney launched [Coney Island Pictures](https://rue-morgue.com/joe-bob-briggs-and-bob-berney-launch-new-horror-studio-coney-island-pictures/), a new horror studio developing a *Basket Case* reimagining with original director Frank Henenlotter attached.

GRINDWORM, a [psychological horror game](https://bloody-disgusting.com/video-games/3976109/dig-your-way-out-next-year-in-psychological-horror-title-grindworm-trailer/) from Snail Bite, arrives on Steam January 18, 2027. The trailer emphasizes oppressive atmosphere and an immersive in-world interface.

Found-footage Christmas horror *Santaphobia* traps characters in [an endless Christmas Day loop](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3975999/santaphobia-trailer-found-footage-holiday-horror-movie-traps-you-in-an-endless-christmas-day-loop/). Director Airell Anthony Hayles' film premieres on Found TV November 30.
