Zach Cregger's *Resident Evil* crossed $400 million worldwide and is now [available on digital](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3974381/watch-the-first-ten-minutes-of-zach-creggers-resident-evil-now-available-on-digital/). The film became the franchise's highest-grossing entry. Fandango is streaming the first ten minutes.

Jamie Jones's supernatural horror *Sketchbook* starts production in Wales early 2027, with [Woody Norman and Billie Piper](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3974324/cursed-drawings-to-torment-woody-norman-and-billie-piper-in-supernatural-horror-sketchbook/) leading. A teenage boy's cursed drawings come to life.

[Neve Campbell confirmed her return as Sidney Prescott in *Scream 8*](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3974320/scream-8-neve-campbell-return-sidney-prescott-ghostface/). The hunt for a director is ongoing, but at least that's settled.

Rob Savage's *Other Mommy* hits digital November 10, with [4K and Blu-ray following December 29](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3974356/other-mommy-digital-release/). The supernatural adaptation of Josh Malerman's novel includes deleted and alternate scenes.

*Obsession* director Curry Barker is [teasing an extended cut](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3974263/obsession-extended-cut-curry-barker/) that runs twenty minutes longer than the theatrical version. He's expressed interest in releasing it.

[Top Hat Studios published the indie horror anthology *Spooky Tales*](https://bloody-disgusting.com/video-games/3974348/updates-aplenty-during-top-hat-studios-fall-showcase-including-horror-anthology-spooky-tales-trailer/) during their Fall Showcase. The retro-inspired game from Liminal Road is coming to Steam.

Glenn McQuaid's *The Restoration at Grayson Manor* lands on demand this week. The filmmaker, known for *I Sell the Dead* and *Tales from Beyond the Pale*, made this one before the streaming boom crept in everywhere.
