Zach Cregger's *Resident Evil* [officially begins production](https://bloody-disgusting.com/editorials/3966643/resident-evil-everything-we-know-about-zach-creggers-horror-adaptation-before-release/), with the filmmaker adapting the game series for the big screen. The shift from Paul W.S. Anderson's action-heavy take to a director who actually engages with the source material is the only thing worth caring about here.

Na Hong-jin, the director behind *The Wailing*, delivered [*Hope*](https://www.dreadcentral.com/reviews/587576/hope-review-a-thrilling-throwback-to-the-big-dumb-monster-movies-of-the-90s/), a creature feature that plays like a '90s monster movie with actual scale and budget behind it. Long wait between films, but the premise—a giant monster emerges—gets wrapped in something with teeth.

Hammer Films' [4K restoration of *Dracula*](https://www.fangoria.com/grimmfest-opening-closing-lineup/) will open Grimmfest in Manchester, uncut and remastered. The vampire classic gets a festival premiere before wider release, alongside *Victorian Psycho* and other lineup additions.

Kane Parsons is [returning to direct *Backrooms 2* for A24](https://ihorror.com/kane-parsons-backrooms-2-a24-director/). The first film did its job; the sequel is his next project.

"Cat in the Hat" has become [the fastest-built creepypasta on the internet](https://ihorror.com/cat-in-the-hat-ai-horror-trend-urban-legend/)—doorbell-camera footage of a tall striped figure on a porch, grainy and real-looking enough. Urban legend as real-time phenomenon.

Steven Fierberg's [*The Spirit Lock*](https://ihorror.com/the-spirit-lock-review/) offers a possession movie that at least swaps out some of the usual ritual theater. Not escaping *Exorcist*'s shadow entirely, but trying a different angle.
