Sam Neill, [beloved New Zealand actor](https://bloody-disgusting.com/editorials/3959695/from-antichrist-to-action-hero-sam-neill-redefined-horrors-leading-man/), died July 13 from pneumonia following stage 3 lymphoma. He appeared in everything from Carpenter's In the Mouth of Madness to Jurassic Park, but Neill's horror work—including Possession and In the Mouth of Madness—defined him for genre audiences as much as dinosaurs did for mainstream ones.

Julius Avery (The Pope's Exorcist, Overlord) is directing [Crush](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3959789/the-popes-exorcist-helmer-julius-avery-to-direct-python-survival-thriller-crush/), a survival thriller for 20th Century Studios about a woman alone in the Everglades who encounters a giant python. Details sparse, but the pairing suggests Avery will lean into practical creature work.

Joko Anwar's [Ghost in the Cell](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3959778/joko-anwars-supernatural-horror-comedy-ghost-in-the-cell-unleashes-gory-new-image/) screens at FrightFest August 28. The Indonesian filmmaker's horror-comedy is set in a prison and involves a mysterious threat. New images suggest gore-forward laughs.

Alice Maio Mackay's next is [Our Effed Up World](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3959721/an-alien-threat-crash-lands-alice-maio-mackays-sci-fi-horror-our-effed-up-world-teaser/), a sci-fi horror with alien invasion and punk rock sensibility. Produced by Dark Star Pictures, featuring Annapurna Sriram and Sara Thompson. Hitting festivals this summer.

The Zellner Brothers (Sasquatch Sunset) are making [Alpha Gang](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3959794/alpha-gang-first-image-introduces-alien-invaders-in-new-comedy-from-sasquatch-sunset-duo/), an alien invasion comedy premiering at Toronto's Special Presentations. First image is out.

[Hellcat](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3959399/shudders-hellcat-exclusive-trailer-traps-an-infected-hostage-in-a-race-against-time/), the feature debut from Brock Bodell (who edited Ultrasound), arrives on Shudder August 14. Claustrophobic setup: a woman wakes in a moving camper with an infection trying to claw its way out.

Folk horror [The Shug](https://rue-morgue.com/a-monster-based-on-real-life-sightings-emerges-in-folk-horror-the-shug-trailer-and-poster/) has a creature based on real-life sightings and explores human trauma. Trailer and poster are out.
