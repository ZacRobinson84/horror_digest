Ti West's *Ebenezer* drops a [second trailer](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3970029/ebenezer-trailer-2-the-ghost-of-johnny-depps-career-haunts-ti-wests-a-christmas-carol-movie/) with Johnny Depp as Scrooge. The film tests whether Depp can still draw an audience; West's *A Christmas Carol* riff doesn't look cozy.

Neon is making an [SCP Foundation horror anthology](https://ihorror.com/neon-scp-foundation-movie-creative-commons-license/) for 2027 under a Creative Commons license, letting anyone legally repost it. The move sidesteps the open-source backlash that greeted A24's now-shelved SCP project.

Sam Raimi and the [*Don't Move* directors](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3969913/every-house-is-haunted-movie-sam-raimi/) are adapting *Every House Is Haunted*. 20th Century bought it off Netflix; details sparse on what the ghost story entails.

Dark Sky Films acquired [*The Vile*](https://www.dreadcentral.com/news/591226/dark-sky-films-acquires-the-vile-out-of-fantastic-fest/), an Arabic-language psychological thriller from Emirati filmmaker out of Fantastic Fest. U.S. distribution rights secured; broader cast TBA.

Eduardo Sánchez (*Blair Witch*) is [teaming with Dean Alioto](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3969964/blair-witch-project-eduardo-sanchez-mcpherson-tape-dean-alioto/) (*The McPherson Tape*) on an unnamed future project. Details scant, but the found-footage pedigree is there.

Garth Ennis's *Freddie the Fix* one-shot—a horror-noir hybrid from *The Boys* creator—[lands at Amazon MGM](https://ihorror.com/freddie-the-fix-movie-garth-ennis-amazon-mgm/) with Lawrence Lamont directing and Kenya Barris producing.
