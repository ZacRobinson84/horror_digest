Zach Cregger's Spielberg-produced sci-fi horror [*The Flood*](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3966648/zach-cregger-says-steve-spielberg-produced-the-flood-isnt-pure-horror-but-may-be-his-most-unsettling-film-yet/) lands August 2028 via New Line. Cregger describes it as unsettling rather than pure horror—details sparse, but Amblin's involvement clearly moved the needle for him.

Badlands picked up *Wildman*, a spec script about a vengeful Sasquatch on a revenge arc. The pitch is "John Wick with Bigfoot," which is either inspired or a placeholder. Either way, folk-horror-adjacent creature revenge beats formulaic casting news.

[*Vincent Must Die*](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3966520/vincent-must-die-french-movie-getting-a-remake-from-blumhouse-atomic-monster/), the French black comedy thriller that played last October, is already getting remade by Blumhouse Atomic Monster with David Leslie Johnson-McGoldrick (*The Conjuring: Last Rites*) writing. Fast turnaround, but the original earned enough goodwill to warrant it.

Beyond Fest is running [*Night Visions*](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3966570/beyond-fest-presents-12-movie-m-night-shyamalan-retrospective-this-september/), a 12-film Shyamalan retrospective this September with the director in attendance. First West Coast retrospective of his work.

A canceled *Scream* [multiplayer game prototype surfaced in a Steam leak](https://www.dreadcentral.com/gaming/587607/canceled-scream-video-game-prototype-discovered-in-massive-steam-leak/), playable and intact. Piece of early-2000s horror gaming history that never saw light.
