Josh Giuliano's [slasher 'Occupant'](https://ihorror.com/occupant-josh-giuliano-blumhouse-focus-features/) premiered at TIFF on Thursday and landed a deal with Blumhouse Atomic Monster and Focus Features by Friday. Fast-track that suggests something worth betting on.

Anna Biller's ['The Face of Horror'](https://www.dreadcentral.com/reviews/588545/the-face-of-horror-review-an-intoxicating-tribute-to-the-gothic-horror-of-the-60s-overflowing-with-furious-vengeance-tiff/) plays TIFF as a 60s Gothic pastiche drenched in vengeance and formal excess. If the review title lands it, Biller's doing something deliberately arch.

['The Mandela Catalogue' movie](https://ihorror.com/mandela-catalogue-movie-release-date-june-2028-spielberg/) is set for June 9, 2028 with Spielberg's Amblin producing and creator Alex Kister directing. The analog horror IP gets a studio push and a summer date—make of that what you will.

A24 picked up worldwide theatrical rights to ['V/H/S: SCP'](https://ihorror.com/vhs-scp-a24-theatrical-scp-foundation-license/) for 2027, except the SCP Foundation (which operates the source material under a Creative Commons license) says nobody can hold exclusive theatrical rights to SCP at all. The dispute is live.

Netflix's ['Below'](https://www.dreadcentral.com/reviews/588552/below-review-an-atmospheric-sea-monster-tale-kept-afloat-by-a-bounty-of-charismatic-performances-tiff/) is a sea monster limited series premiering at TIFF with enough atmosphere and cast charisma to justify the watch.
