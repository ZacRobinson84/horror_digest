Zach Cregger's Resident Evil crossed $400 million worldwide, the [first film in the franchise to reach that milestone](https://ihorror.com/resident-evil-box-office-400-million-worldwide-record/). Cregger said he was [frustrated by how much conversation focused on things other than the movie itself](https://www.dreadcentral.com/news/596091/zach-cregger-resident-evil-backlash/).

Eli Roth's Hostel TV series is [dead at Peacock](https://www.dreadcentral.com/streaming-home-video/596113/hostel-tv-series-peacock-eli-roth/), though Roth still wants to adapt the scripts he wrote for it elsewhere.

Michael Goi's found-footage film Megan Is Missing—which [went viral on TikTok in 2020](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3974219/megan-is-missing-evil-never-dies-official-sequel-to-2011s-viral-horror-movie-debuts-online/)—now has an [official sequel, Megan Is Missing: Evil Never Dies](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3974219/megan-is-missing-evil-never-dies-official-sequel-to-2011s-viral-horror-movie-debuts-online/).

Toho's [Leviathan](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3974205/tohos-leviathan-first-images-brings-the-best-selling-space-survival-horror-manga-to-life/), adapting Shiro Kuroi's best-selling manga about a brutal fight for survival in space, is in post-production and released first images.

Ulrike Ottinger's vampire film [The Blood Countess](https://www.dreadcentral.com/news/596098/the-blood-countess-director-forced-cuts/), starring Isabelle Huppert, was forced to cut 15–20 minutes by studio pressure.

Ken Russell's restored NC-17 director's cut of The Devils [remains in theaters indefinitely](https://ihorror.com/the-devils-directors-cut-theaters-indefinitely-warner-bros-clockwork/) after pulling $3.3 million, a rare call for an older restoration.

Kojima Productions showed the first teaser for [Death Stranding Isolations](https://ihorror.com/death-stranding-isolations-teaser-disney-plus-2027/), a hand-drawn animated series coming to Disney+ in 2027.
